# Timed PYQ mock tests (`/mock`)

_Extracted from CLAUDE.md on 2026-09-16 to keep the always-loaded payload down. This is the per-exam narrative — blueprint rules, per-exam marking schemes, sitting-discovery rules, and why papers are HELD rather than shipped short. For live counts run `npm run stats`; for the shipping history see the Decisions log + [DECISIONS_HISTORY.md](DECISIONS_HISTORY.md). Pure core lives in `src/lib/mocks/`, the builder in `scripts/mocks/build.ts`._

**Timed PYQ mock tests (`/mock`) shipped (2026-07-10; NEET added 2026-07-17):** real past papers served whole as **timed, auto-graded, online** tests — NOT blueprint-sampled mixes ("use PYQPs as is"). A mock = the exact PYQs of one sitting, reconstructed from the bank, delivered NATIVE on pyqvault.com. **193 mocks PUBLIC — 188 PYQ + 5 PRACTICE** (the first non-PYQ mocks, 2026-09-07: five 120-question NDA Paper I papers assembled from the bank, `source='practice'`/`scope='full'` per migration 0088 — see that day's (fifth) Decisions entry; JEE Mains added 24 on 2026-09-07; CDS grew to 55 across 3 papers when GK + Elementary Mathematics shipped 2026-09-06; the count below was left at 123 by that ship): 19 NDA Paper I Mathematics (120 q · 300 marks · +2.5/−0.83; NDA II 2026 added 2026-09-14) + 18 NDA Paper II GAT (150 q · 600 marks · +4/−1.33; English 50 + General-Knowledge 100 across 8 subjects), every NDA sitting 2017→2026, 150 min each; **+ 19 CDS English** (2017-I … 2026-I, every sitting; 120 q · **100 marks** · 120 min · +0.8333/−0.2778 — the first FRACTIONAL marking in the bank, which is why `gradeMock`+`totalMarks` now round `maxScore`; ONE section, since CDS English is one bank subject and the paper's own ~13 Directions-based sections vary by year and live in each question's `context`; `pyq_month` is NULL on all 2,280 rows and two sittings share a year, so it uses the NEET-style `source_file` registry with an edition-aware slug `cds-<year>-<i|ii>-english` — derived from `scripts/cds/config.ts` PAPERS rather than hand-written. See the 2026-08-25 Decisions entry); **+ 8 NEET (UG)** (2021·2022·2023·2024·Re-2024·2025·2026·Re-2026 — Physics/Chemistry/Biology(=Botany+Zoology), +4/−1; 180 q/180 min for 2025+, 200 q/200 min pre-2025; see the 2026-07-17 Decisions entry); **+ 64 MHT-CET** (2026-09-01, merged 2026-09-06 — the PCM group's TWO papers per sitting: **Paper I Mathematics** 50 q · 100 marks · +2 and **Paper II Physics & Chemistry** 50+50 q · 100 marks · +1, both 90 min. **ZERO negative marking — the first such exam in the bank**, which is why `src/lib/mocks/marking.ts` exists: the instructions screen hard-coded "(negative marking)" and "skip if unsure", the second of which COSTS MARKS when a blank scores the same as a wrong answer, and contradicted our own MHT-CET guide. Sittings are `source_file`-keyed because `pyq_month` is not merely null but ACTIVELY MISLEADING — 17 of the 45 share (2023, "May"), so the year+month loop would emit one slug for all 17 and silently overwrite 16 real papers; the registry (`scripts/mocks/mhtcetSittings.ts`) is HALF-DERIVED from `scripts/mhtcet/config.ts` (the 13 April-2025 shifts) and half hand-written (the other 32 have no config anywhere in the repo). **64 of a possible 84 ship: 20 papers are HELD** because a mock is the real paper or it is nothing — 10 short because a flawed question is deliberately PRIVATE (irrecoverable), 20 because rows are absent from the bank (closable by a future ingest). A `hold` is an ASSERTION, not a mute: the builder still attempts the paper and reports a hold whose paper now reconstructs, so closing a corpus hole surfaces as a prompt rather than silence. **The bank has 45 source_file labels but only 42 REAL sittings — three papers were uploaded twice** and, the two uploads being typed independently, `content_hash` deduped only the matching rows, so each was SPLIT across two labels with neither reconstructing and the union exactly 150. They are MERGED via `mergeWith` (rows normalised onto the paper's own numbering first, since a `.docx` runs 1-150 and an `.xlsx` 2-151), not re-ingested: nothing was ever missing. See the 2026-09-01 + 2026-09-06 Decisions entries); **+ 24 JEE Mains Paper 1** (2026-09-07 — **the first mocks whose answers are NOT all option labels**. 75 q · 300 marks · 180 min · +4/−1 uniform, three 25-question subject sections Physics → Chemistry → Maths, each 20 MCQ (Section A) + **5 NUMERIC-answer NAT (Section B)** — no options at all, the key in `questions.numeric_answer`. That forced **migration 0087** (`attempt_answers.numeric_response`, because `selected_label` is CHECK-constrained to A-D at the DB level and a value of 17280 was *rejected by Postgres*), a discriminated-union answer axis (`src/lib/mocks/answers.ts`) replacing the `"A"|"B"|"C"|"D"` that ran from storage through grading, palette, runner and review, and a numeric input in `MockRunner`. **Only 2025+ is expressible**: a 2021-2024 shift printed 90 questions of which a candidate attempted 75 — all 60 MCQ plus ANY 5 OF 10 NAT per subject — and `gradeMock` has no "attempt at most N" concept, so those would score out of 360 and let a student answer all ten; from 2025 Section B is 5 compulsory. **A THIRD sitting-discovery rule**, because for JEE not even `source_file` is a sitting: a 2025 file holds a whole DATE, 150 rows = two 75-question shifts back to back, so a sitting is a `source_row` BLOCK within a file (`scripts/mocks/jeeSittings.ts`, derived from `scripts/jee/papers/*.json`). **24 of 37 ship; 13 HELD** short by 1-3 questions absent from the bank. **The 11 shipped 2025 papers carry an INFERRED shift number** — 2026 states its shift twice (filename `_S1` + pyqNote) while 2025 states it nowhere, so "Shift 1 = rows 1-75" is convention, not evidence; recorded as inferred rather than asserted silently. **The NTA 2-decimal tolerance is INERT on this corpus** — all 546 NAT answers across 2025+2026 are non-negative integers at scale 0 — so it is unit-tested synthetically and exists for the 2021-2024 tier, which does carry decimals and negatives. See the 2026-09-07 Decisions entry).
