subject: Mathematics
chapter: Time, Speed and Distance
subtopic: Trains and Relative Speed
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: B
stem:
Two trains, X and Y, are running on parallel tracks. Train X is 200 m long and travels at 72 km/hr. Train Y is 300 m long. If the trains are traveling in opposite directions, they pass each other completely in 15 seconds. If the trains were traveling in the same direction, how long would it take for the faster train to overtake the slower one?
A: 72 seconds
B: 75 seconds
C: 78 seconds
D: 67.5 seconds
solution:
Total length \(= 200 + 300 = 500\) m. Opposite directions: relative speed \(= \frac{500}{15} = \frac{100}{3}\) m/s.

Speed of X \(= 72 \times \frac{5}{18} = 20\) m/s, so speed of Y \(= \frac{100}{3} - 20 = \frac{40}{3}\) m/s (48 km/hr).

Same direction: relative speed \(= 20 - \frac{40}{3} = \frac{20}{3}\) m/s, so time \(= 500 \div \frac{20}{3} = 75\) seconds.

Check in km/hr: \(72 - 48 = 24\) km/hr; 0.5 km at 24 km/hr takes \(\frac{0.5}{24}\) hr \(= 75\) s.

subject: Mathematics
chapter: Quadrilaterals
subtopic: Trapeziums
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: A
note: As printed the figure cannot exist: the legs 91 and 39 with a base difference of \(74 - 26 = 48\) break the triangle inequality (\(39 + 48 < 91\)). BC and AD are not needed for EF, so the intended answer is still 24.
stem:
ABCD is a quadrilateral in which AB \(\parallel\) DC, and E and F are the mid points of the diagonals AC and BD, respectively.
If AB = 74 cm, BC = 91 cm, DC = 26 cm, and AD = 39 cm,  then what is the length (in cm) of EF?
A: 24
B: 25
C: 21
D: 22
solution:
ABCD is a trapezium with parallel sides AB and DC. The segment joining the midpoints of its diagonals is parallel to the bases and equals half their difference.

(The side lengths BC = 91 and AD = 39 as printed cannot both belong to such a trapezium, but EF does not depend on them.)
