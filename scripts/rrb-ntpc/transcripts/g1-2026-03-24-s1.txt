stem:
Seven boxes P, Q, R, S, T, U and V are kept one over the other but not necessarily in the same order.
No box is kept above U. Only three boxes are kept between U and P. Only one box is kept between R and T. T is kept immediately above P. Only four boxes are kept between R and V. Q is kept at some place above S.
How many boxes are kept between S and U?
A: 3
B: 2
C: 4
D: 1
solution:
Number the positions 1 (bottom) to 7 (top). U is at 7, so P is at 3 and T, immediately above P, is at 4.

R is one box away from T, so R is at 2 or 6. With four boxes between R and V, R at 2 would need V at 7 (taken), so R is at 6 and V at 1.

Q and S take 2 and 5; Q is above S, so Q is at 5 and S at 2.

Between S (2) and U (7) are positions 3, 4, 5 and 6: 4 boxes.

subtopic: Linear and Circular Seating
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: A
stem:
A, B, C, D, E, F and G are sitting around a circular table facing the centre. Only one person sits between G and C when counted from the left of C. E sits third to the right of A. F sits third to the left of D. E sits to the immediate right of F. B is not an immediate neighbour of F. Who sits third to the right of G?
A: F
B: B
C: D
D: E
solution:
Number the seats 0 to 6, counting to the right (anticlockwise, since everyone faces the centre). Put D at 0. F is third to the left of D, so F is at 4; E is immediately right of F, at 5; A is third to the left of E, at 2.

Seats 1, 3 and 6 remain for B, C and G, with G second to the left of C. Either C = 1, G = 6, B = 3 (B next to F, rejected) or C = 3, G = 1, B = 6.

So G is at 1, and third to the right of G is seat 4: F.

Two quadratic equations \(x^2 + x + p = 0\) and \(x^2 + 2x - 3 = 0\) have one root common. What is a possible value of p?
