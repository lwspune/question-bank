Find the area of a quadrilateral ABCD whose area is twice the area of a triangle PQR. The sides of triangle PQR are in the ratio 4:5:6 and the perimeter of the triangle is 90 cm (use \(\sqrt{7} = 2.6\)).
A: 656 \(\text{cm}^2\)

Area of ABCD \(= 2 \times 351 = 702 \text{ cm}^2\).

Check: \(135^2 \times 7 = 18225 \times 7 = 127575\).

Working of hydraulic lift and hydraulic brakes is based on:
A: Faraday's law
B: Gay-Lussac’s law
C: Pascal’s law
D: Coulomb's law
solution:
Pascal's law: pressure applied to an enclosed fluid is transmitted equally in all directions. A small force on a small piston produces a large force on a large piston, which is how hydraulic lifts and brakes work.

Faraday's law is about electromagnetic induction or electrolysis, Gay-Lussac's law about gases and Coulomb's law about electric charges.

subject: Reasoning
chapter: Order, Ranking and Arrangement
subtopic: Linear and Circular Seating
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: D
stem:
P, Q, R, S, T, U and V sit around a circular table facing the centre. Only one person sits between U and P when counted from the right of U. Only one person sits between P and R. Only one person sits between U and Q. Only two people sit between V and R. Only one person sits between T and Q. How many people sit between T and V when counted from the left of T?
A: 3
B: 2
C: 4
D: 1
solution:
Facing the centre, a person's right is the anticlockwise direction. Number the seats 0 to 6 going to the right (anticlockwise) from U at 0.

P is second to the right of U: P = 2. One person between P and R: R = 4 (seat 0 is U).

One person between U and Q: Q = 5 (seat 2 is P). Two people between V and R: V = 1 (seat 0 is U).

One person between T and Q: T = 3 (seat 0 is U). S takes the last seat, 6.

Going right from U: U, V, P, T, R, Q, S. Counting from the left of T (clockwise) T is followed by P and then V, so only one person (P) sits between T and V.
