subject: Mathematics
chapter: Circles
subtopic: Tangents from an External Point
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: A
stem:
Two tangents, AB and AC, are drawn on a circle from an external point A, which is 6 cm away from its centre O. If the radius of the circle is 3 cm, then what is the measure of angle OBC?
A: \(30^\circ\)
B: \(100^\circ\)
C: \(60^\circ\)
D: \(120^\circ\)
solution:
The radius meets the tangent at a right angle, so triangle OBA has \(\angle OBA = 90^\circ\) and \(\sin \angle OAB = \frac{OB}{OA} = \frac{3}{6} = \frac{1}{2}\), so \(\angle OAB = 30^\circ\) and \(\angle AOB = 60^\circ\).

By symmetry \(\angle AOC = 60^\circ\), so \(\angle BOC = 120^\circ\).

Triangle OBC is isosceles (OB = OC), so \(\angle OBC = \frac{180^\circ - 120^\circ}{2} = 30^\circ\).

Check: \(\angle ABC = \frac{180^\circ - 60^\circ}{2} = 60^\circ\) and \(\angle OBC = 90^\circ - 60^\circ = 30^\circ\).
