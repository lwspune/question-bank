Find the volume of a cylinder whose height is 21 cm and its radius is \(\frac{3}{5}\) of the radius of another cylinder with a volume of 317900 \(\text{cm}^3\) and a height of 14 cm. (Use \(\pi = 22/7\))
A: 171656 \(\text{cm}^3\)
B: 161666 \(\text{cm}^3\)
C: 171660 \(\text{cm}^3\)
D: 171666 \(\text{cm}^3\)
solution:
For the second cylinder, \(\frac{22}{7} \times r^2 \times 14 = 317900\), so \(44r^2 = 317900\) and \(r^2 = 7225\), giving \(r = 85\) cm.

Volume \(= \frac{22}{7} \times 51^2 \times 21 = 22 \times 2601 \times 3 = 171666\ \text{cm}^3\).

Check: volume scales as \(r^2 h\), so \(317900 \times \frac{9}{25} \times \frac{21}{14} = 317900 \times 0.54 = 171666\).
