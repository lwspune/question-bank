Pipe A can fill a cistern in 10 hours, pipe B in 15 hours, and pipe C can empty it in 30 hours. All three pipes are opened together. After 6 hours, pipe A is closed, but B and C continue working. How much total time will it take to completely fill the cistern?

B: 10 hours
C: 12 hours

ABCD is a quadrilateral in which AB \(\parallel\) DC, and E and F are the mid points of the diagonals AC and BD, respectively. If AB \(=\) 14 cm, BC \(=\) 64 cm, DC \(=\) 72 cm, and AD \(=\) 47 cm, then what is the length (in cm) of EF?

From the top of a building 20 m high, the angles of depression of the top and the bottom of a tower standing on the same horizontal ground are observed to be \(30^\circ\) and \(60^\circ\), respectively. The height (in meter) of the tower is:
