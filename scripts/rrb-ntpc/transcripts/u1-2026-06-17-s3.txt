A delivery drone is hovering at a fixed point in the air. From two points on the ground in a straight line on opposite sides of the drone, the angles of elevation of the drone are \(30^\circ\) and \(60^\circ\). If the horizontal distance between the two points is 94 m, find the vertical height at which the drone is hovering. (Take \(\sqrt{3} = 1.73\))
A: 42. 562 m
B: 40.752 m
C: 41.652 m
D: 40.655 m
solution:
Let the height be \(h\). The horizontal distances from the foot of the drone are \(h \cot 30^\circ = h\sqrt{3}\) and \(h \cot 60^\circ = \frac{h}{\sqrt{3}}\), on opposite sides.

\(h = 23.5 \times 1.73 = 40.655\) m.
