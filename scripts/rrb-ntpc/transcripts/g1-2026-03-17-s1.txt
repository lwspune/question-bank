subtopic: Age Problems
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: C
stem:
Tejasvi, Trishana and Meena are three sisters. Tejasvi and Trishana are twins. The sum of the ages of Tejasvi and Trishana is the same as that of Meena. Four years earlier, the ratio of the ages of Tejasvi and Meena was 1:3. What will be the age of Trishana 4 years from now?
A: 18 years
B: 16 years
C: 12 years
D: 14 years
solution:
Let each twin be \(t\) years old. Then Meena is \(2t\).

Four years ago: \(\frac{t - 4}{2t - 4} = \frac{1}{3}\), so \(3t - 12 = 2t - 4\) and \(t = 8\).

Trishana 4 years from now: \(8 + 4 = 12\) years.

Check: four years ago the ages were 4 and 12, ratio 1:3.
