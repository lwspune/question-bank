subject: Mathematics
chapter: Statistics
subtopic: Mean, Median and Mode of Grouped Data
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: A
stem:
The marks scored by 100 students in Mathematics examination are given by the following table. Find the mode of this data (up to 2 decimal places).

| Marks scored | 35 - 44 | 45 - 54 | 55 - 64 | 65 - 74 | 75 - 84 | 85 - 94 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| No. of students | 10 | 20 | 30 | 25 | 10 | 5 |
A: 61.17
B: 61.67
C: 60.56
D: 55.44
solution:
The modal class is 55 - 64 (highest frequency, 30). The classes are not continuous, so use the class boundaries: 54.5 to 64.5, with \(h = 10\).

\(f_1 = 30\), \(f_0 = 20\), \(f_2 = 25\).

Mode \(= l + \frac{f_1 - f_0}{2f_1 - f_0 - f_2} \times h = 54.5 + \frac{10}{60 - 45} \times 10 = 54.5 + 6.67 = 61.17\).

(Taking the lower limit as 55 without converting to boundaries would give 61.67, option B.)
