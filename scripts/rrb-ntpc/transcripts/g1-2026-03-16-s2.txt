stem:
If the equations \(x^2 - 3px + q = 0\) and \(x^2 - 2kx + t = 0\) have one common root, and the first equation has equal roots, what is the value of \((t + q)^2\)?

B: \(4pk\)
C: \(9p^2k^2\)
