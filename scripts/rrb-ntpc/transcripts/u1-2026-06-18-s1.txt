subject: Mathematics
chapter: Quadrilaterals
subtopic: Trapeziums
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: D
note: A trapezium with parallel sides 21 and 11 and legs 33 and 52 cannot exist (the legs and the difference 10 of the parallel sides would need 10 + 33 > 52); the intended answer from the midpoint result is 5.
stem:
ABCD is a quadrilateral in which AB \(\parallel\) DC, and E and F are the mid points of the diagonals AC and BD, respectively. If AB = 21 cm, BC = 33 cm, DC = 11 cm, and AD = 52 cm, then what is the length (in cm) of EF?
A: 3
B: 4
C: 6
D: 5
solution:
In a trapezium the segment joining the midpoints of the diagonals is parallel to the parallel sides and equals half their difference.

\(EF = \frac{AB - DC}{2} = \frac{21 - 11}{2} = 5\) cm.

The lengths BC and AD are not needed.

Note: these four lengths cannot form a trapezium (a triangle with sides 10, 33 and 52 would be needed, and \(10 + 33 < 52\)), but the intended answer is 5 cm.

answer: D
stem:
The 7-digit number 77A444B is divisible by 24. What is the maximum value of (A + B)?
A: 13
B: 12
C: 15
D: 16
solution:
Divisible by 24 means divisible by 8 and by 3.

By 8: the last three digits 44B must be a multiple of 8. \(440 = 55 \times 8\), so B = 0 or 8.

By 3: the digit sum \(7 + 7 + A + 4 + 4 + 4 + B = 26 + A + B\) must be a multiple of 3.

With B = 8: \(34 + A\) is a multiple of 3 for A = 2, 5 or 8; the largest is A = 8, giving A + B = 16. With B = 0 the best is A = 7, giving 7.

So the maximum value of A + B is 16 (the number 7784448).
