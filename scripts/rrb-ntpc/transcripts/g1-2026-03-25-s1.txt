subject: Reasoning
chapter: Order, Ranking and Arrangement
subtopic: Linear and Circular Seating
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: A
stem:
Seven people F, G, H, I, V, W, and X are sitting around a circular table facing the centre of the table. I sits third to the right of G. Only two people sit between I and F. Only three people sit between G and V. H sits to the immediate left of W. Who sits fourth to the left of X?
A: H
B: I
C: W
D: F
solution:
Facing the centre, a person's left is the clockwise direction. Number the seats 0 to 6 clockwise with G at 0.

I is third to the right of G, so I is at seat 4. Only two people sit between I and F, so F is at seat 1 (seat 0 is G). Only three people sit between G and V, so V is at seat 3 (seat 4 is I).

Seats 2, 5 and 6 remain for H, W and X. H is immediately left of W (one seat clockwise), so W is at 5 and H at 6, which leaves X at 2.

Clockwise: G, F, X, V, I, W, H. Fourth to the left of X: V, I, W, H. The answer is H.

chapter: Statistics
subtopic: Properties of the Arithmetic Mean
difficulty: HARD
answer: C
stem:
If the standard deviation of 11, 13, 15, 17,...., 71 is m, then find the integral part of m.
A: 16
B: 13
C: 17
D: 18
solution:
The terms run from 11 to 71 in steps of 2, so there are \(\frac{71 - 11}{2} + 1 = 31\) terms, and the mean is \(\frac{11 + 71}{2} = 41\).

The deviations from the mean are \(2k\) for \(k = -15\) to \(15\), so the variance is \(\frac{4 \times 2(1^2 + 2^2 + \dots + 15^2)}{31} = \frac{4 \times 2 \times 1240}{31} = 320\).

\(m = \sqrt{320} \approx 17.89\), so the integral part is 17.

Check with the formula for an arithmetic progression: \(\sigma = d\sqrt{\frac{n^2 - 1}{12}} = 2\sqrt{\frac{960}{12}} = 2\sqrt{80} \approx 17.89\).

Read the given statements and conclusions carefully. Assuming that the information given in the statements is true, even if it appears to be at variance with commonly known facts, decide which of the given conclusion(s) logically follow(s) from the statements.
