answer: B
stem:
Which of the following groups of organisms exhibit the phenomenon of haploid- dominant life cycle?
A: The amphibians
B: The fungi
C: The angiosperms
D: The mammals
solution:
In most fungi (and many algae) the main, long-lived stage is haploid; the only diploid stage is the zygote, which soon divides by meiosis. This is a haploid-dominant (haplontic) life cycle.

Amphibians and mammals are diploid-dominant (only the gametes are haploid), and angiosperms are dominated by the diploid sporophyte.

subtopic: Boats and Streams
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: C
stem:
Boat A, whose speed in still water is 10 km/h, goes downstream from city P to city Q in 3 hours. Boat B goes from city Q to city P in 5 hours. What will be the time taken (in hours) by boat B to travel from city P to city Q, if the speed of the stream is 5 km/h?
A: \(3\frac{4}{15}\)
B: 3
C: \(2\frac{7}{19}\)
D: 4
solution:
P to Q is downstream. Boat A's downstream speed \(= 10 + 5 = 15\) km/h, so PQ \(= 15 \times 3 = 45\) km.

Downstream, boat B goes at \(14 + 5 = 19\) km/h, so P to Q takes \(\frac{45}{19} = 2\frac{7}{19}\) hours.
