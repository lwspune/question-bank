subtopic: Common Roots
difficulty: MODERATE

stem:
If a is a positive integer such that the equations \((a^2 - 7a)x^2 + x + 12 = 0\) and \(240x^2 + 8x + (a^2 - 4) = 0\) both have roots common, then the value of \(\frac{(a + 2)(a + 3)}{a - 4}\) is:
A: 18
B: 26
C: 30
D: 42
solution:
When both roots are common, the coefficients are proportional: \(\frac{a^2 - 7a}{240} = \frac{1}{8} = \frac{12}{a^2 - 4}\).

From \(\frac{12}{a^2 - 4} = \frac{1}{8}\): \(a^2 - 4 = 96\), so \(a^2 = 100\) and \(a = 10\).

Check: \(a^2 - 7a = 100 - 70 = 30\) and \(\frac{30}{240} = \frac{1}{8}\).

\(\frac{(10 + 2)(10 + 3)}{10 - 4} = \frac{12 \times 13}{6} = 26\).
