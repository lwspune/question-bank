In a business, P invests ₹2,00,000 for 8 months, Q invests ₹1,50,000 for 9 months and R invests ₹1,20,000 for some complete months. After a year, out of the profit, R gets a higher profit amount than Q but less than P. If R gets ₹72,000 as profit, then the profit of P is ₹_______.
A: 80,000
B: 90,000
C: 1,00,000
D: 1,20,000
solution:
Capital-months: P = \(2,00,000 \times 8 = 16,00,000\), Q = \(1,50,000 \times 9 = 13,50,000\), R = \(1,20,000 \times m\).

P : R = 16,00,000 : 14,40,000 = 10 : 9, so P's profit = \(72,000 \times \frac{10}{9} = 80,000\).

Check: Q's profit would be \(72,000 \times \frac{13.5}{14.4} = 67,500\), which is below R's ₹72,000, as required.

During a sale, 50% of the goods are sold at 21% profit, 30% of the remaining goods are sold at 43% profit and the still remaining goods are sold at a loss of 17%. If there is an overall profit of x%, then what is the value of x?
