subtopic: Linear and Circular Seating
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: B
stem:
A, B, C, D, E, F and G sit around a circular table, facing the centre.
Only one person sits between D and B when counted from the left of B. F sits third to the right of E. C sits third to the left of G. F sits to the immediate right of C. A is not an immediate neighbour of C.
How many people sit between A and C when counted from the left of A?

B: 1
C: 4
D: 3

Going left from A (seat 2): seat 1 is F, then seat 0 is C. One person sits between them.
