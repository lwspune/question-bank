subtopic: Two Angles of Elevation or Depression
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: A
stem:
From the top of a 75-meter-high lighthouse, the angles of depression of two ships are \(30^\circ\) and \(45^\circ\). If the two ships are on the same side of the lighthouse and in a straight line with its base, find the distance between the two ships. (Use \(\sqrt{3} = 1.732\))
A: 54.9 meters
B: 52.5 meters
C: 58.4 meters
D: 61.2 meters
solution:
The ship seen at \(45^\circ\) is \(75 \div \tan 45^\circ = 75\) m from the base.

The ship seen at \(30^\circ\) is \(75 \div \tan 30^\circ = 75\sqrt{3} = 75 \times 1.732 = 129.9\) m from the base.

Distance between the ships \(= 129.9 - 75 = 54.9\) m.

Check: \(75(\sqrt{3} - 1) = 75 \times 0.732 = 54.9\).

subject: Mathematics
chapter: Linear Equations
subtopic: Age Problems
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: A
stem:
The present age of a father is equal to sum of ages of his son and daughter. Ten years ago from now, the age of the father was double the age of the son and four times the age of the daughter. The total present age of the father, daughter and son is ____ years.
A: 100
B: 120
C: 80
D: 60
solution:
Let the present ages be F, S and D, with \(F = S + D\).

Ten years ago: \(F - 10 = 2(S - 10)\), so \(S = \frac{F + 10}{2}\); and \(F - 10 = 4(D - 10)\), so \(D = \frac{F + 30}{4}\).

\(F = \frac{F + 10}{2} + \frac{F + 30}{4}\) gives \(4F = 3F + 50\), so \(F = 50\), \(S = 30\), \(D = 20\).

Total \(= 50 + 30 + 20 = 100\) years.

Check: ten years ago the ages were 40, 20 and 10, and \(40 = 2 \times 20 = 4 \times 10\).

A faster train overtakes a slower train, both moving in the same direction. The faster train takes 36 seconds to completely pass the slower train. B: 3 : 1
C: 1 : 1
D: 2 : 1
solution:
Let the relative speed be v. The passenger covers only the slower train's length: \(L_s = 12v\).
