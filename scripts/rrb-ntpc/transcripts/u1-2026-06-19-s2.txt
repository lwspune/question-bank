subtopic: Pipes and Cisterns
difficulty: MODERATE

stem:
Pipes A, B and C can together fill a tank in 6 hours. After working together for 2 hours, C is closed and A and B together fill the remaining part in 7 hours. The time taken (in hours) by C alone to fill the tank is:
A: 9
B: 21
C: 12
D: 14
solution:
A, B and C together fill \(\frac{1}{6}\) of the tank per hour, so in 2 hours they fill \(\frac{2}{6} = \frac{1}{3}\).

A and B fill the remaining \(\frac{2}{3}\) in 7 hours, so A and B together fill \(\frac{2}{21}\) per hour.

C alone fills \(\frac{1}{6} - \frac{2}{21} = \frac{7 - 4}{42} = \frac{1}{14}\) per hour, so C takes 14 hours.

Check: in 2 hours C fills \(\frac{2}{14} = \frac{1}{7}\) and A and B fill \(\frac{2}{21}\) per hour for 9 hours, \(\frac{18}{21} = \frac{6}{7}\); together the full tank.

=== 2
subject: Mathematics
chapter: Quadrilaterals
subtopic: Properties of Quadrilaterals
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: A
stem:
Which of the following are true to construct a quadrilateral uniquely?
( i ) Rhombus and square can be constructed when 2 diagonals are given.
( ii ) The lengths of 2 adjacent sides and 2 angles are given.
( iii ) The lengths of 3 sides and 1 angle are given.
( iv ) The lengths of 4 sides and 1 diagonal are given.
A: ( i ) and ( iv )
B: ( ii ) and ( iv )
C: ( ii ) and ( iii )
D: ( i ) and ( iii )
solution:
A quadrilateral needs five independent measurements to be fixed uniquely: four sides and one diagonal, two diagonals and three sides, two adjacent sides and three angles, or three sides and the two included angles.

(iv) gives four sides and a diagonal: true.

(i) is true for special quadrilaterals: the diagonals of a rhombus (and a square) bisect each other at right angles, so the two diagonals fix the figure.

(ii) needs three angles, not two, and (iii) needs two included angles, not one: both are false. So (i) and (iv).

A cyclist travels from town A to town B, a distance of 182 km. For the first 84 km, he rides at a certain speed.
