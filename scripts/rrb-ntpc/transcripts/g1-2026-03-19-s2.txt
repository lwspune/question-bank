At what rate (in percentage, rounded off to two decimal place) per year will a sum of money 5 times itself in 18 years on simple interest?
A: 44.44
B: 20.22
C: 22.22
D: 24.22
solution:

Check: \(22.22 \times 18 \approx 400\), which is 4 times the principal.

stem:
Three friends X, Y and Z started a business by investing a sum of money in the ratio of 6 : 3 : 5. After 6 months, X withdraws half of his capital. If the sum invested by Z is ₹12,000, then out of a total annual profit of ₹69,000, what is the difference between X's and Y's profit?
A: ₹8120
B: ₹9910
C: ₹7290
D: ₹8280
solution:
Z's 5 parts are ₹12,000, so one part is ₹2,400: X invests ₹14,400 and Y ₹7,200.

Capital-months: X \(= 14400 \times 6 + 7200 \times 6 = 129600\), Y \(= 7200 \times 12 = 86400\), Z \(= 12000 \times 12 = 144000\).

Ratio \(129600 : 86400 : 144000 = 9 : 6 : 10\), total 25 parts.

One part of the profit \(= \frac{69000}{25} = 2760\), so X's share minus Y's share \(= 3 \times 2760 = ₹8280\).

In a certain code language,
A + B means ‘A is the son of B’

A x B means ‘A is the wife of B’

stem:
X, Y, Z, S, T and U live on six different floors of the same building. The lowermost floor in the building is numbered 1, the floor above it is numbered 2, and so on till the topmost floor is numbered 6. X lives on an even numbered floor but not on floor number 4. Simplify: \(\frac{\cot\theta - \cos\theta}{\cot\theta + \cos\theta}\).

subject: Reasoning
chapter: Data Sufficiency
subtopic: Data Sufficiency
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: D
stem:
In this question, a question is followed by two statements numbered (I) and (II). You have to decide whether the data provided in the statements are sufficient to answer the question. Read both the statements and decide the appropriate answer.

Question:
Among 5 trees, L, M, N, T and V each having a different height, which tree is the shortest?

Statements:
(I) N is shorter than only three trees.
(II) N is taller than M but shorter than T.
A: Both statements I and II together are NOT sufficient to answer the question
B: Data in Statement II alone is sufficient to answer the question while data in statement I is not
C: Data in Statement I alone is sufficient to answer the question while data in statement II is not
D: Both statements I and II together (and not statement 1 alone or statement II alone) are sufficient to answer the question
solution:
Statement I: N is 4th tallest, so exactly one tree is shorter than N, but we do not know which. Not sufficient.

Statement II: M < N < T. L and V could be anywhere, so the shortest is unknown. Not sufficient.

Together: only one tree is shorter than N, and M is shorter than N, so M is the shortest. Both together are sufficient.

M, N, O, P, Q, R and S are sitting around a circular table facing the centre of the table. M sits fourth to the right of S. Only two people sit between P and Q when counted from the left of P. note: RRB's tentative key gives A, "both parents must be Indian citizens", which Section 4 of the Citizenship Act contradicts: one citizen parent suffices (with the birth registered at a consulate). C is the only option matching a condition the Act sets (Section 4(1A)). Our answer kept.
notice: The paper's tentative key gives A, but the Citizenship Act needs only ONE parent to be an Indian citizen for a child born abroad after 3 December 2004. The condition among the options is C: a citizen by descent who holds another nationality must renounce it within six months of turning 18.
stem:
What condition must be fulfilled by a person born outside India after December 3, 2004, to be considered an Indian citizen by descent?

solution:
