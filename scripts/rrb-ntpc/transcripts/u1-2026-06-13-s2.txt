C: 122

subtopic: Combined and Shaded Regions
difficulty: HARD
answer: A
stem:
From a regular octagon of side 4 units, a largest rectangle is cut out. From the remaining pieces, two largest rectangles are cut out. The remaining area of the octagon is _____ square units.
A: 16
B: 8
C: 32
D: 24
solution:
Each slanted side of the octagon has horizontal and vertical projections \(\frac{4}{\sqrt{2}} = 2\sqrt{2}\), so the octagon spans \(4 + 4\sqrt{2}\) across.

The largest rectangle is the middle strip, \(4 \times (4 + 4\sqrt{2})\). The two pieces left on either side each yield a rectangle \(4 \times 2\sqrt{2}\).

What remains is the four corner triangles, each right-angled and isosceles with legs \(2\sqrt{2}\): area \(\frac{1}{2} \times 2\sqrt{2} \times 2\sqrt{2} = 4\) each, so \(4 \times 4 = 16\).

Check: octagon area \(= 2(1 + \sqrt{2}) \times 16 = 32 + 32\sqrt{2}\); rectangles \(= (16 + 16\sqrt{2}) + 2 \times 8\sqrt{2} = 16 + 32\sqrt{2}\); difference 16.
