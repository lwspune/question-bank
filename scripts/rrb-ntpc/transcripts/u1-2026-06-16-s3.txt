Which of the following numbers is divisible by 11?
A: 869756
B: 978626
C: 447255
D: 112044
solution:
A number is divisible by 11 when the alternating sum of its digits (from the right) is a multiple of 11.

stem:
Two trains P and Q, measure 200 m and 300 m in length, respectively. The faster train, coming from behind, takes 48 seconds to overtake the slower when moving in the same direction. A: 62.75 km/h
B: 61.75 km/h
C: 63.75 km/h
D: 60.75 km/h
solution:
Distance to cover in both cases \(= 200 + 300 = 500\) m.

Check: \(v = 25 - 17.708 = 7.292\) m/s, and \(17.708 - 7.292 = 10.417 = \frac{500}{48}\).

A hollow cylinder of thickness 1 unit and length 14 units is made with 396 cubic units of metal. Its inner volume is _____ cubic units. (Take \(\pi = \frac{22}{7}\))
