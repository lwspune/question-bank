subject: Mathematics
chapter: Time and Work
subtopic: Efficiency Ratios
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: D
stem:
A, B and C together can complete a certain work in 54 days. B and C together can complete the same work in 56 days. B is 75% more efficient than A. A, B and C work together for 52 days. B alone will complete the remaining work in:
A: 30 days
B: 34 days
C: 27 days
D: 32 days
solution:
A's one-day work \(= \frac{1}{54} - \frac{1}{56} = \frac{2}{3024} = \frac{1}{1512}\).

B is 75% more efficient, so B's one-day work \(= 1.75 \times \frac{1}{1512} = \frac{7}{6048} = \frac{1}{864}\).

In 52 days A, B and C finish \(\frac{52}{54} = \frac{26}{27}\) of the work, leaving \(\frac{1}{27}\).

B alone takes \(\frac{864}{27} = 32\) days.

Check: \(32 \times \frac{1}{864} = \frac{1}{27}\).
