subject: Mathematics
chapter: Time, Speed and Distance
subtopic: Races
difficulty: HARD
answer: C
stem:
In a race of 1200 metres, Amit gives Bhushan a head-start of 200 metres. Amit starts 20 seconds after Bhushan. Amit overtakes Bhushan 4 minutes after he (Amit) starts running. Amit finishes the race 10 seconds before Bhushan. What is the running speed (in m/s) of Amit?
A: \(3\frac{4}{9}\)
B: \(4\frac{1}{3}\)
C: \(4\frac{4}{9}\)
D: \(3\frac{1}{3}\)
solution:
Let Amit's speed be \(a\) and Bhushan's \(b\) (m/s). Bhushan runs 1000 m, Amit 1200 m.

Overtaking: in 240 s Amit covers what Bhushan covers in 260 s plus the 200 m start, so \(240a = 200 + 260b\), giving \(b = \frac{12a - 10}{13}\).

Finish: Bhushan's time = Amit's time + 20 + 10, so \(\frac{1000}{b} = \frac{1200}{a} + 30\).

Try \(a = \frac{40}{9}\): \(b = \frac{\frac{160}{3} - 10}{13} = \frac{10}{3}\). Then \(\frac{1000}{b} = 300\) and \(\frac{1200}{a} + 30 = 270 + 30 = 300\). Both equations hold.

Check the overtaking: Amit in 240 s covers \(\frac{3200}{3}\) m; Bhushan in 260 s covers \(200 + \frac{2600}{3} = \frac{3200}{3}\) m from Amit's start line. So Amit's speed is \(4\frac{4}{9}\) m/s.

subject: Mathematics
chapter: Circles
subtopic: Touching Circles
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: B
stem:
Five circles are placed adjacent to one another and have lines M1 and M2 as their common tangents. If the radius of the largest circle is 9 units and that of the smallest circle is 4 units, then what is the radius of the middle circle (in units)?
A: 8
B: 6
C: 5
D: 10
solution:
Circles that touch each other in a row between two common tangents are similar about the point where the tangents meet, so their radii form a geometric progression: \(4, 4k, 4k^2, 4k^3, 4k^4 = 9\).

The middle circle is the third term: \(4k^2 = \sqrt{4 \times 9} = 6\), since the middle term of an odd GP is the geometric mean of the end terms.

Check: \(k^4 = \frac{9}{4}\) gives \(k^2 = \frac{3}{2}\), and \(4 \times \frac{3}{2} = 6\).
