Find the value of m which satisfies
\(\left(\frac{26}{7}\right)^5 \times \left(\frac{7}{26}\right)^{11} \times \left(\frac{26}{7}\right)^{19} = \left(\frac{7}{26}\right)^{9m + 17}\).
A: \(-\frac{10}{3}\)
B: \(-\frac{37}{9}\)
C: \(-\frac{26}{9}\)
D: \(-\frac{24}{9}\)
solution:

So \(9m + 17 = -13\), \(9m = -30\), \(m = -\frac{10}{3}\).

Check: \(9 \times \left(-\frac{10}{3}\right) + 17 = -30 + 17 = -13\).

If \(\sec\theta + \text{cosec}\,\theta = a\) and \(\tan\theta + \cot\theta = b\), find the value of \(\text{cosec}\,2\theta\).

Sides PQ and QR of a \(\triangle PQR\) are produced to points S and T. If \(\angle PRT > \angle RQS > 50^\circ\) and \(\angle PRT + \angle RQS = 220^\circ\), which of the following statements is true?

stem:
A train met with an accident 50 km away from station A. It completed the remaining journey at three-fourth of its original speed and reached station B 35 minutes late. Had the accident occurred 24 km further down the line, it would have been only 25 minutes late. What is the original speed of the train?
A: 60 km/hr
B: 40 km/hr
C: 45 km/hr
D: 48 km/hr
solution:
At \(\frac{3}{4}\) of speed v, a distance d takes \(\frac{4d}{3v} - \frac{d}{v} = \frac{d}{3v}\) extra.

In the second case 24 km more is covered at full speed, saving \(35 - 25 = 10\) minutes: \(\frac{24}{3v} = \frac{10}{60}\), so \(\frac{8}{v} = \frac{1}{6}\), \(v = 48\) km/hr.

Check: the delay is \(\frac{d}{144}\) h, so 35 minutes means \(d = 84\) km after the accident; then 60 km gives \(\frac{60}{144}\) h = 25 minutes.
