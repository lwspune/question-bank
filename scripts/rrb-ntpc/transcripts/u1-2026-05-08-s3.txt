chapter: Surds, Indices and Simplification
subtopic: BODMAS and Brackets
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: B
stem:
Simplify: \(\left\{\frac{16}{36} \div \frac{16}{4}\right\} \div \left(\frac{2}{7} \times \frac{14}{6} + \frac{5}{12}\right) + \frac{4}{7} \div \frac{39}{12} \text{ of } \frac{12}{7}\)
A: \(\frac{12}{47}\)
B: \(\frac{8}{39}\)
C: \(-\frac{1}{43}\)
D: \(\frac{7}{33}\)
solution:
Curly bracket: \(\frac{16}{36} \div \frac{16}{4} = \frac{4}{9} \times \frac{1}{4} = \frac{1}{9}\).

Round bracket: \(\frac{2}{7} \times \frac{14}{6} = \frac{2}{3}\), and \(\frac{2}{3} + \frac{5}{12} = \frac{13}{12}\).

So the first part is \(\frac{1}{9} \div \frac{13}{12} = \frac{12}{117} = \frac{4}{39}\).

"of" comes before division: \(\frac{39}{12} \text{ of } \frac{12}{7} = \frac{39}{7}\), so \(\frac{4}{7} \div \frac{39}{7} = \frac{4}{39}\).

Total: \(\frac{4}{39} + \frac{4}{39} = \frac{8}{39}\).

Check in decimals: \(0.1026 + 0.1026 = 0.2051\), and \(\frac{8}{39} = 0.2051\).
