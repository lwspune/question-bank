Which of the following Acts was enacted by the British Parliament to regulate the administration of the British East India Company’s territories in India, particularly in Bengal?
A: Charter Act, 1813
B: Government of India Act, 1858
C: Regulating Act, 1773
D: Pitt’s India Act, 1784
solution:
The Regulating Act of 1773 was the first step by the British Parliament to control and regulate the affairs of the East India Company in India. It made the Governor of Bengal the Governor-General of Bengal (Warren Hastings) and set up a Supreme Court at Calcutta.

Pitt's India Act (1784) created the Board of Control, the Charter Act of 1813 ended the Company's trade monopoly (except tea and trade with China), and the 1858 Act transferred rule to the Crown.
