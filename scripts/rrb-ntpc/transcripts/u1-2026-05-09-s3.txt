stem:
A motorist travels a certain journey in two stages. In the first stage, he travels for one-third of the total time at 30 km/hr. In the second stage, he travels for the rest of the time at 60 km/hr. B: 62 km/hr
C: 58 km/hr
D: 55 km/hr
solution:
