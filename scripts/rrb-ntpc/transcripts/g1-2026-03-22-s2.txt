=== 38
subject: Mathematics
chapter: Time, Speed and Distance
subtopic: Races
difficulty: HARD
answer: D
stem:
In a 150-meter race, R beats S by 6 meters. In a 250-meter race, R beats S by 2 seconds. As per this, in a race, if R runs for 30 seconds, then he beats S by ___ meters.
A: 5.75
B: 7.25
C: 6.75
D: 6.25
solution:
From the 150 m race, S runs 144 m while R runs 150 m, so S's speed is \(\frac{144}{150} = 0.96\) of R's.

In the 250 m race, if R takes \(t\) s, S takes \(t + 2\) s, and the speed ratio is \(\frac{t}{t+2} = 0.96\), so \(t = 0.96t + 1.92\) and \(t = 48\) s.

R's speed is \(\frac{250}{48}\) m/s and S's is \(0.96 \times \frac{250}{48} = 5\) m/s.

In 30 s, R runs \(30 \times \frac{250}{48} = 156.25\) m and S runs 150 m, so R beats S by 6.25 m.

Check: S takes \(250 \div 5 = 50\) s for 250 m, 2 s more than R's 48 s.
