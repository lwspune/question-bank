Two people, A and B, start running from the same point on a circular track of length 400 meters, but in opposite directions. A runs at 6 m/s and B at 4 m/s. In how many seconds will they meet for the first time?
A: 50 seconds
B: 40 seconds
C: 20 seconds
D: 30 seconds
solution:
Running in opposite directions, they close the gap at \(6 + 4 = 10\) m/s.

They meet first when together they have covered one full lap: \(\frac{400}{10} = 40\) seconds.

Check: in 40 s A runs 240 m and B runs 160 m, and \(240 + 160 = 400\).

subtopic: Three-Statement Syllogism
difficulty: EASY
answer: C
stem:
Read the given statements and conclusions carefully. You have to take the given statements to be true even if they seem to be at variance from commonly known facts. You have to decide which conclusion/s logically follow/s from the given statements.
Statements:
All papers are books. All books are erasers. Some erasers are crayons.
Conclusions:
(I) Some papers are crayons.
(II) Some books are crayons.
A: Both conclusions I and II follow.
B: Only conclusion I follows.
C: Neither conclusion I nor II follows.

subject: Mathematics
chapter: Linear Equations
subtopic: Solving Linear Systems
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: D
stem:
Solve the following system of equations:
\(x + 2y + 4z = 14\)
\(x + 2y + z = 7\)
\(2x + y + 2z = 2\)
A: \(x = -\frac{10}{3}, y = 3, z = \frac{7}{3}\)
B: \(x = -\frac{11}{3}, y = 3, z = \frac{7}{3}\)
C: \(x = -\frac{10}{3}, y = 4, z = \frac{8}{3}\)
D: \(x = -\frac{10}{3}, y = 4, z = \frac{7}{3}\)
solution:
Subtract the second equation from the first: \(3z = 7\), so \(z = \frac{7}{3}\).

Then \(x + 2y = 7 - \frac{7}{3} = \frac{14}{3}\) and \(2x + y = 2 - \frac{14}{3} = -\frac{8}{3}\).

Doubling the first gives \(2x + 4y = \frac{28}{3}\); subtracting \(2x + y = -\frac{8}{3}\) gives \(3y = 12\), so \(y = 4\) and \(x = \frac{14}{3} - 8 = -\frac{10}{3}\).

Check in the first equation: \(-\frac{10}{3} + 8 + \frac{28}{3} = 6 + 8 = 14\).

subtopic: Boats and Streams
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: D
stem:
A boat goes 20,000 m upstream and 44,000 m downstream in 8 hours while it goes 15,000 m upstream and 22,000 m downstream in 5 hours. The speed of boat in still water is:
A: \(2\frac{1}{9}\) m/s
B: \(3\frac{4}{9}\) m/s
C: \(3\frac{2}{9}\) m/s
D: \(2\frac{2}{9}\) m/s
solution:
Work in km: let upstream speed be u and downstream speed d (km/h). \(\frac{20}{u} + \frac{44}{d} = 8\) and \(\frac{15}{u} + \frac{22}{d} = 5\).

Doubling the second: \(\frac{30}{u} + \frac{44}{d} = 10\). Subtracting the first: \(\frac{10}{u} = 2\), so \(u = 5\) km/h. Then \(\frac{44}{d} = 8 - 4 = 4\), so \(d = 11\) km/h.

Still water speed \(= \frac{5 + 11}{2} = 8\) km/h \(= 8 \times \frac{5}{18} = \frac{20}{9} = 2\frac{2}{9}\) m/s.

Check: \(\frac{15}{5} + \frac{22}{11} = 3 + 2 = 5\) hours.
