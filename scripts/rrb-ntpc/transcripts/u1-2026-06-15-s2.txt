By selling lemons at 5 for a rupee, a man loses 34%. To gain 10% how many must he sell for a rupee?
A: 5
B: 4
C: 3
D: 2
solution:
Selling price of one lemon \(= \frac{1}{5}\) rupee, which is 66% of the cost price.

To gain 10% the selling price must be 110% of the cost price, so new price \(= \frac{1}{5} \times \frac{110}{66} = \frac{1}{3}\) rupee per lemon.

So he must sell 3 lemons for a rupee.

Check: number per rupee varies inversely with the selling price, \(5 \times \frac{66}{110} = 3\).
