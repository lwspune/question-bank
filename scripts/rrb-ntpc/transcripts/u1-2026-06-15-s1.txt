stem:
In a school, Class A has 24 students with an average height of 160 cm, and Class B has 20 students with an average height of 168 cm. The average height of students in Class C is 165 cm. If the overall average height of all students from Classes A, B and C is 164 cm, find the number of students in Class C.
A: 20
B: 26
C: 18
D: 16
solution:
Let Class C have \(n\) students. Total heights: \(24 \times 160 = 3840\), \(20 \times 168 = 3360\), and \(165n\).

\(3840 + 3360 + 165n = 164(44 + n)\), so \(7200 + 165n = 7216 + 164n\), giving \(n = 16\).

Check by deviations from 164: Class A gives \(24 \times (-4) = -96\), Class B gives \(20 \times 4 = 80\), Class C gives \(16 \times 1 = 16\), and \(-96 + 80 + 16 = 0\).

Two trains of lengths 160 meters and 140 meters are moving in opposite directions at 55 km/h and 65 km/h, respectively. Find the time taken by them to cross each other completely.
A: 6 seconds
B: 9 seconds
C: 8 seconds
D: 10 seconds
solution:
