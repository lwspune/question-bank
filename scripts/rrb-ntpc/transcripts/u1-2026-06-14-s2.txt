subtopic: Two Angles of Elevation or Depression
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: B
stem:
From the top of a cliff 80 meters high, the angles of depression of two ships in the same direction are \(30^\circ\) and \(60^\circ\). Find the distance between the ships (rounded off to two decimal places).
A: 92.58 meters
B: 92.38 meters
C: 92.18 meters
D: 92.78 meters
solution:
The nearer ship is seen at \(60^\circ\): its distance from the foot of the cliff is \(\frac{80}{\tan 60^\circ} = \frac{80}{\sqrt{3}} = 46.188\) m.

The farther ship is seen at \(30^\circ\): its distance is \(\frac{80}{\tan 30^\circ} = 80\sqrt{3} = 138.564\) m.

Distance between the ships \(= 138.564 - 46.188 = 92.376 \approx 92.38\) m.

Check: \(80\left(\sqrt{3} - \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\right) = \frac{160}{\sqrt{3}} = 92.376\).

A train overtakes two runners moving in the same direction at speeds of 5 km/h and 7 km/h, passing them completely in 18 seconds and 20 seconds, respectively. What is the length of the train (in metres)?
A: 70
B: 90
C: 100
D: 80
solution:
Let the train's speed be \(s\) km/h. Length = relative speed \(\times\) time, with 1 km/h \(= \frac{5}{18}\) m/s.

First runner: \(L = (s - 5) \times \frac{5}{18} \times 18 = 5(s - 5)\).

Second runner: \(L = (s - 7) \times \frac{5}{18} \times 20 = \frac{50}{9}(s - 7)\).

So \(45(s - 5) = 50(s - 7)\), giving \(5s = 125\), \(s = 25\) km/h.

\(L = 5 \times 20 = 100\) m.

Check: \(\frac{50}{9} \times 18 = 100\) m.
