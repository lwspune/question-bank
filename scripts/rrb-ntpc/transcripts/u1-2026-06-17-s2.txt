stem:
A shopkeeper offers the following schemes on toys of the same marked price.
(A) Successive discounts of 5% and 21% on any number of toys bought.
(B) Successive discounts of 38%, 33% and 21% on any number of toys bought.
(C) 8% discount on the first 6 toys and 27% discount on each toy thereon.
(D) 3 toys free of cost on buying 7 toys.
A customer wants to buy 7 toys. Which of the above schemes is the least beneficial to her?
A: A
B: C
C: B
D: D
solution:
Compare the discount each scheme gives on the toys she gets.

(A) \(0.95 \times 0.79 = 0.7505\), a discount of about 25%.

(B) \(0.62 \times 0.67 \times 0.79 = 0.32816\), a discount of about 67%.

(C) \(6 \times 0.92 + 1 \times 0.73 = 5.52 + 0.73 = 6.25\) marked prices for 7 toys, a discount of about 10.7%.

(D) she pays for 7 toys and gets 10, a discount of \(\frac{3}{10} = 30\%\).

The smallest discount is scheme C's, so scheme C is the least beneficial: option B.
