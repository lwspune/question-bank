answer: C
stem:
The rate constant of reaction is \(1.5 \times 10^7\ \mathrm{s^{-1}}\) at 300 K and \(3.0 \times 10^7\ \mathrm{s^{-1}}\) at 330 K. What is the activation energy for the reaction? [ \(R \times 2.303 = 19.15\ \mathrm{JK^{-1}mol^{-1}}\)]
