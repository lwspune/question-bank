subtopic: Mole Concept and Interconversions
difficulty: EASY

Calculate the mass of \(100\) molecules of oxygen in amu and in grams.
A: \(3200\) u and \(5.314 \times 10^{-21}\) g
B: \(1600\) u and \(2.657 \times 10^{-21}\) g
C: \(32\) u and \(1.328 \times 10^{-21}\) g

chapter: Chemical Thermodynamics and Energetics
subtopic: Thermochemistry, Hess's Law and Bond Enthalpy
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: A
stem:
Calculate the enthalpy change for the following reaction, using given bond energy (kJ/mol) (C-H = \(414\), H-O = \(463\), H-Cl = \(431\), C-Cl = \(326\) and C-O = \(335\))
\(\mathrm{CH_3OH}_{(g)} + \mathrm{HCl}_{(g)} \rightarrow \mathrm{CH_3Cl}_{(g)} + \mathrm{H_2O}_{(g)}\)
A: \(-23\ \mathrm{kJmol^{-1}}\)
B: \(-42\ \mathrm{kJmol^{-1}}\)
C: \(-59\ \mathrm{kJmol^{-1}}\)
D: \(-51\ \mathrm{kJmol^{-1}}\)
solution:
The three C-H bonds and one O-H bond appear on both sides and cancel.

Bonds broken: C-O \(+\) H-Cl \(= 335 + 431 = 766\) kJ. Bonds formed: C-Cl \(+\) H-O \(= 326 + 463 = 789\) kJ.

\(\Delta H = 766 - 789 = -23\ \mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}\).

In a cubic unit cell of an ionic compound, the eight corners are occupied by anions and cations at the center of the cube. Calculate the volume of the unit cell if density of the unit cell is \(4\ \mathrm{g\,cm^{-3}}\).

difficulty: MODERATE
answer: A
stem:
Vapour pressure of \(\mathrm{CCl_4}\) at \(25^\circ\mathrm{C}\) is \(143\) mm Hg. If \(0.5\) g of a non-volatile solute is dissolved in \(100\ \mathrm{cm^3}\) of \(\mathrm{CCl_4}\). Find the vapour pressure of the solution.
