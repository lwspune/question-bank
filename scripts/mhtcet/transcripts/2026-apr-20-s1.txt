stem:
In the band structure of n-type semiconductor , the free electrons donated by impurity atoms occupy energy levels in
A: the conduction band.
B: the valence band.
C: the band gap and are close to conduction band.
D: the band gap and are close to valence band.
solution:
The donor impurity introduces energy levels in the band gap just below the conduction band, from which its extra electrons are easily excited into the conduction band.
