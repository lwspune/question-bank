=== 6
chapter: Chemical Thermodynamics and Energetics
subtopic: Gibbs Free Energy and Spontaneity
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: D
stem:
Which of the following statements is not correct ?
A: \(\Delta G^\circ\) is an extensive property.
B: \(E^\circ\) cell is an intensive property.
C: For a chemical reaction to be spontaneous \(\Delta G\) must be \(-\)ve.
D: Electrical work is equal to \(\Delta G\)
solution:
\(\Delta G^\circ\) depends on the amount of reaction (extensive), \(E^\circ_{cell}\) does not (intensive), and a spontaneous reaction at constant T and P has \(\Delta G < 0\). So A, B and C are correct.

D is not: the maximum electrical work a cell can do is \(-\Delta G\) (\(\Delta G = -nFE\)), equal in size but opposite in sign to \(\Delta G\).
