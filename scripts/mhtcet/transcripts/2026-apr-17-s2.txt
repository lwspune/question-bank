Which of the following statements regarding Pi \((\pi)\) bond is NOT true ?

Calculate the quantity of heat released from system when 2 moles of and ideal gas compressed isothermally from volume \(25\ \mathrm{dm^3}\) to \(10\ \mathrm{dm^3}\) at constant external pressure 4 bar.

difficulty: EASY

Identify the correct statement from following.
A: The vapour pressure of a solvent increases by dissolving nonvolatile solute into it.
