Match **List I** with **List II**

| List I | List II |
|---|---|
| (A). Inductive effect | (I). Delocalization of \(\pi\) electrons |
| (B). Hyper-conjugation | (II). Displacement of \(\pi\) electrons |
| (C). Resonance effect | (III). Delocalization of \(\sigma\) electrons |
|  | (IV). Displacement of \(\sigma\) electrons |

Choose the **correct** answer from the options given below:
A: \((A) - (IV), (B) - (III), (C) - (I)\)
B: \((A) - (II), (B) - (IV), (C) - (I)\)
C: \((A) - (I), (B) - (II), (C) - (IV)\)
D: \((A) - (III), (B) - (IV), (C) - (I)\)
solution:
The inductive effect is a permanent displacement of \(\sigma\) electrons along a chain: (A) - (IV). Hyperconjugation is delocalisation of C-H \(\sigma\) electrons into an adjacent p orbital or \(\pi\) bond: (B) - (III). The resonance effect is delocalisation of \(\pi\) electrons: (C) - (I).
