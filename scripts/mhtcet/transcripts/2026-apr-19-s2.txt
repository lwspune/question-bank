B: \(0.50\log_e 3\)
C: \(0.75\log_e 2\)
D: \(0.8\log_e 3\)
solution:
The activity fell 8 times, three half-lives in 4 minutes: \(T_{1/2} = \dfrac{4}{3}\) min and \(\lambda = \dfrac{\log_e 2}{4/3} = 0.75\log_e 2\) per minute.

The Boolean expression for the given combination of logic gates is
A: \(Y = (A + B) \cdot \bar{C}\)
B: \(Y = (A \cdot B) \cdot \bar{C}\)
C: \(Y = (A + B) + C\)
D: \(Y = (A \cdot B) + \bar{C}\)
solution:
The AND gate gives \(A \cdot B\), the NOT gate gives \(\bar{C}\), and the OR gate combines them: \(Y = (A \cdot B) + \bar{C}\).

In the following electrical circuit, the reading in the milliammeter is
(Take knee voltage of silicon diode \(= 0.7\) V)
A: \(25.5\) mA
