chapter: Chemical Bonding and Molecular Structure
subtopic: Dipole Moment, Polarity and Intermolecular Forces
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: C
stem:
Select the incorrect statement from the following.
A: \(\mathrm{NH_3}\) and \(\mathrm{NF_3}\) both are polar molecules
B: \(\mathrm{NH_3}\) and \(\mathrm{NF_3}\) both have pyramidal shape
C: In \(\mathrm{NH_3}\) and \(\mathrm{NF_3}\) the orbital dipole due to lone pair is in the same direction as that of resultant dipole moment of other three bonds
D: Dipole moment of \(\mathrm{NH_3}\) is greater than \(\mathrm{NF_3}\)
solution:
In \(\mathrm{NH_3}\) the N-H bond dipoles point towards N, the same way as the lone-pair dipole, so they add (1.47 D). In \(\mathrm{NF_3}\) the N-F bond dipoles point towards F, opposite to the lone-pair dipole, so they largely cancel (0.24 D). Statement (C) is true only for \(\mathrm{NH_3}\), so it is the incorrect one.
