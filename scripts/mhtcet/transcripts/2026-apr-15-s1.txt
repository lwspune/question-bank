chapter: Some Basic Concepts of Chemistry
subtopic: Stoichiometry and Concentration
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: B
stem:
What is the approximate mass of the precipitate formed when \(50\) mL of \(16.9\%\) solution of \(\mathrm{AgNO_3}\) is mixed with \(50\) mL of \(7.45\%\) KCl solution? (Molar mass of \(\mathrm{AgNO_3} = 169\) g/mol, \(\mathrm{KCl} = 74.5\) g/mol, AgCl \(= 143.3\) g/mol)
A: \(3.5\) g
B: \(7\) g
C: \(14\) g
D: \(28\) g
solution:
\(50\) mL of a \(16.9\%\) (w/v) solution holds \(8.45\) g of \(\mathrm{AgNO_3}\), that is \(\dfrac{8.45}{169} = 0.05\) mol.

\(50\) mL of a \(7.45\%\) solution holds \(3.725\) g of KCl, that is \(\dfrac{3.725}{74.5} = 0.05\) mol.

\(\mathrm{AgNO_3 + KCl \rightarrow AgCl\downarrow + KNO_3}\) reacts \(1:1\), so \(0.05\) mol of AgCl precipitates: \(0.05 \times 143.3 = 7.17\) g, about \(7\) g.

difficulty: EASY
answer: C
stem:
Which group elements have the smallest atomic radii in their respective periods?
A: group 15
B: group 16
C: group 17
D: group 18
solution:
Atomic radius falls across a period as the nuclear charge grows, so the halogens (group 17) are the smallest in each period. Noble gases (group 18) are quoted as van der Waals radii, which are larger than covalent radii, so they do not count as the smallest.

stem:
What happens to the size of atoms in p-block elements when we move from left to right in the same period?
A: Size does not change
B: Size increases then decreases
C: Size increases
D: Size decreases
solution:
Across a period electrons enter the same shell while the nuclear charge rises by one each step. The outer electrons are pulled in more strongly, so atomic size decreases from left to right.

=== 5
chapter: Chemical Bonding and Molecular Structure
subtopic: Dipole Moment, Polarity and Intermolecular Forces
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: D
stem:
Which of the following order of dipole moment for stated molecules is correct?
A: \(\mathrm{BF_3 > NF_3 > NH_3}\)
B: \(\mathrm{NF_3 > BF_3 > NH_3}\)
C: \(\mathrm{NH_3 > BF_3 > NF_3}\)
D: \(\mathrm{NH_3 > NF_3 > BF_3}\)
solution:
\(\mathrm{BF_3}\) is trigonal planar and symmetric, so its dipole moment is zero.

In \(\mathrm{NH_3}\) the N-H bond dipoles point towards N, the same way as the lone-pair moment, so they add (about \(1.47\) D). In \(\mathrm{NF_3}\) the N-F bond dipoles point away from N, against the lone-pair moment, so they partly cancel (about \(0.23\) D).

Order: \(\mathrm{NH_3 > NF_3 > BF_3}\).

Which of the following processes has \(\Delta S\) negative?
A: Dissolving Sugar in Water

C: \(2\mathrm{NH_3}(g) \rightarrow \mathrm{N_2}(g) + 3\mathrm{H_2}(g)\)

Which of the following properties of a system depends upon the amount of matter and path?
A: Heat
B: Free energy
C: Enthalpy
D: Entropy
solution:
Free energy, enthalpy and entropy are state functions: they depend on the amount of matter but not on the path. Heat is a path function, and the heat exchanged also grows with the amount of matter, so heat is the answer.
