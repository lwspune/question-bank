What is uncertainty in velocity of an electron if uncertainty in measurement of position is 50 pm ?
\((m_e = 9.1 \times 10^{-31}\mathrm{kg},\ h = 6.63 \times 10^{-34}\mathrm{Js},\ \pi = 3.142)\)
A: \(0.98 \times 10^6\ \mathrm{ms^{-1}}\)
