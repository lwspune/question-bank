subtopic: Mole Concept and Interconversions
difficulty: MODERATE

Which of the following contains maximum number of molecules?
A: \(0.4\) g \(\mathrm{H_2}\) gas
B: \(3.2\) g of \(\mathrm{O_2}\) gas

(C) has the most molecules.
