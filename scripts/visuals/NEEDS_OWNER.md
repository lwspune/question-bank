# Questions I could not fix (owner to decide)

Found during the animated-solutions work. A wrong SOLUTION is corrected in place
as found (`npm run visuals:fix-solutions`, batches in `solution-fixes/`). What
lands here is what that tool cannot or should not do: a wrong KEY, a wrong or
incomplete STEM, or a question that cannot be answered as printed. Each row
stays until it is settled by the owner's policy (below the Settled heading:
source first; a key the source prints wrong is corrected with a notice; a
question defective as printed keeps its key, gets a notice and joins the grace
list, `src/lib/mocks/flawed.json`). Settled rows move to the bottom with the
date and what was done.

Every row found up to 2026-10-29 is settled below. Of the 38 rows found on 2026-10-31 in
the SSC CGL Data Interpretation and Mensuration 3D animation run, 34 turned out to be
settled already at ingestion (`flawed: yes` in the transcript, a notice above the
solution, grace in mocks) and moved below on 2026-11-01. The 4 left were not defects either (rounded options,
the key the nearest one) and were animated; settled below.
