## Report

### 1. Figure numbers referenced but never printed
**None.** All 35 captions `Fig. 2.1` … `Fig. 2.35` are physically present on the pages, each
exactly once. No caption is missing, duplicated, or skipped. No book defect of this class in
this chapter.

Conversely, every numbered figure is actually *used* — no orphan figure printed with nothing
referring to it (the closest to an orphan are the pure-theory figures 2.1–2.10 and 2.25,
which the exposition refers to in prose rather than by question number).

### 2. Figures read by more than one question
**No figure is read by more than one numbered question.** The pairing is strictly 1:1 for
every question-bearing figure. Two near-misses worth knowing about, neither of which needs a
duplicated crop across two *questions*:

- **Fig. 2.10** is read by **two sub-items of the same "Remember this!" box** — item (1)(a)
  (similarity) and item (1)(b) (theorem of geometric mean, whose text opens "In the above
  figure"). If the ingest splits that box into two rows, both rows need this crop.
- **Fig. 2.8 + Fig. 2.9** are a **pair read by one proof** (the converse of Pythagoras: 2.8 is
  the given △ABC, 2.9 the constructed △PQR). This is the inverse of the multi-question case —
  one item needs *two* figures. They are printed side by side on the same page, so a stitched
  composite is optional; separate crops work, but the two must stay together on whatever row
  carries that proof, because neither is meaningful alone.

### 3. Figures spanning a page break
**No figure is physically split across a page break.** Every diagram is wholly contained on a
single page, with its caption directly beneath or beside it on the same page. The known
stitched-composite failure mode does not apply to this chapter.

**One question/figure page split, which is a different problem and does need attention:**

- **SE-B Ex. (1)** — the question text ("In the figure 2.26, seg PM is a median of △PQR.
  PM = 9 and PQ² + PR² = 290, then find QR") and the first two lines of its solution are on
  **`p-50` (printed 41)**, while **Fig. 2.26 and the rest of the solution are on `p-51`
  (printed 42)**. A band whose page range ends at `p-50` will emit this question with no
  figure and no crop-box, and will not error. The figure crop is clean and needs no stitching —
  it just has to be attached from the *next* page's render.

### 4. Large colour photographs / near-full-page art
**None.** Every figure in this chapter is a small black-line geometric diagram on white — no
photographs, no colour fills, no shading, no near-full-page art. The only colour on any of
these pages is the magenta chapter chrome (headers, the "Let's study / Let's recall / Let's
learn / Remember this / ICT Tools" boxes, the page-footer rule, the pink answer-boxes in the
two fill-in-the-blank activities) and the QR code on printed p.46 — none of which is a figure.

The largest figures by page area are **Fig. 2.34** (trapezium, ~18% of page) and **Fig. 2.4**
(trapezium proof, ~15%). Both are line art and will compress to a few tens of KB even at high
zoom. **The 1 MB storage cap is not a risk anywhere in this chapter** — no step-down or JPEG
fallback should be needed.

Two figures *do* contain magenta ink and are worth a deliberate colour crop rather than a
grayscale one: **Fig. 2.20** and **Fig. 2.23**, whose associated activities print pink
answer-boxes. The boxes sit in the *question text* beside the figure, not inside the figure —
the bboxes above deliberately exclude them.

### 6. Other things worth flagging before the diff

- **Fig. 2.16 has the tightest crop in the chapter.** Its caption sits at roughly the same
  height as the tail of the proof line `∴ AB² − BD² = AC² − CD² ……… from I and II`, which runs
  right up to the figure's `B` vertex label. Any box wide enough to keep the `B` label will
  probably clip the last word or two of that proof line. The bbox above chooses to keep `B`.
  **This one needs a human look at the crop** — it is the only figure in the chapter where
  "contains the diagram but no question text" cannot be satisfied exactly.
- **Fig. 2.29's owner is inferred from position, not from the text.** Practice set 2.2 Q.4
  reads "In △ABC, point M is the midpoint of side BC. If AB² + AC² = 290 cm², AM = 8 cm, find
  BC" and **never names a figure number**. Fig. 2.29 (△ABC with median AM and tick marks on
  BM/MC) is printed in the left column immediately beside Q.4 and matches it exactly, and no
  other question on the page is unaccounted for, so the attribution is safe — but it is the
  one row in this catalogue that rests on layout rather than on a citation. If a band drops
  this figure, that is the likely reason.
- **Two figures serve fill-in-the-blank "activity" questions** (Fig. 2.20 → Practice set 2.1
  Q.5, Fig. 2.23 → APP Ex. (1)). These are numbered questions with determinate answers, not
  open-ended activity boxes, so they should be ingested — but their stems carry pink empty
  answer-boxes that the text layer will render as nothing. If a band's stem for either of
  these looks truncated, the boxes are why.
- **Fig. 2.19's caption is to the RIGHT of its diagram, not below it** — the only figure in the
  chapter laid out that way. A crop rule that assumes "caption sits under the drawing" will
  cut the caption off.
- **Fig. 2.7's caption is also offset to the right** of its diagram (top-right of printed p.34),
  same hazard as 2.19 though less extreme.
