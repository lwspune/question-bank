In a certain code language,
‘M & N’ means ‘M is the wife of N’,
‘M @ N’ means ‘M is the brother of N’,

chapter: Syllogism
subtopic: Three-Statement Syllogism
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: D
stem:
Read the given statements and conclusions carefully. Assuming that the information given in the statements is true, even if it appears to be at variance with commonly known facts, decide which of the given conclusions logically follow(s) from the statements.
Statements:
All rings are bangles.
Some bangles are anklets.
No anklet is a pendant.
Conclusions:
(I) All rings being anklets is a possibility.
(II) All bangles can never be pendants.
A: Only conclusion II follows
B: None of the conclusions follow
C: Only conclusion I follows
D: Both conclusions I and II follow
solution:

II: some bangles are anklets and no anklet is a pendant, so those bangles are not pendants. Hence all bangles can never be pendants. II follows.

Both follow.

subtopic: Colloids and Suspensions
difficulty: EASY
answer: A
stem:
Which of the following contains small droplets of liquid or particles of solid dispersed in a gas?
A: Aerosol
B: Gel
C: Vapour
D: Foam
solution:
An aerosol is a colloid in which the dispersion medium is a gas and the dispersed phase is a liquid (fog, mist) or a solid (smoke, dust).

A gel is a liquid dispersed in a solid, a foam is a gas dispersed in a liquid, and a vapour is the gaseous state of a substance, not a colloid.

D: .docc

Which of the following is the second nearest star to Earth?
A: Proxima Centauri
B: Polaris
C: Sirius
D: Delta Velorum
solution:
The nearest star to Earth is the Sun. The next nearest is Proxima Centauri, a red dwarf in the Alpha Centauri system about 4.24 light-years away.

Sirius is about 8.6 light-years away, Delta Velorum about 80 and Polaris over 400, so none of them comes close.

=== 100
subject: Current Affairs
chapter: Awards, Honours, Books and Culture
subtopic: Indian Art, Architecture and Cultural Practices
difficulty: HARD
answer: A
stem:
Bharat Muni, in his Natya Shastra, categorises musical instruments into four different parts. What is the categorisation of musical instruments by Bharat Muni based on?
A: Different sounds of the musical instruments
B: The shape of the musical instruments
C: Different materials used in the musical instruments
D: The uses of the musical instruments in different occasions, such as wedding, festivals and public gathering
solution:
In the Natya Shastra, Bharata classifies instruments into four groups by how they produce their sound: tata vadya (stringed instruments, sound from vibrating strings), sushira vadya (wind instruments, sound from air in a hollow tube), avanaddha vadya (instruments with a membrane stretched over them, such as drums) and ghana vadya (solid instruments struck against each other, such as cymbals). The basis is the sound each kind produces, not the instrument's shape or the occasion it is used for.
