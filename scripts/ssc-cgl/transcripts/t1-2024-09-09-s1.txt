=== 1
chapter: Mathematical Operations
subtopic: Value After Interchanging Signs
difficulty: EASY
answer: D
stem:
What will come in place of the question mark (?) in the following equation, if ‘+’ and ‘–’ are interchanged and ‘\(\times\)’ and ‘\(\div\)’ are interchanged?
\(4515 \times 5 - 431 \div 3 + 821 = ?\)
A: 1335
B: 1775
C: 1575
D: 1375
solution:
After the interchange the expression is \(4515 \div 5 + 431 \times 3 - 821\).

\(4515 \div 5 = 903\) and \(431 \times 3 = 1293\), so \(903 + 1293 - 821 = 1375\).

=== 12
chapter: Syllogism
subtopic: Three-Statement Syllogism
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: A
stem:
Three statements are given followed by three conclusions numbered I, II and III. Assuming the statements to be true, even if they seem to be at variance with commonly known facts, decide which of the conclusions logically follow(s) from the statements.

Statements:
No card is a parcel.
All cards are envelopes.
Some envelopes are bags.

Conclusions:
I. All envelopes can never be parcels.
II. No card is a bag.
III. At least some bags are postcards.
A: Only I follows
B: Only II follows
C: Only I and III follow
D: Only II and III follow
solution:
All cards are envelopes and no card is a parcel, so some envelopes (the cards) are never parcels. All envelopes can therefore never be parcels: I follows.

Cards and bags may or may not overlap, so "No card is a bag" is not definite: II does not follow.

Postcards are not mentioned in the statements: III does not follow.

=== 23
chapter: Blood Relations
subtopic: Coded Statements and Expressions
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: B
stem:
In a certain code language,
‘A + B’ means ‘A is the mother of B’,
‘A – B’ means ‘A is the father of B’,
‘A $ B’ means ‘A is the son of B’,
‘A % B’ means ‘A is the sister of B’,
‘A / B’ means ‘A is the wife of B’ and
‘A = B’ means ‘A is the brother of B’.

Which of the following means A is the daughter of K?
i. A + K / J – P = D
ii. J % P = K + A = D
iii. K + A / P – D % J
iv. D + K = A / P – J
A: i
B: iii
C: ii
D: iv
solution:
A must be K's child and female.

i: A + K makes A the mother of K. ii: K + A makes K the mother of A, but A = D makes A a brother, so A is a son. iv: K = A makes K the brother of A.

iii: K + A makes K the mother of A, and A / P makes A the wife of P, so A is female: A is the daughter of K.
