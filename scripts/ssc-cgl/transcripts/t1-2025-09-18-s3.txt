stem:
If 6 @ 2 = 9, 15 @ 3 =125, then 8 @ 4 = ?
A: 4
B: 8
C: 16
D: 10
solution:
The first number is divided by the second, and the quotient is raised to the power of the second number: \((6 \div 2)^2 = 9\) and \((15 \div 3)^3 = 125\). So \(8 @ 4 = (8 \div 4)^4 = 16\).

subtopic: Scheduling and Puzzles
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: A
stem:
Direction: Study the information carefully and answer the given questions.

Eight People Tina, Raj, Dia, Viraj, Soma, Meena, Neha and Ria are living in an eight-storey building, but not necessarily in the same order. The ground floor is numbered 1 and the floor above it is numbered 2, and so on, until the topmost floor is numbered 8. Dia lives on the lowermost floor. Only three people live between Dia and Ria. Tina lives on the 4th floor. Only two people live between Tina and Raj. Only five people live between Viraj and Soma. Neha lives on an odd numbered floor. Only one person lives between Meena and Viraj.

Who lives three floors below the one who lives immediate below Raj?
A: Neha
B: Tina
C: Dia
D: Raj
solution:
Dia is on 1, so Ria is on 5. Tina is on 4, so Raj is on 7 (floor 1 is taken). Viraj and Soma need five people between them, and only floors 2 and 8 are left as a pair, so they take 2 and 8. Meena is two floors from Viraj; floor 4 is Tina's, so Viraj is on 8 and Meena on 6, leaving Soma on 2 and Neha on 3 (odd, as required).

The person immediately below Raj (7) is Meena (6), and three floors below her is floor 3: Neha.
