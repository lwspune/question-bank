chapter: Mathematical Operations
subtopic: Hidden Operation Rules
difficulty: EASY
answer: C
stem:
If 6 + 2 = 16, 7 + 3 = 25, then 8 + 4 = ?
A: 32
B: 28
C: 36
D: 27
solution:
The rule is the square of the sum, divided by 4: \(\frac{(6+2)^2}{4} = \frac{64}{4} = 16\) and \(\frac{(7+3)^2}{4} = \frac{100}{4} = 25\).

So 8 + 4 gives \(\frac{12^2}{4} = \frac{144}{4} = 36\).

A second reading gives the same answer: product plus 4, \(6 \times 2 + 4 = 16\), \(7 \times 3 + 4 = 25\), \(8 \times 4 + 4 = 36\).

chapter: Order, Ranking and Arrangement
subtopic: Scheduling and Puzzles
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: B
stem:
Directions: Study the following information carefully and answer the questions based on it.

Comprehension:
Seven boxes are kept one above another to make up a stack. The topmost box is numbered as 1, while the bottommost box is numbered as 7. Each box contains different fruits, including Apples, Oranges, Cherries, Bananas, Melons, Kiwis, and Mangoes. Each box contains a different number of fruit: 10, 20, 30, 40, 50, 60, and 70.
There are 20 Oranges. The box of Bananas is two boxes above the box of Melon. There are 70 Melons. There are 20 and 60 fruits in the topmost and bottommost boxes respectively. The box of Apples is just above the box of bananas. The box of Kiwi is just above the box of Mango. There are 30 Cherries in the box. The box containing 50 fruits is just above the box containing 10 fruits.
There are ___ Apples.
A: 60
B: 50
C: 70
D: 80
solution:
Box 1 has 20 fruits and there are 20 Oranges, so box 1 is Oranges. Box 7 has 60, so it is not Melons (70) or Cherries (30).

Apples, Bananas, a gap, then Melons take four boxes in a row. With them in boxes 2, 3 and 5, the boxes left are 4, 6 and 7; Kiwi just above Mango gives Kiwi 6, Mango 7 and Cherries 4. With them in 3, 4 and 6, the boxes left (2, 5, 7) have no two in a row for Kiwi and Mango. With Melons in 7 it would hold 60, not 70. So the stack is Oranges, Apples, Bananas, Cherries, Melons, Kiwi, Mango.

The counts left are 10, 40 and 50 for Apples (2), Bananas (3) and Kiwi (6). The 50 box is just above the 10 box; boxes 2 and 3 are the only such pair still free, so Apples have 50, Bananas 10 and Kiwi 40.

In a family there are eight members, P, Q, R, S, T, U, V, and W. V is the sister of the mother of U. W is the father of Q who is the brother of U. R is married to V. T is the father-in-law of W. S is the daughter of R.

chapter: Syllogism
subtopic: Two-Statement Syllogism
difficulty: EASY
answer: B
stem:
Read the following statement carefully and identify the conclusion that follows.

Statement:
All books are papers. Some papers are journals.

**Conclusions:**
I. Some journals are books.
II. Some papers are books.
A: Only I follows
B: Only II follows
C: Both I and II follow
D: Neither I nor II follows
solution:
All books are papers, so some papers are books: II follows.

The journals are only some of the papers, and they may lie entirely outside the books, so I does not follow. I and II are not a complementary pair, so the either-or rule does not apply.

Statement:
Some cars are electric. All electric vehicles are eco-friendly. Some eco-friendly things are expensive.

II. Some expensive things are not cars.

B: Only II follows
C: Both I and II follow

chapter: Figure Counting
subtopic: Counting Squares and Other Figures
difficulty: HARD
answer: C
flawed: yes
notice: SSC's provisional key gives 22, which holds only if no slanting line met a corner; as printed, the top-left, top-right and bottom-right corner pieces are triangles, so the count is 19, the answer shown.
note: SSC key B (22) counts every strip as a quadrilateral (left 6 + right 15 + whole 1). In the printed figure two lines meet the top of the middle line and one ends in the bottom-right corner, leaving three triangles: left 5 + right 13 + whole 1 = 19. Ours kept (counted again in the main session on the full-size figure).
figure: stem
stem:
How many **quadrilaterals** are there in the given figure?
A: 21
B: 22
C: 19
D: 18
solution:
The figure is a rectangle split by a vertical line in the middle. Call the top of the middle line M.

Left half: besides the top and bottom edges, two slanting lines run from the left edge to the middle line, the upper one to M. Any two of these four lines (top edge, upper slant, lower slant, bottom edge) bound a quadrilateral, except the top edge with the upper slant, which meet at M and make a triangle. That is \(6 - 1 = 5\).

Right half: besides the top and bottom edges, four slanting lines run from the middle line to the right edge; the top one starts at M and the bottom one ends at the bottom-right corner. Any two of these six lines bound a quadrilateral, except the top edge with the top slant (meet at M) and the bottom slant with the bottom edge (meet at the corner). That is \(15 - 2 = 13\).

Across both halves, a straight top and bottom line are needed; only the top and bottom edges run straight across (the slants bend at the middle line, making pentagons), so the whole rectangle adds 1.

Total = 5 + 13 + 1 = 19.
