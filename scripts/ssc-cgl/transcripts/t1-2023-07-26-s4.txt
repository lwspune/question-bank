subtopic: Coded Statements and Expressions
difficulty: MODERATE

stem:
‘A + B’ means ‘A is the brother of B’.
‘A – B’ means ‘A is the mother of B’.
‘A \(\times\) B’ means ‘A is the husband of B’.
‘A \(\div\) B’ means ‘A is the sister of B’.
If P \(\div\) Q – R + S – K \(\times\) L, then which of the following statements is correct?.
A: Q is L’s mother
B: R is K’s mother’s brother
C: Q is S’s sister
D: R is L’s husband’s brother
solution:
P is Q's sister; Q is R's mother; R is S's brother; S is K's mother; K is L's husband.

subtopic: Alphanumeric and Pattern Series
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: D
stem:
Select the figure from the options that can replace the question mark (?) and complete the given pattern.

| S L A T E D | A L S D E T | T E L S D A | L E T A D S | ? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
A: S T E D A L
B: S L E T A D
C: S D A T E L
D: S D E T A L
solution:
Number the positions 1 to 6. From SLATED to ALSDET, letters 1 and 3 swap and letters 4 and 6 swap. TELSDA to LETADS uses the same swaps.
