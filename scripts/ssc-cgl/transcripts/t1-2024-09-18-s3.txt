=== 1
chapter: Blood Relations
subtopic: Coded Relations
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: C
stem:
In a certain code language,
‘A + B’ means ‘A is the mother of B’;
‘A – B’ means ‘ A is the father of B’;
‘A $ B’ means ‘A is the daughter of B’ and
‘A % B’ means ‘A is the sister of B’.
Based on the above, how is P related to S if P + Q − R $ S % T?
A: Mother
B: Sister’s daughter
C: Husband’s mother
D: Sister’s husband
solution:
P + Q: P is the mother of Q. Q − R: Q is the father of R. R $ S: R is the daughter of S. S % T: S is the sister of T, so S is female.

R's father is Q and R's parent S is female, so S is R's mother and Q's wife.

P is the mother of Q, S's husband. So P is S's husband's mother.

In a certain code language, ‘MORE’ is coded as ‘14 - 12 - 9 - 22’ and ‘KNOW’ is coded as ‘16 - 13 - 12 - 4’. How will ‘SAID’ be coded in the given language?
A: 8 - 26 - 18 - 23
B: 8 - 26 - 17 - 23
C: 7 - 25 - 18 - 23
D: 7 - 26 - 18 - 22
solution:

chapter: Syllogism
subtopic: Three-Statement Syllogism
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: B
stem:
In this question, three statements are given, followed by two conclusions numbered I and II. Assuming the statements to be true, even if they seem to be at variance with commonly known facts, decide which of the conclusion(s) logically follows/follow from the statements.
Statements:
No medicine is a capsule.
Some capsules are tablets.
Some tablets are laptops.
Conclusions:
I. Some capsules are laptops.
II. All tablets can never be medicines.
A: Neither Conclusion I nor II follow.
B: Only Conclusion II follows.
C: Both Conclusions I and II follow.
D: Only Conclusion I follows.
solution:
I: the tablets that are laptops need not be the tablets that are capsules, so capsules and laptops may not overlap. I does not follow.

II: some tablets are capsules, and no capsule is a medicine, so those tablets are not medicines. Hence all tablets can never be medicines. II follows.

=== 14
chapter: Syllogism
subtopic: Three-Statement Syllogism
difficulty: EASY
answer: A
stem:
Read the given statements and conclusions carefully. You have to take the given statements to be true even if they seem to be at variance from commonly known facts. You have to decide which conclusion/s logically follow/s from the given statements.
Statements:
All beaches are sand.
Some deserts are sand.
All mountains are rocky.
Conclusions:
(I) At least some beaches are desert.
(II) At least some sands are rocky.
A: Neither conclusion (I) nor (II) follows.
B: Both conclusions (I) and (II) follows.
C: Only conclusion (I) follows.
D: Only conclusion (II) follows.
solution:
Beaches and deserts both lie inside sand, but they need not overlap, so (I) does not follow.

Sand and rocky are not linked by any statement (mountains are rocky, but mountains are not linked to sand), so (II) does not follow.

chapter: Analogy
subtopic: Square and Cube Rules
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: B
stem:
40 is related to 53 following certain logic. Following the same logic, 8 is related to 13. To which of the following numbers is 68 related, following the same logic? (NOTE : Operations should be performed on the whole numbers, without breaking down the numbers into its constituent digits. E.g. 13 – Operations on 13 such as adding /deleting /multiplying etc., to 13 can be performed. Breaking down 13 into 1 and 3 and then performing mathematical operations on 1 and 3 is not allowed.)
A: 84
B: 85
C: 83
D: 73
solution:
