=== 1
chapter: Syllogism
subtopic: Three-Statement Syllogism
difficulty: EASY
answer: C
stem:
Read the given statements and conclusions carefully. Assuming that the information given in the statements is true, even if it appears to be at variance with commonly known facts, decide which of the given conclusion(s) logically follow(s) from the statements.

Statements:
All pastries are chocolates.
Some chocolates are cookies.
All cookies are cakes.

Conclusions:
(I) No pastry is a cake.
(II) Some cakes are chocolates.
A: Both conclusions I and II follow.
B: Neither conclusion I nor II follows.
C: Only conclusion II follows.
D: Only conclusion I follows.
solution:
Some chocolates are cookies and all cookies are cakes, so those chocolates are cakes: some cakes are chocolates. II follows.

Nothing links pastries to cakes either way. Pastries may or may not overlap cakes, so "No pastry is a cake" is not definite. I does not follow. Only II follows.

solution:

chapter: Mole Concept and Stoichiometry
subtopic: Stoichiometry and Laws of Chemical Combination
difficulty: EASY
answer: C
stem:
How many carbon dioxide and water molecules will be there in the product side, if the following equation is made balanced?
\(\text{C}_2\text{H}_5\text{OH}+\text{O}_2\rightarrow\text{CO}_2+\text{H}_2\text{O}\)

B: 1 and 1, respectively
C: 2 and 3, respectively
