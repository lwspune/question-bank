A person walks 5 km to a destination in 1 hour. On the return journey, they walk the same 5 km distance back to the starting point in 30 minutes. What is the average speed of the person for the entire journey?

subtopic: Hidden Operation Rules
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: C
stem:
If 5 @ 2 = 17 and 6 @ 3 = 27, what is 7 @ 4?
A: 35
B: 37
C: 39
D: 41
solution:
The rule is \(a @ b = a \times b + a + b\): \(10 + 5 + 2 = 17\) and \(18 + 6 + 3 = 27\).

So \(7 @ 4 = 28 + 7 + 4 = 39\).

Check with the equivalent form \((a + 1)(b + 1) - 1\): \(8 \times 5 - 1 = 39\).

Which of the following best captures the key rituals and symbolism of the Nocte tribe's Loku festival?

B: "Chamkatja" includes prayers at Chaatam, Chamwangbong dance, and gunfire at the chief's house.
