Four letter-clusters have been given, out of which three are alike in some manner and one is different. Select the letter-cluster that is different.

C: LJO
D: OMQ

subtopic: Three-Statement Syllogism
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: B
stem:
Three statements are given followed by three conclusions numbered I, II and III. Assuming the statements to be true, even if they seem to be at variance with commonly known facts, decide which of the conclusions logically follow(s) from the statements.

Statements:
Some cups are glasses.
No glass is a stone.
All stones are metals.

Conclusions:
I. All cups can never be stones.
II. No cup is a metal.
III. All metals can never be glasses.
A: Only II and III conclusion follow
B: Only I and III conclusion follow
C: Only conclusion I follows
D: All I, II and III conclusion follow
solution:

II: cups may or may not be metals; nothing rules it out. II does not follow.

III: every stone is a metal and no stone is a glass, so some metals are not glasses and all metals can never be glasses. III follows.

chapter: Figure Counting
subtopic: Counting Triangles
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: B
figure: stem
stem:
How many triangles are there in the given figure?
A: 10
B: 11
C: 13
D: 12
solution:
The figure is a square (no top side) with both diagonals, under a roof: lines from the apex to each top corner of the square and to the two eaves, with a short line in each eave triangle from the roof edge to the square's corner.

Each eave gives 3 triangles (the two small ones and the whole eave triangle): \(3 \times 2 = 6\).

The square gives 5: the bottom triangle under the crossing, the two side triangles at the crossing, and the two half-squares cut off by each diagonal with the bottom side. There is no top side, so the top triangle and any triangle with the apex across the square are not closed.

Total \(6 + 5 = 11\).
