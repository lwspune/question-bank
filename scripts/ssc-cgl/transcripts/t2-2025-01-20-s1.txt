chapter: Circles
subtopic: Tangents from an External Point
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: B
stem:
In a circle centered at O, a diameter AB is extended to a point C outside the circle. CD is a tangent at point D on the circle. If CD = \(7\sqrt{3}\) cm and \(\angle DBC = 120^\circ\), then the length of the diameter of the circle is equal to:
A: 16 cm
B: 14 cm
C: 18 cm
D: 20 cm
solution:
B lies between A and C, so \(\angle DBO = 180^\circ - 120^\circ = 60^\circ\).

OD = OB (radii), so triangle ODB has \(\angle ODB = \angle OBD = 60^\circ\): it is equilateral and \(\angle DOC = 60^\circ\).

The radius OD is perpendicular to the tangent CD, so in right triangle ODC, \(\tan 60^\circ = \frac{CD}{OD}\), giving \(OD = \frac{7\sqrt{3}}{\sqrt{3}} = 7\) cm. The diameter is \(14\) cm.

Check: \(OC = 2 \times OD = 14\) (since \(\cos 60^\circ = \frac{OD}{OC}\)), and \(OC^2 = 49 + 147 = 196\), which is \(14^2\).

subtopic: Angles in a Circle
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: A
stem:
In a circle with centre O, an arc ABC subtends an angle of \(134^\circ\) at the centre of the circle. The chord AB is produced to a point P. \(\angle CBP\) is equal to:
A: \(67^\circ\)
B: \(113^\circ\)
C: \(45^\circ\)
D: \(89^\circ\)
solution:
The arc ABC (through B) subtends \(134^\circ\) at O, so the other arc AC, not containing B, subtends \(360^\circ - 134^\circ = 226^\circ\).

\(\angle ABC\) is an inscribed angle on that other arc: \(\angle ABC = \frac{226^\circ}{2} = 113^\circ\).

P is on AB produced, so \(\angle CBP = 180^\circ - 113^\circ = 67^\circ\).

Check: take any point D on arc AC away from B; ABCD is cyclic, so \(\angle CBP = \angle ADC = \frac{134^\circ}{2} = 67^\circ\).

stem:
A tank when full can be emptied by an outlet pipe A in 5.6 hours, while an inlet pipe B can fill the same empty tank in 7 hours. If pipes A and B are turned on alternatively for 1 hour each starting with pipe A when the tank is full, how long will it take to empty the tank?
A: 48 hours
B: 47 hours
C: 56 hours
D: 55 hours
solution:
Take the tank as 28 units (LCM of 5.6 and 7 in suitable units). A empties \(\frac{28}{5.6} = 5\) units an hour and B fills \(\frac{28}{7} = 4\) units an hour.

After 23 cycles (46 hours) \(28 - 23 = 5\) units remain, and A empties them in the 47th hour. (After 22 cycles 6 units remain, too many for A in one hour.)

Total time: 47 hours.

chapter: Probability
subtopic: Independent and Mutually Exclusive Events
difficulty: EASY
answer: C
note: Two options are true as printed. With P(A) = 1/2, P(A and B) = 1/4 = P(A)P(B), so A and B are independent (C, the evident intent); but P(A and B) is not 0, so "Not Mutually Exclusive" (A) is also true.
flawed: yes

stem:
If A and B are two events such that \(P(A \cup B) = \frac{3}{4}\), \(P(A \cap B) = \frac{1}{4}\) and \(P(B) = \frac{1}{2}\), then the events A and B are ____.
A: Not Mutually Exclusive
B: Mutually Exclusive
C: Independent
D: Dependent

(They are also not mutually exclusive, since \(P(A \cap B) \ne 0\); the question is testing independence.)
