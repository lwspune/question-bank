chapter: Syllogism
subtopic: Three-Statement Syllogism
difficulty: EASY
answer: B
stem:
Three statements are given followed by three conclusions numbered I, II and III. Assuming the statements to be true, even if they seem to be at variance with commonly known facts, decide which of the conclusions logically follow(s) from the statements.

Statements:
Some teachers are scientists.
All scientists are doctors.
All doctors are engineers.

Conclusions:
I. Some doctors are teachers.
II. Some engineers are teachers.
III. Some doctors are scientists.
A: Only conclusions I and II follow
B: All conclusions follow
C: Only conclusions I and III follow
D: Only conclusions II and III follow
solution:
The teachers who are scientists are doctors (all scientists are doctors), so some doctors are teachers: I follows.

Those same people are engineers (all doctors are engineers), so some engineers are teachers: II follows.

All scientists are doctors, so some doctors are scientists: III follows. All three follow.

chapter: Dice and Cubes
subtopic: Turning About a Common Face
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: D
figure: stem
stem:
Two different positions of the same dice are shown. Identify the letter on the face opposite the face showing ‘X’.
A: W
B: S
C: V
D: T
solution:
First position: X on top, U at front left, S at front right. Second position: S on top, U at front left, T at front right.

U is common. Going round U in the same direction, the first position gives X then S, and the second gives S then T. So the faces round U run X, S, T, and the fourth. X and T are two steps apart in that ring, so they are opposite.

S cannot be opposite X, since both show together in the first position. The answer is T.
