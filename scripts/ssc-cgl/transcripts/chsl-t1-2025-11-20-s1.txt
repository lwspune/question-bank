=== 26
chapter: Arithmetic Reasoning
subtopic: Equations from Word Problems
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: A
stem:
Some friends planned on a leisure trip where they had planned Rs.96 on eatables. 4 of them did not come. So the rest of them had to pay Rs. 4 each extra. The number of people who turned up on the trip was:
A: 8
B: 12
C: 16
D: 24
solution:
Let \(x\) friends plan the trip. Then \(\frac{96}{x - 4} - \frac{96}{x} = 4\).

So \(96 \times 4 = 4x(x - 4)\), that is \(x(x - 4) = 96\), giving \(x = 12\).

The number who turned up is \(12 - 4 = 8\).

Check: 96 shared by 12 is Rs. 8 each, shared by 8 is Rs. 12 each, which is Rs. 4 extra.
