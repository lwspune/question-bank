From the alternatives given below, select the correct passive voice of the following sentence.
Do not put your hand over the stove lest you should burn your fingers.

C runs alone for L minutes, then all three run for 4 minutes: \(\frac{7L}{480} + \frac{4 \times 37}{480} = 1\).

\(7L = 480 - 148 = 332\), so \(L = \frac{332}{7}\).

(Check: \(\frac{7}{480} \times \frac{332}{7} = \frac{332}{480}\), and \(\frac{332 + 148}{480} = 1\). If C were taken as closed after A and B open, \(7L = 480 - 120 = 360\), which is not an option.)
