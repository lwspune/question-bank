chapter: Spotting Errors
subtopic: Prepositions, Word Choice and Idioms
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: D
stem:
The following sentence has been split into four segments. Identify the segment that contains a grammatical error.
In geography class, / the teacher told the students, / "Switzerland lies / inside Germany, France, Austria, and Italy."
A: Switzerland lies
B: In geography class,
C: the teacher told the students,
D: inside Germany, France, Austria, and Italy.
solution:
Switzerland is not inside these countries; it lies between (among) them, surrounded by them. The preposition "inside" is wrong: "Switzerland lies between Germany, France, Austria, and Italy." The other segments are correct.

chapter: Blood Relations
subtopic: Coded Statements and Expressions
difficulty: EASY
answer: B
stem:
In a certain code language,
‘A + B’ means ‘A is the sister of B’,
‘A – B’ means ‘A is the brother of B’,
‘A \(\times\) B’ means ‘A is the father of B’,
‘A \(\div\) B’ means ‘A is the wife of B’.
Based on the above, how is P related to T if ‘P + Q \(\div\) R \(\times\) S − T’?
A: Father’s sister
B: Mother’s sister
C: Sister
D: Mother
solution:
S − T: S is T's brother. R \(\times\) S: R is S's father, so R is also T's father.

Q \(\div\) R: Q is R's wife, so Q is T's mother. P + Q: P is Q's sister.

So P is the sister of T's mother: T's mother's sister.

Read the given statements and conclusions carefully. Assuming that the information given in the statements is true, even if it appears to be at variance with commonly known facts, decide which of the given conclusions logically follow(s) from the statements.

How many rectangles are there in the given figure?

difficulty: MODERATE

stem:
If 19 February 2004 was a Thursday, then what was the day of the week on 2 March 2019?
A: Friday
B: Monday
C: Saturday
D: Thursday
solution:
From 19 February 2004 to 19 February 2019 is 15 years. The leap days 29 February 2004, 2008, 2012 and 2016 fall in between, so the day moves 15 + 4 = 19 days, which is 2 weeks and 5 days. Thursday + 5 = Tuesday: 19 February 2019 was a Tuesday.

February 2019 has 28 days, so 2 March 2019 is 9 + 2 = 11 days later, which is 1 week and 4 days. Tuesday + 4 = Saturday.

Who calculated the approximate location of the planet Neptune by studying gravity- induced disturbances in the motion of Uranus?

Which of the following is called the cyclic arrangement of time units in Hindustani Classical music?
A: Tala
B: Raga
C: Jati
D: Alap
solution:
Tala is the rhythmic cycle of a fixed number of beats (matras) grouped into sections (vibhags), such as Teental of 16 beats, which repeats through a performance.

Raga is the melodic framework, Jati classifies ragas or talas by the number of notes or beats, and Alap is the slow unmetred opening of a raga.
