=== 1
chapter: Spotting Errors
subtopic: Extra, Missing and Place-Time Prepositions
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: D
stem:
The following sentence has been split into four segments. Identify the segment that contains a grammatical error.
The couple / preferred to walk / until the last house / of the town.
A: of the town
B: preferred to walk
C: The couple
D: until the last house
solution:
*Until* marks a limit in time, not in place. A limit of distance takes *up to*, *till* or *as far as*: the couple preferred to walk up to the last house of the town. The error is in "until the last house".

chapter: Analogy
subtopic: Letter Analogy
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: C
stem:
LBXF is related to SYCM in a certain way based on the English alphabetical order. In the same way, TGFY is related to AGHF. To which of the following is ZDMK related, following the same logic?
A: GREN
B: GRNE
C: GNER
D: GNRE
solution:
The first and last letters each move 7 places forward: L to S and F to M; T to A and Y to F.

The middle two letters swap places and each moves 1 place forward: LBXF gives X + 1 = Y, then B + 1 = C, so SYCM; TGFY gives F + 1 = G, then G + 1 = H, so AGHF.

For ZDMK: Z + 7 = G, M + 1 = N, D + 1 = E, K + 7 = R. The answer is GNER.

chapter: Syllogism
subtopic: Three-Statement Syllogism
difficulty: EASY
answer: C
stem:
Read the given statements and conclusions carefully. Assuming that the information given in the statements is true, even if it appears to be at variance with commonly known facts, decide which of the given conclusions logically follow(s) from the statements.
Statements:
Some keys are chains.
All chains are locks.
No lock is a rock.
Conclusions:
(I) No rock is a key.
(II) At least some keys are locks.
A: Only conclusion I follows
B: Both conclusions I and II follow
C: Only conclusion II follows
D: None of the conclusion follow
solution:
The keys that are chains are locks, so at least some keys are locks: II follows.

stem:
Identify the figure given in the options that when put in place of the question mark (?) will logically complete the series.
A: [figure]
B: [figure]

solution:
Each figure holds five symbols along the diagonal from top left to bottom right. Reading them in that order: figure 1 is triangle, L, dome, F, 3; figure 2 is 3, triangle, L, dome, F; figure 3 is triangle, 3, dome, L, U; figure 4 is U, triangle, 3, dome, L.

‘A + B’ means ‘A is the brother of B’.
‘A \(-\) B’ means ‘A is the wife of B’.
‘A \(\times\) B’ means ‘A is the father of B’.
