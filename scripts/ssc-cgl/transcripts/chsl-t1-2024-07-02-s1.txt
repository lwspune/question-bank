chapter: Figure Counting
subtopic: Counting Squares and Other Figures
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: B
figure: stem
stem:
What is the maximum number of rectangles in the given figure?
A: 12
B: 9
C: 10
D: 8
solution:
The figure is a wide rectangle cut by two vertical lines into three strips, a tall strip whose middle part is shared with the wide rectangle (so it rises above it), and a separate low rectangle at the left.

Wide rectangle: it has 4 vertical lines (its two sides and the two inner lines) between one top and one bottom line, so it holds \(\binom{4}{2} = 6\) rectangles.

Tall strip: the part above the wide rectangle is 1 rectangle, and the whole strip from its top to the bottom is 1 more: 2.

Low rectangle at the left: 1 (its top line does not continue, so it joins nothing else).

Total \(6 + 2 + 1 = 9\).

chapter: Syllogism
subtopic: Three-Statement Syllogism
difficulty: EASY
answer: D
stem:
Three statements are followed by conclusions numbered I, II. You have to consider these statements to be true, even if they seem to be at variance with commonly known facts. Decide which of the given conclusions logically follow/s from the given statement.

Statements:
All journals are books.
Some books are fans.
All fans are wood.
Conclusion (I): Some journals are wood.
Conclusion (II): Some fans are books.
A: Only conclusion (I) follows.
B: Neither conclusion (I) nor (II) follows.
C: Both conclusions (I) and (II) follow.
D: Only conclusion (II) follows.
solution:
"Some books are fans" converts to "Some fans are books", so II follows.

The books that are fans (and so wood) need not include any journal: journals can sit in the part of books that are not fans. So I does not follow. Only II follows.

chapter: Calendar and Clock
subtopic: Calendar
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: D
stem:
If 9 March 2007 was Friday, then what was the day of the week on 13 March 2012?
A: Wednesday
B: Monday
C: Thursday
D: Tuesday
solution:
From 9 March of one year to 9 March of the next the day moves 1 forward, or 2 when the 29 February of a leap year falls in between.

2007 to 2008 (29 Feb 2008 in between): 2. 2008 to 2009: 1. 2009 to 2010: 1. 2010 to 2011: 1. 2011 to 2012 (29 Feb 2012 in between): 2. Total 7 days, so 9 March 2012 was again a Friday.

13 March 2012 is 4 days later: Saturday, Sunday, Monday, Tuesday.

subtopic: Symbol Grids
difficulty: HARD

figure: stem+options
stem:
Identify the figure given in the options that when put in place of the question mark (?) will logically complete the series.
A: [figure]
B: [figure]
C: [figure]
D: [figure]
solution:
Each figure holds six symbols at six places: top left, top right, centre, bottom left, bottom middle, bottom right. The four figures read:

Figure 1: P, 4, \(\uparrow\) (top left, top right, centre); triangle, V, 3 (bottom row).
Figure 2: \(\uparrow\), P, 4; 3, triangle, V.
Figure 3: V, \(\uparrow\), P; 4, L, 3.
Figure 4: P, V, \(\uparrow\); 3, 4, L.

From figure 1 to 2 (and again 3 to 4) the top three turn round (top left to top right to centre to top left) and the bottom row moves one step right, the last one coming round to the left.

From figure 2 to 3 the symbols instead move along top left, top right, centre, bottom left, bottom right and back to top left, while the bottom-middle symbol is replaced by a new one (the triangle gives way to L).

So figure 5 follows the 2 to 3 step applied to figure 4: P goes to top right, V to the centre, \(\uparrow\) to bottom left, 3 to bottom right, L to top left, and the bottom-middle 4 is replaced by a new symbol. That is L, P, V; \(\uparrow\), =, 3: option 3.

The loss of water in the form of water droplets from leaves of plants is called _____.
A: Translocation
B: Plasmolysis
C: Pressure gradient
D: Guttation
solution:
Guttation is the loss of water as liquid droplets from the leaf margins through hydathodes, driven by root pressure, usually at night or early morning. Transpiration, by contrast, loses water as vapour.

Translocation is the movement of food in the phloem, and plasmolysis is the shrinking of a cell's protoplast away from its wall in a hypertonic solution.

=== 88
subject: Geography
chapter: Climatology, Atmosphere and Weather
subtopic: Insolation, Temperature and Solar Geometry
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: A
stem:
Which theory describes the collective effects of changes in Earth's movements on its climate over thousands of years?
A: Milankovitch theory
B: Copernicus' heliocentric theory
C: Plate tectonics theory
D: Continental drift theory
solution:
Milutin Milankovitch showed that cyclic changes in Earth's orbit (eccentricity), axial tilt (obliquity) and the wobble of its axis (precession) change the sunlight Earth receives, driving climate changes such as ice ages over tens of thousands of years.

The heliocentric theory places the Sun at the centre of the solar system, and plate tectonics and continental drift explain the movement of the crust, not orbital effects on climate.
