chapter: Blood Relations
subtopic: Coded Relations
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: B
stem:
In a certain code language,
A + B means ‘A is the husband of B’,
A \(-\) B means ‘B is the mother of A’,
A \(\times\) B means ‘B is the sister of A’ and
A \(\div\) B means ‘A is the daughter of B’.
Based on the above, how is Q related to T if ‘\(P + Q \times R - S \div T\)’?
A: Sister
B: Daughter’s daughter
C: Mother’s mother
D: Daughter’s husband
solution:
\(P + Q\): P is the husband of Q, so Q is female.

\(Q \times R\): R is the sister of Q.

\(R - S\): S is the mother of R, so S is also Q's mother.

\(S \div T\): S is the daughter of T.

Q is the daughter of T's daughter: Q is T's daughter's daughter.
