chapter: Analogy
subtopic: Letter Analogy
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: B
stem:
Select the option that is related to the fifth letter-cluster in the same way as the second letter-cluster is related to the first letter-cluster and the fourth letter-cluster is related to the third letter-cluster.
CWE:ZFX ::PYT:MDI :: KIL:?
A: PRO
B: RTQ
C: PQO
D: STQ
solution:
Each letter is replaced by its opposite letter (the pair that adds to 27 in position) and then moved 2 places forward.

CWE: C to X to Z, W to D to F, E to V to X, giving ZFX. PYT: P to K to M, Y to B to D, T to G to I, giving MDI.

KIL: K to P to R, I to R to T, L to O to Q, giving RTQ.

chapter: Classification
subtopic: Number Classification
difficulty: EASY
answer: B
stem:
Three of the following numbers are alike in a certain way and one is different. Pick the odd one out.
(NOTE : Operations should be performed on the whole numbers, without breaking down the numbers into its constituent digits. E.g. 13 – Operations on 13 such as adding /deleting /multiplying etc., to 13 can be performed. Breaking down 13 into 1 and 3 and then performing mathematical operations on 1 and 3 is not allowed.)
A: 36 – 84
B: 33 – 72
C: 66 – 154
D: 72 – 168
solution:
In each pair the numbers are in the ratio 3 : 7: \(36 = 12 \times 3\) and \(84 = 12 \times 7\); \(66 = 22 \times 3\) and \(154 = 22 \times 7\); \(72 = 24 \times 3\) and \(168 = 24 \times 7\).

\(33 = 11 \times 3\), but \(11 \times 7 = 77\), not 72. So 33 – 72 is the odd one.

subtopic: Counting Triangles
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: B
figure: stem
stem:
How many triangles are there in the given figure?
A: 6
B: 8
C: 4
D: 5
solution:
Left part: the small triangle at the left holds two vertical lines, giving the whole triangle plus the small triangles at its left and right ends (3), and the triangle below its base (1). That is 4.

Right part: the slanting line from the parallelogram's lower side to the bottom-right corner, with the vertical line under it, gives the triangle left of the vertical line, the triangle right of it and the two together (3). The small triangle at the top between the parallelogram's side, the inner vertical line and the slanting line adds 1. That is 4.

Total 4 + 4 = 8.

In a certain code language,
\(A + B\) means ‘A is the son of B’,
\(A - B\) means ‘A is the sister of B’,
\(A \times B\) means ‘A is the brother of B’ and

D: Son
