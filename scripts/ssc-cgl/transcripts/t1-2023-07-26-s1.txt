C: [figure]

solution:

=== 5
chapter: Non-Verbal Series and Analogy
subtopic: Symbol Grids
difficulty: HARD
answer: D
figure: stem+options
stem:
Select the figure that will replace the question mark (?) in the following figure series.
A: [figure]
B: [figure]
C: [figure]
D: [figure]
solution:
Each figure holds five symbols (a V, a C, a T, a pin and an arrow) in the four corners and the centre. The series alternates a move step and a rotate step.

Move step (figures 1 to 2 and 3 to 4): the two top symbols swap, and the centre, bottom-right and bottom-left symbols move round (centre to bottom-right, bottom-right to bottom-left, bottom-left to centre).

Rotate step (figure 2 to 3): positions stay; the two top symbols turn 90 degrees anticlockwise (V becomes a V opening left, the C opening down becomes a C opening right), the two bottom symbols turn 90 degrees clockwise (the arrow pointing right now points down, the upright T lies on its side) and the centre symbol turns 180 degrees (the pin flips).

Figure 4 to 5 is a rotate step: the top-left V becomes an upright inverted V, the top-right C opens upward, the centre arrow turns from down to up, the bottom-left T turns upside down and the bottom-right pin turns to lie with its circle on the left. This is option 4.

chapter: Paper Folding and Cutting
subtopic: Other Folds and Transparent Sheets
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: A
figure: stem+options
stem:
A paper is folded and cut as shown below. How will it appear when unfolded?
A: [figure]
B: [figure]
C: [figure]
D: [figure]
solution:
The square is folded along the vertical diagonal (right half onto left), then the top half is folded down onto the bottom, leaving the lower-left quarter. Cuts made there: a right-pointing arrow and a parallelogram near the horizontal fold, and an upward arrow near the vertical fold.

Unfolding the horizontal fold mirrors them into the upper-left quarter: the arrow still points right and the upward arrow becomes a downward arrow at the top.

Unfolding the vertical fold mirrors everything into the right half: the right-pointing arrows become left-pointing.

So the unfolded sheet has two down arrows at the top, two up arrows at the bottom, right-pointing arrows on the left and left-pointing arrows on the right, all pointing inward. This is option 1.

=== 17
chapter: Syllogism
subtopic: Three-Statement Syllogism
difficulty: EASY
answer: B
stem:
Three statements are given followed by three conclusions numbered I, II and III. Assuming the statements to be true, even if they seem to be at variance with commonly known facts, decide which of the conclusions logically follow(s) from the statements.

Statements:
Some seeds are fruits.
Some fruits are jams.
All jams are desserts.

Conclusions:
I. Some jams are seeds.
II. Some desserts are jams.
III. Some fruits are desserts.
A: All conclusions follow
B: Only conclusions II and III follow
C: Only conclusions I and III follow
D: Only conclusions I and II follow
solution:
I: seeds and jams are linked only through fruits, by two "some" statements, so no definite relation: I does not follow.

II: all jams are desserts, so some desserts are jams: II follows.

III: some fruits are jams and all jams are desserts, so those fruits are desserts: III follows.
