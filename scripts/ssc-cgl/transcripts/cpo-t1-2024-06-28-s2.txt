=== 1
chapter: Syllogism
subtopic: Two-Statement Syllogism
difficulty: EASY
answer: B
stem:
Given below are two statements and two conclusions. Take the statements to be true even if they are at variance with commonly known facts, and decide whether the conclusion(s) follow(s) the given statements.
Statements:
All trains are buses.
Some buses are cars.
Conclusions:
I. All cars are buses.
II. Some buses are trains.
A: Neither conclusion I nor II follows
B: Only conclusion II follows
C: Both the conclusions follow
D: Only conclusion I follows
solution:
"All trains are buses" converts to "Some buses are trains", so conclusion II follows.

"Some buses are cars" says only that some cars are buses; the cars may lie partly outside the buses, so "All cars are buses" does not follow. Only conclusion II follows.

=== 18
chapter: Order, Ranking and Arrangement
subtopic: Linear and Circular Seating
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: A
stem:
J, K, L, M, O and P are sitting around a circular table facing the centre. Both L and M are immediate neighbours of J. Only one person sits between M and P. O sits second to the right of J. O is not an immediate neighbour of L. Who is sitting to the immediate right of K?
A: L
B: J
C: P
D: O
solution:
Facing the centre, a person's right is the anticlockwise direction. Number the seats anticlockwise from J at seat 1, so O, second to the right of J, is at seat 3.

L and M take seats 2 and 6, J's neighbours. Seat 2 is next to O, and O is not a neighbour of L, so L is at 6 and M at 2.

One person sits between M and P, so P is at seat 4 (seat 6 is taken). K takes the last seat, 5.

The immediate right of K (seat 5) is seat 6: L.
