The seminar in Chandigarh was held 3 days before the Delhi seminar. The seminar in Lucknow was 2 days after the Chandigarh seminar.
If the Delhi seminar was on \(10^{\text{th}}\) March, when was the Lucknow seminar?
A: \(6^{\text{th}}\) March
B: \(7^{\text{th}}\) March

chapter: Syllogism
subtopic: Three-Statement Syllogism
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: A
stem:
Read the following statement carefully and identify the conclusion that follows.

Statement:
All engineers are graduates. Some graduates are teachers. No teacher is a doctor.

**Conclusions:**
I. Some engineers may be teachers.
II. Some doctors are not engineers.
A: Only I follows
B: Only II follows
C: Both I and II follow
D: Neither I nor II follows
solution:
Engineers lie inside graduates, and only some graduates are teachers. Nothing links engineers to teachers, and nothing rules it out, so engineers and teachers may overlap: conclusion I, a possibility, follows.

Doctors are kept apart only from teachers. Every doctor could be an engineer who is not a teacher, so "Some doctors are not engineers" is not certain: II does not follow. Only I follows.

flawed: yes
note: Ambiguous. Reading house 1 as the top (V = 1), the clues fix Z at house 4 uniquely. Reading house 6 as the top (V = 6), several layouts fit and house 4 can hold W (X 5, W 4, Y 1, Z 2) or Z (X 3, W 2, Z 4, Y 5) or nobody named. Only W and Z are ever possible; Z fits both readings.

B: 3-4-2-1
C: 2-3-1-4
D: 2-4-1-3
solution:
Sentence 2 introduces the topic (industrialization brings growth). "However, it ..." (3) gives the downside, "This can lead to environmental degradation" (1) explains that cost, and 4 offers the remedy for "these negative impacts". So 2-3-1-4.
