chapter: Mensuration 3D
subtopic: Prisms and Pyramids
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: D
stem:
A pyramid has an equilateral triangle as its base, of which each side is 8 cm. Its slant edge is 24 cm. The whole surface area of the pyramid (in \(\text{cm}^2\)) is:
A: \((16\sqrt{3} + 24\sqrt{35})\)
B: \((12\sqrt{3} + 24\sqrt{35})\)
C: \((24\sqrt{3} + 36\sqrt{35})\)
D: \((16\sqrt{3} + 48\sqrt{35})\)
solution:
Base area \(= \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} \times 8^2 = 16\sqrt{3}\).

Each lateral face is a triangle with sides 24, 24 and 8. Its height on the side 8 is \(\sqrt{24^2 - 4^2} = \sqrt{560} = 4\sqrt{35}\), so its area is \(\frac{1}{2} \times 8 \times 4\sqrt{35} = 16\sqrt{35}\).

Three faces give \(48\sqrt{35}\), so the whole surface area is \(16\sqrt{3} + 48\sqrt{35}\).

Check: by Heron, \(s = 28\), area \(= \sqrt{28 \times 4 \times 4 \times 20} = \sqrt{8960} = 16\sqrt{35}\).

=== 3
chapter: Polynomials
subtopic: HCF and LCM of Polynomials
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: A
stem:
If the highest common factor (HCF) of x and y is 15, then the HCF of \(36x^2 - 81y^2\) and \(81x^2 - 9y^2\) is divisible by ________.
A: 135
B: 120
C: 180
D: 90
solution:
Write \(x = 15a\), \(y = 15b\) with a and b coprime.

\(36x^2 - 81y^2 = 225 \times 9(4a^2 - 9b^2)\) and \(81x^2 - 9y^2 = 225 \times 9(9a^2 - b^2)\), so their HCF is a multiple of \(2025 = 3^4 \times 5^2\).

\(135 = 3^3 \times 5\) divides 2025, so the HCF is divisible by 135.

Check: the HCF need not be even. With a, b coprime, \(4a^2 - 9b^2\) and \(9a^2 - b^2\) are never both even (b even makes \(9a^2 - b^2\) odd; b odd makes \(4a^2 - 9b^2\) odd), so 120, 180 and 90 do not always divide it (for example \(x = 15\), \(y = 30\): the HCF is \(2025 \times \text{HCF}(-32, 5) = 2025\), odd).

subtopic: Data Sufficiency
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: B
stem:
A question is given, followed by two statements numbered (I) and (II). You have to decide whether the data provided in the statements is sufficient to answer the question. Read both the statements and select the appropriate answer.

Question: How many people are standing in the row where all people are facing north?

Statements:
(I) A is fourth from the left end; M is second to the right of A; and R is second from the left end.
(II) M is fourth from the right end; only two people Stand between M and B.
