Seminars were held in Delhi, Mumbai, and Kolkata on \(1^{\text{st}}\), \(4^{\text{th}}\), and \(6^{\text{th}}\) July.

- The Kolkata seminar happened after the Mumbai one.
- Delhi's seminar was not on \(4^{\text{th}}\) or \(6^{\text{th}}\).

If 10 % 5 = 6, 8 % 4 = 6, then 6 % 2 = ?
A: 2
B: 9
C: 3
D: 7

Eight students A, B, C, D, E, F, G, and H like different subjects namely - English, Hindi, Physics, Computer, Maths, Sanskrit, Chemistry, and Biology but not necessarily in the same order.
E likes Sanskrit. B likes either Maths or Physics. H does not like Hindi and English. A likes Chemistry. G neither likes English nor Hindi. D does not like English. G and H do not like Computer. Neither G nor H likes Math. G does not like Biology. Neither C nor D likes Hindi.

IV. All roses are flowers
Which of the above conclusions definitely follows?
A: Both I and II
B: Only II
C: Only I
D: Only IV
solution:
Some flowers are roses and all roses are red, so those flowers are red: some flowers are red, and I follows.

Only some flowers are known to be roses, so II does not follow. III contradicts the first statement. Only some roses are known to be flowers, so IV does not follow. II and III are not a complementary pair (both can be false), so no either-or case arises. Only I follows.
