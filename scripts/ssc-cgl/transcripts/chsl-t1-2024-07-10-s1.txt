chapter: Calendar and Clock
subtopic: Calendar
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: B
stem:
If 23 June 2008 was Monday, then what was the day of the week on 23 June 2020?
A: Sunday
B: Tuesday
C: Saturday
D: Wednesday
solution:
From 23 June 2008 to 23 June 2020 is 12 years. Each year moves the day 1 forward, and each 29 February in between adds 1 more.

The 29 Februaries in between are those of 2012, 2016 and 2020 (29 February 2020 comes before 23 June 2020): 3.

Total shift \(12 + 3 = 15\) days, and \(15 = 2 \times 7 + 1\), so the day moves 1 forward: Monday becomes Tuesday.

Identify the figure given in the options that when put in place of the question mark (?) will logically complete the series.

B: [figure]
C: [figure]

solution:

flawed: yes
notice: SSC's provisional key gives 11, but the figure holds 12 quadrilaterals (each is listed in the solution); the answer shown is ours.

stem:
How many quadrilaterals are there in the given figure?
A: 10
B: 12
C: 11
D: 7
solution:
The figure is a wide rectangle cut by a vertical line near its right side and a horizontal line through its middle. Its right strip continues down below the wide rectangle to make a tall right column. In that column, below the middle line, two slanting lines run from the point P where the middle line meets the right edge to the point Q where the wide rectangle's bottom line meets the vertical line, and from Q to the bottom right corner R.

Rectangles: the left part gives 3 (upper half, lower half, both together); the right column gives 3 (top part above the middle line, the part below the middle line down to the bottom, and the whole column); the whole width above the middle line gives 1. That is 7.

Quadrilaterals using the slanting lines: the top of the right column down to PQ (1); the top of the right column down to QR (1); the column below the middle line cut off by QR (1); the trapezium P, R, bottom left corner of the column, Q (1); and the left lower half joined to the triangle up to PQ, bounded by the middle line, PQ and the bottom line (1). That is 5.

Total \(7 + 5 = 12\).
