chapter: Blood Relations
subtopic: Coded Relations
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: B
stem:
In a certain code language,
‘A + B’ means ‘A is the mother of B’;
‘A \(-\) B’ means ‘A is the brother of B’;
‘A x B’ means ‘A is the sister of B’ and
‘A \(\div\) B’ means ‘A is the daughter of B’.
Based on the above, if ‘J + K \(\div\) L \(-\) T x R’, then how is J related to L?
A: Husband’s sister
B: Wife
C: Father’s sister
D: Sister
solution:
J + K: J is the mother of K. K \(\div\) L: K is the daughter of L. L \(-\) T: L is the brother of T, so L is male.

So L is K's father and J is K's mother: J is L's wife. (T x R only adds L's siblings.)

=== 10
chapter: Syllogism
subtopic: Three-Statement Syllogism
difficulty: EASY
answer: D
stem:
Three statements are given, followed by three conclusions numbered I, II and III. Assuming the statements to be true, even if they seem to be at variance with commonly known facts, decide which of the conclusions logically follow(s) from the statements.

Statements:
Some circles are rectangles.
Some rectangles are squares.
All squares are triangles.

Conclusions:
I. No circle is a triangle.
II. Some rectangles are triangles.
III. All rectangles are triangles.
A: Both conclusion I and II follow
B: Only conclusion I follows
C: Only conclusion III follows
D: Only conclusion II follows
solution:
The rectangles that are squares are all triangles, so some rectangles are triangles: II follows.

Nothing links circles to triangles, so "No circle is a triangle" is not certain: I does not follow. Only some rectangles are known to be triangles, so III does not follow.

stem:
How many triangles are there in the given figure?
A: 16
B: 12
C: 10
D: 14
solution:

Small and quarter triangles: the upper-left and lower-right quarters give 1 each; the upper-right quarter is split in two, giving 2 small + 1 whole = 3; the lower-left quarter likewise gives 3. That is 8.

=== 32
subject: Chemistry
chapter: Carbon and Its Compounds
subtopic: Functional Groups and Isomerism
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: D
stem:
Which of the following is the most acidic?
A: \(\text{C}_3\text{H}_7\text{COOH}\)
B: \(\text{CH}_3\text{COOH}\)
C: \(\text{C}_2\text{H}_5\text{COOH}\)
D: HCOOH
solution:
Alkyl groups push electrons (+I effect) towards the carboxyl group, which destabilises the carboxylate ion and lowers acidity. Formic acid (HCOOH) has no alkyl group, so it is the strongest of these acids (pKa about 3.75, against about 4.76 for acetic acid). Acidity falls as the alkyl chain grows: HCOOH > \(\text{CH}_3\text{COOH}\) > \(\text{C}_2\text{H}_5\text{COOH}\) > \(\text{C}_3\text{H}_7\text{COOH}\).
