=== 1
chapter: Coding-Decoding
subtopic: Word-Code Sentences
difficulty: EASY
answer: A
stem:
In a certain code,
'it is raining' is coded as 'dy po ko',
'is raining now' is coded as 'po jo ko' and
'is now it' is coded as 'po jo dy'.
(All the codes are two-letter codes only.)
What is the code for 'raining'?
A: ko
B: dy
C: jo
D: po
solution:
'is' appears in all three sentences and 'po' in all three codes, so is = po.

'raining' appears in the first two sentences; their common codes are 'po' and 'ko'. Since po = is, raining = ko.

Check: 'now' is common to the second and third sentences, whose common codes are po and jo, so now = jo, and 'it' = dy from the first and third.

Select the number from among the given options that can replace the question mark (?) in the following series.
625, 382, 301, 274, 265, ?
A: 258
B: 262
C: 248
D: 260
solution:

subtopic: Figure Series

stem:
Select the figure from among the given option that can replace the question mark (?) in the following series.
A: [figure]
B: [figure]
C: [figure]
D: [figure]
solution:
Each figure is a 3 by 3 box holding seven symbols (6, 2, =, 1, P, C, M): one at the top right, three in the middle row and three in the bottom row.

Figure 1: top right 6; middle 2, =, 1; bottom P, C, M.
Figure 2: top right C; middle M, 6, P; bottom 1, =, 2.
Figure 3: top right 1; middle P, M, 6; bottom =, 2, C.
Figure 4: top right 2; middle C, 1, =; bottom 6, M, P.

In that move the top-right symbol goes to bottom right, middle-left to centre, centre to middle-right, middle-right to middle-left, bottom-left to top right, bottom-middle to bottom-left and bottom-right to bottom-middle.

Applied to figure 4: top right 6; middle =, C, 1; bottom M, P, 2. Option 4 shows exactly this.

chapter: Syllogism
subtopic: Three-Statement Syllogism
difficulty: EASY
answer: A
stem:
In this question, three statements are given, followed by two conclusions numbered I and II. Assuming the statements to be true, even if they seem to be at variance with commonly known facts, decide which of the conclusion(s) logically follows/follow from the statements.
Statements:
All teachers are dancers.
All dancers are engineers.
Some engineers are beautiful.
Conclusions:
I. All teachers are beautiful.
II. Some dancers are beautiful.
A: Neither conclusion I nor II follows.
B: Only conclusion II follows.
C: Both conclusions I and II follow.
D: Only conclusion I follows.
solution:
Teachers lie inside dancers, which lie inside engineers. The beautiful ones overlap engineers only somewhere, and that overlap may lie wholly outside the dancers circle.

So nothing links beautiful to dancers or to teachers: neither conclusion follows.

13 is related to 119 following a certain logic. Following the same logic, 9 is related to 31. To which of the following is 8 related, following the same logic?

In a certain code language, 'MAIN' is written as 'OHBL' and 'MAIL' is written as 'MHBL'. How will 'MANY’ be written in that language?
A: ZBLM
B: ZMLB
C: ZMBL
D: ZLMB
solution:
The word is reversed and then the letters move +1, -1, +1, -1. MAIN reversed is NIAM: N+1 = O, I-1 = H, A+1 = B, M-1 = L, giving OHBL. MAIL reversed is LIAM, giving MHBL.

In a certain code language,
'A ! B' means ‘A is the father of B’,
'A & B' means ‘A is the brother of B’,
'A \(*\) B' means ‘A is the wife of B’ and
