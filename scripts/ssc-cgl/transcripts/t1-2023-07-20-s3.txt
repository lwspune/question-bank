=== 1
chapter: Analogy
subtopic: Number Analogy
difficulty: EASY
answer: B
stem:
Select the option that is related to the fifth term in the same way as the second term is related to the first term and the fourth term is related to the third term.
32 : 8 :: 72 : 12 :: 128 : ?
A: 18
B: 16
C: 14
D: 15
solution:
The second term is the square root of twice the first: \(\sqrt{2 \times 32} = \sqrt{64} = 8\) and \(\sqrt{2 \times 72} = \sqrt{144} = 12\).

So \(\sqrt{2 \times 128} = \sqrt{256} = 16\).

Check: \(16^2 = 256 = 2 \times 128\).

chapter: Syllogism
subtopic: Three-Statement Syllogism
difficulty: EASY
answer: C
stem:
In this question, three statements are given, followed by two conclusions numbered I and II. Assuming the statements to be true, even if they seem to be at variance with commonly known facts, decide which of the conclusion(s) logically follows/follow from the statements.

Statements:
All cricketers are wealthy.
Some wealthy are Indians.
All Indians are honest.

Conclusions:
I. All Indians are cricketers.
II. Some cricketers are honest.
A: Only conclusion II follows.
B: Only conclusion I follows.
C: Neither conclusions I nor II follows.
D: Both conclusions I and II follow.
solution:
Cricketers sit inside Wealthy, and Indians overlap Wealthy only in part, so nothing links Cricketers to Indians.

I: Indians need not be cricketers at all, so I does not follow.

II: Honest contains Indians, but the cricketers may lie in the part of Wealthy outside Indians, so no cricketer need be honest. II does not follow.

chapter: Blood Relations
subtopic: Coded Relations
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: B
stem:
If,
‘B % D’ means ‘B is the brother of D’,
‘B & D’ means ‘B is the mother of D’,
‘B \(\times\) D’ means ‘B is the husband of D’,
‘B # D’ means ‘B is the sister of D’,
‘B \(\text{\textdollar}\) D’ means ‘B is the son of D’,
‘B @ D’ means ‘B is the father of D’,
then which of the given options is correct regarding the following expression?
S & Z \(\text{\textdollar}\) F % B % E @ G
A: E is the sister of F.
B: Z is the son of E’s brother
C: S is the mother of G.
D: B is the maternal uncle of Z.
solution:
S is Z's mother and Z is F's son, so F is Z's father. F is B's brother and B is E's brother, so F, B and E are siblings. E is G's father, so E is male.

Z is the son of F, and F is E's brother: option 2 is correct.

E is male, so not F's sister; G is E's child, not S's; B is Z's father's brother, a paternal uncle, not a maternal one.
