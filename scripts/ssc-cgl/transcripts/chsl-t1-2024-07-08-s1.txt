subtopic: Three-Statement Syllogism
difficulty: EASY
answer: C
stem:
In this question, three statements are given, followed by three conclusions numbered I, II and III. Assuming the statements to be true, even if they seem to be at variance with commonly known facts, decide which of the conclusion(s) logically follows/follow from the statements.
Statements:
No copper is silver.
All gold is copper.
All aluminium is gold.
Conclusions:
I. No aluminium is silver.
II. Some gold is silvers
III. All aluminium is copper.
A: Only conclusion II follows.
B: Only conclusion I follows.
C: Both conclusions I and III follow.
D: Both conclusions I and II follow.
solution:
All aluminium is gold and all gold is copper, so all aluminium is copper: III follows.

Both I and III follow.

=== 35
chapter: Blood Relations
subtopic: Coded Statements and Expressions
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: B
stem:
In a certain code language,
‘A + B’ means ‘A is the mother of B’,
‘A – B’ means ‘A is the daughter of B’,
‘A \(\times\) B’ means ‘A is the husband of B’,
‘A \(\div\) B’ means ‘A is the sister of B’.
Based on the above, how is M related to Q if ‘M \(\div\) N – O \(\times\) P + Q’?
A: Daughter
B: Sister
C: Father’s sister
D: Mother
solution:
O \(\times\) P: O is P's husband. P + Q: P is Q's mother, so Q is a child of O and P.

N – O: N is O's daughter, so N is also a child of O and P, a sibling of Q.

M \(\div\) N: M is N's sister, so M is a daughter of O and P as well. Hence M is Q's sister.

=== 36
chapter: Calendar and Clock
subtopic: Calendar
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: B
stem:
If 19 November 2003 is Wednesday, then what will be the day of the week on 25 March 2011?
A: Thursday
B: Friday
C: Tuesday
D: Sunday
solution:
From 19 November of one year to 19 November of the next the day moves 1 forward, or 2 when a 29 February falls in between.

2003 to 2004 (29 Feb 2004): 2. 2004 to 2005: 1. 2005 to 2006: 1. 2006 to 2007: 1. 2007 to 2008 (29 Feb 2008): 2. 2008 to 2009: 1. 2009 to 2010: 1. Total 9, that is 2 odd days, so 19 November 2010 was a Friday.

From 19 November 2010 to 25 March 2011: 11 (rest of November) + 31 + 31 + 28 + 25 = 126 days, which is exactly 18 weeks. So 25 March 2011 was also a Friday.

stem:
Which of the following causes enlargement and extension growth of cells?
A: Pressure potential
B: Osmotic pressure
C: Imbibition
D: Turgor pressure
solution:
When water enters a cell by osmosis, the protoplast presses against the cell wall; this turgor pressure stretches the wall and is responsible for the enlargement and extension growth of cells.

Osmotic pressure drives water entry but does not itself stretch the wall, and imbibition is the swelling of dry hydrophilic materials such as seeds. (Pressure potential is the water-potential term for the same wall pressure, but the standard answer names it turgor pressure.)
