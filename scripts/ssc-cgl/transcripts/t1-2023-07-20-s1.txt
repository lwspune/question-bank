chapter: Classification
subtopic: Number Classification
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: A
stem:
The second number in the given number-pairs is obtained by performing certain mathematical operation(s) on the first number. The same operation(s) is/are followed in all the number-pairs except one. Find that odd number-pair.
A: 80 : 9
B: 142 : 12
C: 23 : 5
D: 7 : 3
solution:
Rule: second number = \(\sqrt{\text{first} + 2}\).

\(142 + 2 = 144 = 12^2\), \(23 + 2 = 25 = 5^2\), \(7 + 2 = 9 = 3^2\).

\(80 + 2 = 82\), not \(9^2 = 81\). So 80 : 9 is the odd pair.

=== 13
chapter: Blood Relations
subtopic: Coded Relations
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: D
stem:
‘A + B’ means ‘A is the son of B’.
‘A – B’ means ‘A is the wife of B’.
‘A \(\times\) B’ means ‘A is the father of B’.
‘A \(\div\) B’ means ‘A is the sister of B’.
If A + P – Q \(\times\) R – S, then which of the following statements is NOT correct?
A: A is the brother of wife of S.
B: P is the mother of A.
C: Q is the father of A.
D: A is the son of S.
solution:
A is the son of P; P is the wife of Q; Q is the father of R; R is the wife of S.

So P and Q are A's parents (B and C are correct). R is Q's daughter, A's sister, and S's wife, so A is the brother of S's wife (A is correct).

S is A's brother-in-law, not A's parent. "A is the son of S" is NOT correct.

subtopic: Hydrocarbons and Fuels
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: D
stem:
Which of the following has the highest boiling point?
A: Alkenes
B: Alkanes
C: Free carbon dioxide
D: Alkynes
solution:
For the same number of carbon atoms, alkynes have higher boiling points than alkanes and alkenes: the linear triple bond lets the molecules pack closer, giving stronger intermolecular forces (ethyne boils at about \(-84^\circ\text{C}\), ethane at about \(-89^\circ\text{C}\), ethene at about \(-104^\circ\text{C}\)). Carbon dioxide sublimes at \(-78^\circ\text{C}\) and is not a hydrocarbon series.

Together they do \(6 + 3 + 2 = 11\) units a day, so they take \(\frac{726}{11} = 66\) days.
