=== 1
chapter: Syllogism
subtopic: Two-Statement Syllogism
difficulty: EASY
answer: B
stem:
Two statements are given followed by two conclusions numbered I and II. Assuming the statements to be true, even if they seem to be at variance with commonly known facts, decide which of the conclusions logically follow(s) from the statements.
Statements:
1) All games are tournaments.
2) Some tournaments are matches.
Conclusions:
I. All games are matches.
II. No game is a tournament.
A: Only Conclusion I follows.
B: Neither Conclusion I nor II follows.
C: Both Conclusions I and II follow.
D: Only Conclusion II follows.
solution:
All games lie inside tournaments, and only some tournaments are matches. The matches may or may not include the games, so "All games are matches" is not certain: I does not follow.

Since all games are tournaments, "No game is a tournament" is false: II does not follow. Neither conclusion follows.

=== 17
chapter: Order, Ranking and Arrangement
subtopic: Linear and Circular Seating
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: B
stem:
Six friends - A,B, C, D, E and F - are sitting around a circular table, facing towards the centre of the table.
D and C are immediate neighbours of A. B is second to the right of A. F is an immediate neighbour of E , and fourth to the left of D. What is the position of E?
A: Third to the left of A
B: Immediate left of C
C: Second to the right of A
D: Immediate neighbour of D and F
solution:
Facing the centre, a person's left is the clockwise direction. Number the seats clockwise 0 to 5 with A at 0. B is second to the right of A, so B is at 4. D and C take seats 1 and 5.

F is fourth to the left of D. If D were at 1, F would be at 5, which is taken, so D is at 5 and F is at 5 + 4 = 9, that is seat 3. Then C is at 1, and E, a neighbour of F, takes the last seat, 2.

C is at 1 and the seat to its left (clockwise) is 2, which is E. So E is immediately to the left of C. Check the others: third to the left of A is seat 3 (F), second to the right of A is seat 4 (B), and the seat between D (5) and F (3) is 4 (B).
