Select the option that is related to the fifth letter-cluster in the same way as the second letter-cluster is related to the first letter-cluster and the fourth letter-cluster is related to the third letter-cluster.
XPF : DLV :: VAN : FAN :: ERY : ?
A: VIC
B: WJC
C: VJC
D: WIC
solution:
Take the opposite letter of each letter (A-Z, B-Y and so on) and move it one place forward.

XPF: opposites C, K, U, then +1 gives D, L, V (DLV).

ERY: opposites V, I, B, then +1 gives W, J, C. The answer is WJC.

stem:
Six letters M, N, O, P, Q and R are written on different faces of a dice. Two positions of this dice are shown in the figure. Find the letter on the face opposite to a vowel.
A: M
B: N
C: P
D: R
solution:
The first view shows Q on top with M and R on the side faces; the second shows N on top with O and Q on the side faces.

Q touches M, R, N and O, so Q is opposite P. The only vowel is O.

Reading each corner clockwise from Q: first view Q, R, M; second view Q, O, N. So around Q the faces run R, M, O, N in order, which puts R opposite O and M opposite N.

The letter opposite the vowel O is R.
