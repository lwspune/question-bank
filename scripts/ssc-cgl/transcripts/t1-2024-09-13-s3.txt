=== 1
chapter: Blood Relations
subtopic: Coded Statements and Expressions
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: A
stem:
In a certain code language,
'A ? B' means 'A is the mother of B',
'A ! B' means 'A is the wife of B',
'A & B' means 'A is the brother of B' and
'A x B' means 'A is the father of B'.
Which of the following means that Z is the wife's father's mother of L?
A: Z ? B x T & J ! L
B: Z ? B ! T & J x L
C: Z ! B x T ? J & L
D: Z & B ! T x J ? L
solution:
Read option A: J ! L means J is L's wife. T & J means T is J's brother, and B x T means B is T's father, so B is J's father. Z ? B means Z is B's mother.

So Z is the mother of the father of L's wife: the wife's father's mother.

stem:
Six letters G, H, I, J, K and L are written on different faces of a dice. Two positions of this dice are shown in the figure below. Find the letter on the face opposite to G.
A: J
B: K
C: I
D: L
solution:
The first position shows L on top, K in front and J on the right; the second shows G on top, K in front and I on the right. K is common and in the same place, so the four faces around K, taken clockwise from the top, run L, J, ... and G, I, ... in the two views.

L, J, G, I fits both views (G is followed by I), so the faces around K in order are L, J, G, I. Faces two apart in this ring are opposite: L is opposite G (and J opposite I, K opposite H).
