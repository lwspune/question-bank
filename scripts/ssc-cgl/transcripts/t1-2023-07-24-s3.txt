=== 1
chapter: Classification
subtopic: Number Classification
difficulty: EASY
answer: C
stem:
Three of the following four triads are alike in a certain way as they are formed by performing same mathematical operations among themselves and thus form a group. Which triad does NOT belong to that group?
A: 11 : 21 : 121
B: 2 : 3 : 4
C: 7 : 15 : 49
D: 9 : 17 : 81
solution:
In each triad (a : b : c), \(b = 2a - 1\) and \(c = a^2\).

11 : 21 : 121 gives \(2 \times 11 - 1 = 21\) and \(11^2 = 121\). 2 : 3 : 4 gives \(2 \times 2 - 1 = 3\) and \(2^2 = 4\). 9 : 17 : 81 gives \(2 \times 9 - 1 = 17\) and \(9^2 = 81\).

7 : 15 : 49 has \(2 \times 7 + 1 = 15\), not \(2 \times 7 - 1 = 13\), so it is the odd one.

chapter: Calendar and Clock
subtopic: Calendar
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: D
stem:
The fifteenth day of the month of June is a Saturday. What will be the last day of the next month which has 31 days?
A: Tuesday
B: Monday
C: Friday
D: Wednesday
solution:
The next month after June with 31 days is July.

From 15 June to 31 July: 15 days left in June plus 31 days of July = 46 days.

\(46 = 6 \times 7 + 4\), so the day moves 4 places forward from Saturday: Sunday, Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday.

Check: 29 June is a Saturday, so 6 July, 13, 20 and 27 July are Saturdays, and 31 July is a Wednesday.

difficulty: MODERATE

stem:
Select the correct combination of mathematical signs to sequentially replace the @ signs and to balance the given equation.
72 @ 9 @ 3 @ 6 @ 6 @ 6 @ 6
A: \(\div, \times, +, =, -, +\)

stem:
Select the figure that will replace the question mark (?) in the following figure series.
A: [figure]
B: [figure]
C: [figure]

solution:

chapter: Syllogism
subtopic: Three-Statement Syllogism
difficulty: EASY
answer: C
stem:
Three statements are given followed by three conclusions numbered I, II and III. Assuming the statements to be true, even if they seem to be at variance with commonly known facts, decide which of the conclusions logically follow(s) from the statements.

Statements:
Some boats are ships.
All ships are yachts.
All yachts are barges.

Conclusions:
I. Some barges are ships.
II. Some barges are boats.
III. Some yachts are boats.
A: Only conclusions II and III follow
B: Only conclusions I and III follow
C: All conclusions follow
D: Only conclusions I and II follow
solution:
All ships are yachts and all yachts are barges, so all ships are barges: some barges are ships (I follows).

The boats that are ships are also yachts and barges, so some yachts are boats (III follows) and some barges are boats (II follows).

All three follow.

subtopic: Letter Coding
difficulty: MODERATE

stem:
In a certain code language, 'LIFETIME' is written as 'FGJMFNJU' and 'INTEGRAL' is written as 'FUOJMBSH'. How will 'INTEREST' be written in that language?
A: FUOKUTGS
B: FUOJUTFS
C: FUOJVUFS
D: ETNITSER
solution:
The word is split into two halves of four letters; each half is reversed and every letter moves one place forward.

LIFE becomes EFIL, then FGJM; TIME becomes EMIT, then FNJU. INTE becomes ETNI, then FUOJ; GRAL becomes LARG, then MBSH.

INTEREST: INTE becomes ETNI, then FUOJ; REST becomes TSER, then UTFS. The code is FUOJUTFS.
