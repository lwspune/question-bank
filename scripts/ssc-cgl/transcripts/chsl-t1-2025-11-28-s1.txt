In the following question, select the number which can be placed at the sign of question mark (?) from the given alternatives.

| 4 | 7 | 2 | 112 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | 3 | 8 | 240 |
| 6 | 4 | ? | 96 |
A: 2
B: 4

=== 75
chapter: Time and Work
subtopic: Workers Joining and Leaving
difficulty: HARD
answer: B
stem:
P, Q and R working alone can complete a work in 24, 30 and 40 days respectively. All three of them started the work together but after 8 days P left the work. Q left the work 6 days before the work was completed. R completed the remaining work alone. In how many days was the total work completed?
A: \(\frac{97}{7}\)
B: \(\frac{104}{7}\)
C: \(\frac{109}{7}\)
D: \(\frac{152}{7}\)
solution:
Take the work as 120 units: P does 5, Q does 4 and R does 3 units a day.

First 8 days: \(8 \times 12 = 96\) units, leaving 24.

Let the work take \(T\) days. R alone works the last 6 days (18 units), and Q and R together work \(T - 14\) days in between: \(7(T - 14) + 18 = 24\), so \(T - 14 = \frac{6}{7}\) and \(T = \frac{104}{7}\) days.

(Check: P works 8 days \(= 40\); Q works \(\frac{104}{7} - 6 = \frac{62}{7}\) days \(= \frac{248}{7}\); R works \(\frac{104}{7}\) days \(= \frac{312}{7}\); total \(40 + \frac{560}{7} = 120\).)
