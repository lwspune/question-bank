=== 1
chapter: Mathematical Operations
subtopic: Interchanging Two Signs
difficulty: EASY
answer: C
stem:
Which two signs should be interchanged to make the given equation correct?
\(10 - 4 + 13 \div 15 \times 3 = 71\)
A: + and \(\times\)
B: \(-\) and \(\div\)
C: \(\div\) and \(\times\)
D: \(-\) and \(\times\)
solution:
Interchanging \(\div\) and \(\times\) gives \(10 - 4 + 13 \times 15 \div 3 = 10 - 4 + 65 = 71\), which is correct.

Check the others: + and \(\times\) gives \(10 - 4 \times 13 \div 15 + 3\), not a whole number; \(-\) and \(\div\) gives \(10 \div 4 + 13 - 15 \times 3 < 0\); \(-\) and \(\times\) gives \(10 \times 4 + 13 \div 15 - 3\), not 71.

=== 10
chapter: Syllogism
subtopic: Three-Statement Syllogism
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: A
stem:
Three statements are given followed by three conclusions numbered I, II and III. Assuming the statements to be true, even if they seem to be at variance with commonly known facts, decide which of the conclusions logically follow(s) from the statements.

Statements:
All metals are gold.
All jewellery are gold.
All gold are ornaments.

Conclusions:
I. Some ornaments are jewellery.
II. Some metals are jewellery.
III. All jewellery are ornaments.
A: Only conclusions I and III follow
B: All conclusions follow
C: Only conclusions II and III follow
D: Only conclusions I and II follow
solution:
All jewellery are gold and all gold are ornaments, so all jewellery are ornaments: III follows, and so does its converse in part, some ornaments are jewellery: I follows.

Metals and jewellery are both inside gold but need not overlap, so II does not follow. Only I and III follow.

=== 86
chapter: Sentence Rearrangement
subtopic: Ordering Sentences in a Paragraph
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: A
stem:
Sentences of a paragraph are given below. While the first and the last sentences (S1 and S6) are in the correct order, the sentences in between are jumbled up and named P, Q, R, S. Arrange the sentences in the correct order to form a meaningful and coherent paragraph.
(S1) Today, the world has become a much smaller place, thanks to the adventures and miracles of science.
(P) We are slowly realising that the world is a single cooperative group.
(Q) Other religions have become forces with which we have to reckon, and we are seeking for ways and means by which we can live together in peace and harmony.
(R) Mingling of population is bringing about interchange of thought.
(S) Foreign nations have become our next-door neighbours.
(S6) We cannot have religious unity and peace so long as we assert that we are in possession of the light and all others are groping in the darkness.
A: S, R, P, Q
B: P, Q, R, S
C: S, R, Q, P
D: Q, R, S, P
solution:
S follows S1 directly: the world is smaller, so foreign nations are now neighbours. R follows: this mingling of peoples brings interchange of thought. P draws the conclusion that the world is one cooperative group. Q narrows to religions seeking to live in peace, which leads into S6 on religious unity. Order: S, R, P, Q.
