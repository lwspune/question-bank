chapter: Syllogism
subtopic: Three-Statement Syllogism
difficulty: EASY
answer: C
stem:
Read the given statements and conclusions carefully. You have to take the given statements to be true even if they seem to be at variance from commonly known facts. You have to decide which conclusion(s) logically follow(s) from the given statements.
Statements: All Grapes are Berries.
Some Berries are Juices.
All Juices are Sodas.
Conclusions: (I) Some Berries are Grapes.
(II) Some Sodas are Berries.
A: Only conclusion (I) follows
B: Only conclusion (II) follows
C: Both the conclusions (I) and (II) follow
D: Both the conclusions (I) and (II) do not follow
solution:
All Grapes are Berries, so some Berries are Grapes: I follows.

Some Berries are Juices and all Juices are Sodas, so those Berries are Sodas: some Berries are Sodas, which is the same as some Sodas are Berries. II follows.

difficulty: MODERATE
answer: B

stem:
How many triangles are there in the figure given below?
A: 8
B: 10
C: 12
D: 14
solution:
The upper part is a right triangle with three horizontal lines across it. Each horizontal line, together with the base, closes a triangle with the top vertex: 4 triangles.

The lower part is a square cut by a diagonal. The half above the diagonal is 1 triangle.

The half below the diagonal is 1 triangle; inside it, the short horizontal line near the top corner makes 1 small triangle, the slanting line to the bottom-right corner makes 2 (one on each side of it), and the vertical line near the bottom-left corner makes 1. That is 5.

Total \(4 + 1 + 5 = 10\).

In a certain code language,

‘A + B’ means ‘A is the mother of B’;
‘A – B’ means ‘A is the brother of B’;

Founding father / is / of / New Historicism / Greenbalt / considered / the.

The team ________________ the championship before their star player got injured.

D: DCBA
