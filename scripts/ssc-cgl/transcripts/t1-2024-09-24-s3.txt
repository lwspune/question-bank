subtopic: Number Analogy
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: B
stem:
7 is related to 40 by certain logic. Following the same logic, 5 is related to 16. To which of the following is 9 related, following the same logic? (NOTE : Operations should be performed on the whole numbers, without breaking down the numbers into its constituent digits. E.g. 13 – Operations on 13 such as adding /deleting /multiplying etc., to 13 can be performed. Breaking down 13 into 1 and 3 and then performing mathematical operations on 1 and 3 is not allowed.)
A: 48
B: 72
C: 54
D: 70
solution:

Select the combination of letters that when sequentially placed in the blanks of the given series will complete the series.
B_DE_GDGBK_IB_DK
A: CBDP
B: OCBD
C: CODB
D: CBDO
solution:
Read the series in groups of four: B _ D E | _ G D G | B K _ I | B _ D K. Position by position across the groups: the first letters are B, B, B, B; the second letters move 4 places forward, C, G, K, O; the third letters are D, D, D, D; the fourth letters move 2 places forward, E, G, I, K.

chapter: Dice and Cubes
subtopic: Opposite Faces of a Dice
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: C
figure: stem
stem:
Two different positions of the same dice are given below. Which is the number on the face opposite the face containing 5?
A: 3
B: 2
C: 1
D: 6
solution:
The first position shows 2 on top with 1 and 3 on the visible sides; the second shows 2 on top with 5 and 4 on the visible sides.

With 2 on top in both, the four side faces go round 2 in one order. The first view gives 1 followed by 3, the second gives 5 followed by 4, so the order round the dice is 1, 3, 5, 4. Faces two steps apart in this ring are opposite: 1 is opposite 5 and 3 is opposite 4 (and 2 is opposite 6).

So 1 is opposite 5.

chapter: Syllogism
subtopic: Three-Statement Syllogism
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: B
stem:
In this question, three statements are given, followed by two conclusions numbered I and II. Assuming the statements to be true, even if they seem to be at variance with commonly known facts, decide which of the conclusion(s) logically follows/follow from the statements.
Statements:
All grains are cereals.
Some cereals are fruits.
No fruit is a vegetable.
Conclusions:
I. All cereals can be vegetables.
II. All grains are fruits.
A: Only Conclusion I follows.
B: Neither Conclusion I nor II follow.
C: Only Conclusion II follows.
D: Both Conclusions I and II follow.
solution:
Some cereals are fruits, and no fruit is a vegetable, so those cereals can never be vegetables. Conclusion I does not follow.

Grains are inside cereals, but nothing ties grains to fruits; all grains being fruits is only a possibility. Conclusion II does not follow.
