chapter: Spotting Errors
subtopic: No Error (Correct Sentence)
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: B
stem:
The following sentence has been divided into parts. One of them may contain an error. Select the part that contains the error from the given options. If you don’t find any error, mark ‘No error’ as your answer.
Mrs. Meera and family / received his letter / a week ago.
A: a week ago
B: No error
C: Mrs. Meera and family
D: received his letter
solution:
The verb "received" is in the simple past, which agrees with the past time marker "a week ago". "His letter" is a letter written by some man (for example a relative), so the possessive does not have to refer to Mrs. Meera, and "Mrs. Meera and family" is an accepted way of naming a household. No part needs changing, so the answer is "No error".

subtopic: Opposite Faces of a Dice
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: B
figure: stem
stem:
Six numbers 1, 2, 5, 6, 8 and 9 are written on different faces of a dice. Two positions of this dice are shown in the figure. Which is the number on the face opposite to the face containing 8?
A: 1
B: 2
C: 9
D: 5
solution:
The first position shows 6 on top, 1 at the front left and 8 at the front right. The second shows 5 on top, 1 at the front left and 2 at the front right.

1 touches 6, 8, 5 and 2, so 9 is opposite 1, and 6, 8, 5, 2 run round 1 as a ring.

With 1 in the same place in both views, going round the ring from the top to the right gives 6 then 8 in the first view and 5 then 2 in the second. So the ring in order is 6, 8, 5, 2, and faces two apart in the ring are opposite: 6 opposite 5 and 8 opposite 2.

So 2 is opposite 8.

subtopic: Counting Triangles
difficulty: HARD
answer: C

stem:
How many triangles are there in the given figure?
A: 11
B: 12
C: 13
D: 14
solution:
Small square at the top left, with a diamond inside it whose corners are the midpoints of its sides; the big rectangle's top-left corner sits at the centre of this square, so its top and left edges run from the centre to the right and bottom midpoints. Triangles: the four corner triangles outside the diamond (4), and the triangle cut from the diamond by the two edges meeting at the centre (1): 5.

V at the top: from the right corner of the small square a slanting line runs down to the inner vertical line, and another runs up from there to the top edge. With the vertical line between them this gives 2 small triangles and the whole V: 3.

The cell with a diagonal in the middle row: 2 triangles.

The bottom-left corner cut by a slanting line: 1.

Total \(5 + 3 + 2 + 2 + 1 = 13\).

Select the correct option that when filled in the blanks in the same sequence will make the series logically complete.
TRIC_TRI_KT_IWK_R_TK
A: KATIR
B: KBTIR
C: KIRTA
D: KZRTI
solution:
The series splits into four groups of five: TRIC_, TRI_K, T_IWK, _R_TK. Each group reads TRI, a changing letter, then K.

B: Bhabar
C: Bhangar
D: Khadar
solution:
Bhangar is the older alluvium lying above the flood plains, and it forms the largest part of the northern plains; its soil holds kankar (calcareous) nodules.

Khadar is the newer alluvium of the flood plains, Bhabar is the narrow pebble belt at the foot of the Shiwaliks, and Terai is the wet, marshy belt south of the Bhabar.
