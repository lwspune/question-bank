subtopic: Alphanumeric and Pattern Series
difficulty: EASY
answer: B
stem:
Select the option that can replace the question mark (?) in the following series.
AH21, CJ18, EL15, GN12, ?
A: HR11
B: IP9
C: J8
D: HQ10
solution:
First letters A, C, E, G move 2 places forward, so next is I. Second letters H, J, L, N move 2 places forward, so next is P. The numbers 21, 18, 15, 12 fall by 3, so next is 9. The term is IP9.

chapter: Mathematical Operations
subtopic: Interchanging Signs and Numbers
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: B
stem:
Which of the following interchanges of numbers (not digits) would make the given equation correct?
\(\frac{480 \div 0.3 \times 5 + 500}{190 + 9 \times 27 \div 1.6} = 2\)
A: 5 and 9
B: 0.3 and 1.6
C: 27 and 480
D: 500 and 190
solution:
Interchange 0.3 and 1.6: numerator \(480 \div 1.6 \times 5 + 500 = 300 \times 5 + 500 = 2000\).

Denominator \(190 + 9 \times 27 \div 0.3 = 190 + 243 \div 0.3 = 190 + 810 = 1000\).

\(2000 \div 1000 = 2\), so the equation holds.

Check option A: \(1600 \times 9 + 500 = 14900\) over \(190 + 5 \times 27 \div 1.6 = 274.375\) is far from 2.

30, 20, ?, 60, 140
A: 30
B: 40
C: 35
D: 25
solution:
The rule is multiply by 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2 and add 5, 10, 15, 20 in turn:

chapter: Non-Verbal Series and Analogy
subtopic: Figure Series
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: C

stem:
Which figure should replace the question mark (?) if the following series were to be continued?
A: [figure]
B: [figure]
C: [figure]
D: [figure]
solution:
Each figure holds six letters: one at the top left, four in a middle row and one at the bottom right. The figures are A / B L U E / M, then L / A B E M / U, then U / M A B T / L, then A / U M T L / B.

Applying the even step to A / U M T L / B gives top B, middle L U M and a new letter, bottom A: B / L U M H / A, which is option 3.

=== 11
chapter: Blood Relations
subtopic: Coded Relations
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: B
stem:
In a certain code language,
\(X + Y\) means ‘X is the father of Y’,
\(X - Y\) means ‘X is the brother of Y’,
\(X \times Y\) means ‘X is the sister of Y’,
\(X \div Y\) means ‘X is the wife of Y’.
Based on the above, how is D related to H if ‘\(D \times E \div F + G - H\)’?
A: Father’s sister
B: Mother’s sister
C: Sister
D: Mother’s mother
solution:
\(D \times E\): D is the sister of E. \(E \div F\): E is the wife of F. \(F + G\): F is the father of G. \(G - H\): G is the brother of H.

So F is H's father and E, F's wife, is H's mother. D is E's sister, so D is H's mother's sister.

stem:
Which of the following terms will replace the question mark (?) in the given series?
KES, MIQ, PMN, TQJ, YUE, ?
A: EYY
B: EXY
C: EXZ
D: FYX
solution:
First letters K, M, P, T, Y move \(+2, +3, +4, +5\), so next is \(+6\): Y(25) + 6 = 31, which wraps to E(5).
