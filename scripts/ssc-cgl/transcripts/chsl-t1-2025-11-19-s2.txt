chapter: Grammar
subtopic: Active and Passive Voice
difficulty: EASY
answer: A
flawed: yes
notice: Two options are correct passive forms (either object can become the subject); the answer shown is SSC's key, the more usual form.
note: Two options are correct passives of a sentence with two objects: A (indirect object "us" as subject) and D (direct object "stories" as subject). A is the usual form and is given as the best answer.
stem:
**Choose the correct passive voice transformation of the sentence:**
My grandmother tells us stories.
A: We are told stories by my grandmother.
B: We were told stories by my grandmother.
C: We have been told stories by my grandmother.
D: Stories are told to us by my grandmother.
solution:
The verb "tells" is simple present, so the passive is "is/are + told". "Tells" has two objects, "us" and "stories", and either can become the subject: "We are told stories by my grandmother" or "Stories are told to us by my grandmother". B changes the tense to the past and C to the present perfect. Taking the indirect object (the person) as the subject is the more common form, so A is given.

subtopic: Reversal and Opposite Letters
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: A
stem:
If in a specific coding system, DOG is coded as 231220, CAT is coded as 24267, and BIRD is coded as 2518923, then following this numerical coding pattern, what would FISH be coded as?
A: 2118819
B: 2018819
C: 1918819
D: 2118821
solution:
Each letter is replaced by its position in the reversed alphabet (Z = 1, ..., A = 26), that is \(27 -\) its position.

DOG: \(27 - 4 = 23\), \(27 - 15 = 12\), \(27 - 7 = 20\), giving 231220. CAT: 24, 26, 7, giving 24267.

FISH: \(27 - 6 = 21\), \(27 - 9 = 18\), \(27 - 19 = 8\), \(27 - 8 = 19\), giving 2118819.

A: It promotes consumers spend more
B: It promotes consumers spend less

solution:
In the traditional view of government debt, a tax cut financed by borrowing raises households' disposable income, so consumers spend more; this raises demand in the short run and lowers national saving.
