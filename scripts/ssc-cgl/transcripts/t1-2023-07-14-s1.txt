chapter: Syllogism
subtopic: Three-Statement Syllogism
difficulty: EASY
answer: A
stem:
Three statements are given, followed by four conclusions numbered I, II, III and IV. Assuming the statements to be true, even if they seem to be at variance with commonly known facts, decide which of the conclusions logically follow(s) from the statements.

Statements:
Some desks are trays.
Some trays are plates.
Some plates are desks.

Conclusions:
I. All desks are plates.
II. All plates are desks.
III. Some plates are trays.
IV. All trays are desks.
A: Only conclusion III follows
B: Only conclusion IV follows
C: Only conclusion II follows
D: Only conclusion I follows
solution:
"Some trays are plates" converts to "Some plates are trays", so III follows.

The statements are all "some" statements, so no "all" conclusion (I, II, IV) can be drawn. Only III follows.

stem:
Select the option that expresses the given sentence in passive voice.
He won’t receive any better choice than this from anywhere.
A: Any better choice won’t be received by him than this from anywhere.
B: Any better choice wouldn’t have received by him than this from anywhere.
C: Any better choice shouldn’t be received by him than this from anywhere.

solution:
"Won't receive" (simple future, negative) becomes "won't be received" in the passive, with "him" as the agent. B and C change the modal (wouldn't, shouldn't) and B lacks "been"; D has the ungrammatical "have been be".
