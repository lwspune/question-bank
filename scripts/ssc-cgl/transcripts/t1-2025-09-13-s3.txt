chapter: Statement-Based Reasoning
subtopic: Statement and Assumptions
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: A
stem:
**Instruction: Identify the assumptions that must hold for the statement to be valid, then choose the correct option.**
**Statement:**
The government has decided to implement stricter regulations on industrial pollution to curb the growing environmental concerns.
**Assumptions:**
I. Industrial pollution is a significant cause of environmental degradation.
II. Stricter regulations will reduce industrial pollution.
III. Environmental concerns are primarily related to industrial activities.
A: Only I and II are implicit
B: Only I and III are implicit
C: All I, II, and III are implicit
D: Only I is implicit
solution:
I is implicit: the government targets industrial pollution to ease environmental concerns, so it must take that pollution to be a significant cause of the damage.

II is implicit: the decision only makes sense if stricter rules are expected to cut the pollution.

III is not implicit: the statement needs industry to be a significant cause, not the primary one. Other causes can exist.

So only I and II are implicit.

stem:
How many diagonals does a hexagon have?
A: 9
B: 12
C: 15
D: 18
solution:
A polygon with n sides has \(\frac{n(n-3)}{2}\) diagonals. For n = 6: \(\frac{6 \times 3}{2} = 9\).

Check: each of the 6 vertices joins 3 non-adjacent vertices, and each diagonal is counted twice: \(18 \div 2 = 9\).

subtopic: Reversal and Opposite Letters
difficulty: HARD

stem:
If 'PHANTOM' is coded as 'KLSQZLN', then how is 'SPECTOR' coded?
A: HFKXGLI
B: HGKXGQI
C: HTWFZLI
D: HGLXGQJ
solution:
The first and last letters are replaced by their opposite letters (A-Z, B-Y, ...): P to K and M to N.

The middle letters move by fixed amounts position by position: H +4 = L, A -8 = S, N +3 = Q, T +6 = Z, O -3 = L.

For SPECTOR: S to H and R to I (opposites); P +4 = T, E -8 = W, C +3 = F, T +6 = Z, O -3 = L. The code is HTWFZLI.
