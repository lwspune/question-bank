subtopic: Linear and Circular Seating
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: D
stem:
Eight persons A, B, C, D, E, F, G, and H are sitting around a circle facing the center.
C is second to the right of A.
E is opposite C.
F is to the immediate left of E.
B is to the immediate right of A.
G is to the immediate left of D.
Who is sitting to the right of D?
A: H
B: U
C: B
D: F
solution:
Number the seats 1 to 8 going to the left (clockwise for people facing the centre) and put A in seat 1. Going right from A: B in seat 8 (immediate right), C in seat 7 (second to the right).

The person immediately to the right of D (seat 5) is in seat 4: F.

chapter: Coding-Decoding
subtopic: Word-Code Sentences
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: A
stem:
**Instruction:** In a certain coding system:
**‘dream focus build skill’** is coded as **‘trq, xnz, hmd, clv’**
**‘focus logic dream’** is coded as **‘trq, jkp, xnz’**
**‘trust logic build dream’** is coded as **‘xnj, clv, jkp, trq’**
**Question:** What is the code for ‘build skill logic trust’?
A: clv, hmd, jkp, xnj
B: clv, xnj, jkp, trq
C: hmd, xnz, jkp, xnj
D: hmd, clv, xnj, xnz
solution:
"dream" is in all three sentences and trq is the only code in all three: dream = trq.

Sentences 1 and 2 share dream and focus, codes trq and xnz: focus = xnz.

Sentences 2 and 3 share logic and dream, codes jkp and trq: logic = jkp.

Sentences 1 and 3 share build and dream, codes clv and trq: build = clv.

Left over: skill = hmd (sentence 1), trust = xnj (sentence 3).

build skill logic trust = clv, hmd, jkp, xnj.
