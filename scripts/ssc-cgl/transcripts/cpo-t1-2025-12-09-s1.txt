The Nagpur seminar was 5 days before the Indore seminar. The Bhopal seminar was 2 days before the Nagpur seminar. If Indore seminar was held on \(22^{\text{nd}}\) May, what was the date of the Bhopal seminar?
A: \(14^{\text{th}}\) May

subtopic: Cause, Effect and Courses of Action
difficulty: EASY

Consider the following statement and choose the option that follows.

Problem: The number of ATM fraud cases is increasing in the city.

Course of Action:
1. Install CCTV cameras at all ATMs.
2. Stop issuing ATM cards to customers.
A: Only 1 follow
B: Only 2 follow
C: Both follow

If 25 \(\rightarrow\) 29, 41 \(\rightarrow\) 17, 38 \(\rightarrow\) 73, then 63 \(\rightarrow\)?
A: 125
B: 22
C: 83
D: 45
solution:

difficulty: EASY

notice: SSC's provisional key gives "Neither", but from T >= V exactly one of "T > V" and "T = V" must hold, so either I or II follows; the answer shown is ours.

chapter: Arithmetic Reasoning
subtopic: Speed, Time and Work
difficulty: EASY
answer: B
stem:
A can complete a work in 12 days, and B in 16 days. They work together for 3 days. How much work is left?
A: \(\frac{7}{12}\)
B: \(\frac{9}{16}\)
C: \(\frac{5}{8}\)
D: \(\frac{1}{2}\)
solution:
In one day A does \(\frac{1}{12}\) and B does \(\frac{1}{16}\) of the work; together \(\frac{4}{48} + \frac{3}{48} = \frac{7}{48}\).

In 3 days they do \(3 \times \frac{7}{48} = \frac{21}{48} = \frac{7}{16}\), so the work left is \(1 - \frac{7}{16} = \frac{9}{16}\).

stem:
In a mixture, the ratio of milk to water is 7:2. If 9 liters of water is added, the ratio becomes 7:5. Find the original quantity of milk.
A: 21 liters
B: 28 liters
C: 35 liters
D: 42 liters
solution:

notice: SSC's provisional key gives "Only I", but conclusion II follows too (all C are D and no C is B, so some D are not B); the answer shown is ours.

subtopic: Direction Sense
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: B
stem:
A person starts to walk from a ground in the west direction to reach his home. After 13km he reaches at point Y. From point Y, he takes a right turn, walks 7 km and reaches point C. From point C, he takes a left turn, walks 9km and reaches point T. From point T, he takes a left turn and walks 4km and reaches his home.

What is the shortest distance between his home and point Y?
A: \(6\sqrt{5}\) km
B: \(3\sqrt{10}\) km
C: \(4\sqrt{5}\) km
D: \(7\sqrt{10}\) km
solution:
Walking west, a right turn faces north: Y to C is 7 km north. A left turn from north faces west: C to T is 9 km west. A left turn from west faces south: T to home is 4 km south.

From Y the home is 9 km west and 7 - 4 = 3 km north, so the distance is \(\sqrt{9^2 + 3^2} = \sqrt{81 + 9} = \sqrt{90} = 3\sqrt{10}\) km.

answer: C
blind: D
flawed: yes
notice: The printed series fits no single rule cleanly; the answer shown is SSC's key, which reads it as the Fibonacci series 0, 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, where 4 does not belong.

chapter: Grammar
subtopic: Direct and Indirect Speech
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: B
stem:
A sentence is provided in direct speech. From the four given options, choose the one that most accurately conveys the sentence in its corresponding indirect speech.
She said, “If only I hadn’t trusted him!”
A: She had regret that she had trusted him.
B: She said that she wished she hadn’t trusted him.
C: She said if only she hadn’t trusted him.
D: She wished she didn’t trust him.
solution:
"If only ...!" expresses a wish or regret, so it is reported with "wished", keeping the past perfect for a past regret: "She said that she wished she hadn't trusted him." A is clumsy ("had regret") and changes the structure, C keeps the exclamation "if only" inside reported speech, and D changes "hadn't trusted" (past) to "didn't trust" (present).

=== 199

subtopic: Ordering Sentences in a Paragraph
difficulty: EASY
answer: A
stem:
Rearrange the following sentences in correct order to make a logical passage.
1. Employees receive a performance review at the end of each quarter.
2. Based on performance, goals are set for the next quarter.
3. Feedback is provided during the review session.
4. The employees then work towards meeting the set targets.
A: 1-3-2-4
B: 2-1-4-3
C: 3-2-1-4
D: 4-1-3-2
solution:
1 introduces the quarterly review. 3 follows with what happens during that review session (feedback). 2 then sets goals for the next quarter on that basis, and 4 ("then work towards meeting the set targets") refers back to those goals. Order: 1-3-2-4.

Rearrange the following sentences in correct order to make a logical passage.
1. This leads to a decline in their overall well-being.
2. Many people today suffer from excessive stress.
3. Consequently, coping mechanisms become essential for a balanced life.
4. It often manifests as physical and mental exhaustion.
A: 2-4-1-3
B: 1-3-2-4
C: 4-3-1-2
D: 3-2-4-1
solution:
2 introduces the topic (stress). 4 ("It") refers to the stress and says how it shows itself. 1 ("This leads to") follows from the exhaustion, and 3 ("Consequently") draws the conclusion. Order: 2-4-1-3.
