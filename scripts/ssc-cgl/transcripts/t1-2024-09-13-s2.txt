=== 1
chapter: Coding-Decoding
subtopic: Position Values and Sums
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: A
stem:
In a certain code language, 'GLOBAL' is written as '221714272817', 'FOREST' is written as '23141124109', how will 'IMPACT' be written in that language?
A: 20161328269
B: 18141126247
C: 20161226259
D: 20161327259
solution:
Each letter is written as its reverse alphabetical position plus 2 (reverse position of a letter = 27 minus its position).

GLOBAL: G 20+2=22, L 15+2=17, O 12+2=14, B 25+2=27, A 26+2=28, L 15+2=17, giving 221714272817. FOREST: 23, 14, 11, 24, 10, 9, giving 23141124109.

IMPACT: I 18+2=20, M 14+2=16, P 11+2=13, A 26+2=28, C 24+2=26, T 7+2=9, giving 20161328269.

chapter: Blood Relations
subtopic: Coded Statements and Expressions
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: D
stem:
In a certain code language,
'A + B' means 'A is the mother of B',
'A – B' means 'A is the father of B',
'A $ B' means 'A is the son of B',
'A % B' means 'A is the sister of B',
'A / B' means 'A is the wife of B' and
'A * B' means 'A is the husband of B'.

Which of the following means P is the Son's wife of T?
i. T / B – K * P + O
ii. T + K * P + B % O
iii. K * P + B % T + P
iv. P + T * K + B % O
A: Only ii
B: iii and iv
C: Only i
D: Both i and ii
solution:
i. T is the wife of B and B is the father of K, so K is T's son. K is the husband of P, so P is the wife of T's son.

ii. T is the mother of K, and K is the husband of P, so again P is the wife of T's son.

iii. Here P is the mother of B, B is the sister of T, and T is the mother of P, which is contradictory and does not make P T's son's wife. iv. P is the mother of T, not his son's wife.

Both i and ii.

subtopic: Three-Statement Syllogism
difficulty: EASY

stem:
Read the given statements and conclusions carefully. Assuming that the information given in the statements is true, even if it appears to be at variance with commonly known facts, decide which of the given conclusions logically follow(s) from the statements.

Some Toys are Plastic.
Some Plastic are Metal.

stem:
Six symbols %, &, ?, +, $ and @ are written on different faces of a dice. Two positions of this dice are shown in the figure below. Find the symbol on the face opposite to the one having @.
A: $
B: %
C: &
D: ?
solution:
The first view shows % on top with & and ? on the side faces; the second shows + on top with & and @. & appears on the left in both, so its neighbours are %, ?, + and @, and & is opposite $.

Going round & in the same direction, the first view gives % then ?, the second gives + then @. The four neighbours must form one ring in which % is followed by ?, and + by @; that ring is %, ?, +, @. So % is opposite + and ? is opposite @.

subtopic: Lenses and Lens Formula
difficulty: EASY
answer: A
stem:
A small object is placed on the focus on the left side of a convex lens. Where will be the image formed?
A: At infinity on the right side of the lens.
B: At infinity on the left side of the lens.
C: At the focus on the right side of the lens.
D: At the centre on the right side of the lens.
solution:
Rays from an object at the principal focus of a convex lens leave the lens parallel to each other, so they meet only at infinity on the other (right) side. The image is real, inverted and highly enlarged, at infinity.

With the lens formula, \(\frac{1}{v} = \frac{1}{f} + \frac{1}{u} = \frac{1}{f} - \frac{1}{f} = 0\), so \(v\) is infinite.
