chapter: Mathematical Operations
subtopic: Interchanging Two Signs
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: D
stem:
Which two signs should be interchanged to make the given equation correct?
\(60 \times 4 + 9 \div 3 - 8 = 34\)
A: \(+\) and \(-\)
B: \(\div\) and \(+\)
C: \(+\) and \(\times\)
D: \(\div\) and \(\times\)
solution:
Interchange \(\div\) and \(\times\): \(60 \div 4 + 9 \times 3 - 8 = 15 + 27 - 8 = 34\). Correct.

Check the others: \(+\) and \(-\) gives \(240 - 3 + 8 = 245\); \(\div\) and \(+\) gives \(60 \times 4 \div 9 + 3 - 8\), not a whole number; \(+\) and \(\times\) gives \(60 + 36 \div 3 - 8 = 64\).

chapter: Blood Relations
subtopic: Coded Relations
difficulty: EASY
answer: C
stem:
‘P @ Q’ means ‘P is the wife of Q’.
‘P # Q’ means ‘P is the brother of Q’.
‘P \$ Q’ means ‘P is the father of Q’.
‘P – Q’ means ‘P is the daughter of Q’.
If Z – B \$ D \$ G, then how is Z related to G?
A: Z is the mother of G
B: Z is the sister of G
C: Z is the father’s sister of G
D: Z is the daughter of G
solution:
Z – B: Z is the daughter of B. B \$ D: B is the father of D. D \$ G: D is the father of G.

So Z is D's sister, and D is G's father: Z is G's father's sister.

stem:
Three of the following four letter-clusters are alike in a certain way and one is different. Pick the odd one out.
A: JQM
B: DWG
C: BXE
D: AZD
solution:
In each cluster the second letter is the opposite (reverse) letter of the first, and the third letter is the first letter + 3.

B: Were the return tickets to Dehradun not being bought by you?
C: Had the return tickets to Dehradun not bought by you?
D: Were the return tickets to Dehradun not bought by you?
solution:
A simple past question "Did ... buy" becomes "Was/Were + object + past participle" in the passive. "Tickets" is plural, so "Were the return tickets ... not bought by you?" A uses singular "Was", B changes the tense to past continuous, and C uses "Had" with no "been".
