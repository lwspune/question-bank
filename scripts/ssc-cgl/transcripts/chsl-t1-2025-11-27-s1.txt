B: He suggested that I take rest.
C: He suggested me that I take rest.

solution:

chapter: Grammar
subtopic: Direct and Indirect Speech
difficulty: EASY
answer: C
stem:
Choose the correct indirect narration of the following statement from the given alternatives:
The farmer said, "There is a well nearby our village which is filled with different kinds of snails."
A: The farmer said that there was a well nearby their village which had been filled with different kinds of snails.
B: The farmer said that there had been a well nearby their village which was filled with different kinds of snails.
C: The farmer said that there was a well nearby their village which was filled with different kinds of snails.
D: The farmer said that there was a well nearby your village which was filled with different kinds of snails.
solution:
The reporting verb "said" is in the past, so both present verbs move one step back: "is" becomes "was" and "is filled" becomes "was filled". "Our" (the farmer and his people) becomes "their". Option A wrongly makes "is filled" into "had been filled", B wrongly makes "there is" into "there had been", and D wrongly changes "our" to "your".

answer: C
stem:
**Change the following sentence into the Active form.**
It was being said that the new policy would be a disaster.
A: The new policy would be a disaster.
B: The news said the new policy would be a disaster.
C: People were saying that the new policy would be a disaster.
D: Someone has said that the new policy was a disaster.
solution:
"It was being said" is the past continuous passive with no agent named, so the active supplies a general subject ("people") and keeps the past continuous: "People were saying that the new policy would be a disaster." (Check: a discount of \(\frac{2}{3}\) of the marked price leaves \(\frac{1}{3}\), and \(\frac{1}{\frac{1}{3}} = 3\).)
