=== 17
chapter: Blood Relations
subtopic: Coded Statements and Expressions
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: A
stem:
‘\(A + B\)’ means ‘A is brother of B’
‘\(A - B\)’ means ‘A is mother of B’
‘\(A \times B\)’ means ‘A is husband of B’
‘\(A \div B\)’ means ‘A is sister of B’
If \(C \div B - E + A \times F - D\), then which of the following statements is NOT correct?
A: D is the daughter of B.
B: C is the sister of the mother of A.
C: F is the wife of the son of B.
D: B is the mother of A.
solution:
C is the sister of B; B is the mother of E; E is the brother of A, so B is A's mother too; A is the husband of F; F is the mother of D.

So D is the child of A and F, that is, B's grandchild, not B's daughter. Statement A is not correct. B (C is the sister of B, A's mother), C (F is the wife of A, B's son) and D are all correct.
