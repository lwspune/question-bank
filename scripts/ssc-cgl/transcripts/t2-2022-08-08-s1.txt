The cost price of an article is ₹ 2800. Profit as a percentage of selling price is 20 percent. What is the actual profit (in ₹)?

subtopic: Cylinders
difficulty: EASY
answer: C
stem:
The sum of the curved surface area and total surface area of a solid cylinder is 2068 \(\text{cm}^2\). If radius of its base is 7 cm, then what is the volume of this cylinder? ( use \(\pi = 22/7\))
A: 2060 \(\text{cm}^3\)
B: 2480 \(\text{cm}^3\)
C: 3080 \(\text{cm}^3\)
D: 2760 \(\text{cm}^3\)
solution:
CSA + TSA \(= 2\pi rh + (2\pi rh + 2\pi r^2) = 4\pi rh + 2\pi r^2\).

With \(r = 7\): \(2\pi r^2 = 2 \times \frac{22}{7} \times 49 = 308\), so \(4\pi rh = 2068 - 308 = 1760\), that is \(88h = 1760\) and \(h = 20\) cm.

Volume \(= \frac{22}{7} \times 49 \times 20 = 3080\ \text{cm}^3\).

Check: CSA \(= 2 \times \frac{22}{7} \times 7 \times 20 = 880\), TSA \(= 880 + 308 = 1188\), and \(880 + 1188 = 2068\).

A alone can do a work in 11 days. B alone can do the same work in 22 days. C alone can do the same work in 33 days. They work in the following manner:
Day 1: A and B work.
Day 2: B and C work.
Day 3: C and A work.
Day 4: A and B work. And so on.
In how many days will the work be completed?
A: 9 days
B: 12 days
C: 3 days
D: 6 days
solution:
Take the work as 66 units: A does 6, B 3, C 2 units a day.

Day 1: 9 units, day 2: 5 units, day 3: 8 units, so each 3-day cycle does 22 units.

\(66 \div 22 = 3\) cycles, so the work ends exactly at the end of day 9.

Check: after 8 days \(44 + 9 + 5 = 58\) units, and day 9 adds 8 to reach 66.
