=== 94
subject: Physics
chapter: Astronomy and Space
subtopic: The Universe and Satellites
difficulty: EASY
answer: C
stem:
Why can a geo-stationary satellite monitor the movement of clouds accurately?
A: Because it moves in North to South direction.
B: Because it moves in South to North direction.
C: Because it is stationary relative to any place on Earth.
D: Because it rotates faster than Earth.
solution:
A geostationary satellite orbits above the equator in the same direction as the Earth's rotation with a period of 24 hours, so it stays fixed over the same place on Earth. It can therefore watch the same region continuously and track cloud movement.

Polar satellites move north to south, and a satellite that went round faster than the Earth would not stay over one place.

What immediate measure was taken in 1991 to resolve the balance of payments crisis in India?
A: Increases restrictions on import.
B: Devaluation of the rupee against foreign currencies.
C: Revaluation of the rupee against foreign currencies.
D: Introduction of fixed exchange rate.
solution:
In July 1991 the rupee was devalued against foreign currencies in two steps (about 18-19%), making exports cheaper and imports dearer, while India also pledged gold and took IMF support. The reforms that followed liberalised imports and moved towards a market-determined exchange rate, not a fixed one.

=== 100
subject: History

subtopic: Mahajanapadas, Magadha and Mauryan Empire
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: D
stem:
The most important achievement of Shishunaga was _____.
A: conquering Kalinga
B: building Pataliputra fort
C: holding the first Buddhist council
D: defeating Avanti (Malwa) and annexing it to Magadh
solution:
