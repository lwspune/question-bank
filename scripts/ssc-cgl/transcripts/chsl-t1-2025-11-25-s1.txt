From the alternatives given below, select the correct passive voice of the following sentence.
Make sure that your children have completed their tasks before going to school.
A: Let it be made sure that their tasks have been completed by your children before going to school.

C: Let it be sure that their tasks have been completed by your children before going to school.

=== 91
subject: Physics
chapter: Work, Energy and Power
subtopic: Work-Energy Theorem and Power
difficulty: EASY
answer: B
stem:
A girl with 50 kg mass climbs up a rope through a height of 5 metres. She takes 25 s to accomplish the task. What is the power (in watts) expended by the girl?
A: 49
B: 98
C: 68.6
D: 88.2
solution:
Work done against gravity \(= mgh = 50 \times 9.8 \times 5 = 2450\) J.

Power \(= \frac{2450}{25} = 98\) W.
