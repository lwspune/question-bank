=== 1
chapter: Coding-Decoding
subtopic: Reversal and Opposite Letters
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: A
stem:
In a certain code language, 'MOBILE' is coded as BJFZLK and 'TABLET' is coded as QCIZXR. How will 'KINDLE' be coded in the same language?
A: BJALFI
B: BJBKFI
C: CIBKGC
D: CJBLGI
solution:
Write the word backwards, then move the letters back 3, 2, 3, 2, 3, 2 places.

MOBILE backwards is ELIBOM: E-3 = B, L-2 = J, I-3 = F, B-2 = Z, O-3 = L, M-2 = K, giving BJFZLK. TABLET backwards is TELBAT: T-3 = Q, E-2 = C, L-3 = I, B-2 = Z, A-3 = X, T-2 = R, giving QCIZXR.

KINDLE backwards is ELDNIK: E-3 = B, L-2 = J, D-3 = A, N-2 = L, I-3 = F, K-2 = I, giving BJALFI.

stem:
Which two signs and two numbers should be interchanged to make the following equation correct?
\(840 \div 38 - 7 + 10 \times 12 = 462\)
A: 12 and 10, \(\div\) and \(+\)
B: 12 and 38, \(\times\) and \(-\)
C: 12 and 7, \(+\) and \(\times\)
D: 840 and 10, \(+\) and \(-\)

In a certain code language, ‘RELAX’ is written as ‘CPPZC’ and ‘STAKE’ is written as ‘RQEGM’. How will ‘WINDS’ be written in that language?

B: HUSVG
