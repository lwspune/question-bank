chapter: Blood Relations
subtopic: Coded Statements and Expressions
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: C

In a certain code language
A + B means ‘A is the brother of B’
A – B means ‘A is the father of B’
A \(\times\) B means ‘A is the wife of B’

If 4 December 2014 was Thursday, then what was the day of the week on 10 December 2018?

=== 35
chapter: Syllogism
subtopic: Three-Statement Syllogism
difficulty: EASY
answer: D
stem:
In this question, three statements are given, followed by three conclusions numbered I, II and III. Assuming the statements to be true, even if they seem to be at variance with commonly known facts, decide which of the conclusion(s) logically follows/follow from the statements.
Statements:
Some strawberries are oranges.
Some oranges are kiwis.
All kiwis are melons.
Conclusions:
I. No strawberry is a melon.
II. All strawberries are melons.
III. Some strawberries are kiwis.
A: Both conclusions I and II follow.
B: Both conclusions II and III follow.
C: All conclusions I, II and III follow.
D: Neither conclusions I, II nor III follows.
solution:
Strawberries are linked to oranges only through "some", and the oranges that are kiwis need not be the oranges that are strawberries. So nothing definite links strawberries to kiwis or to melons.

III (some strawberries are kiwis) is possible but not certain. I and II are each possible but neither is certain, and they are not an either-or pair (that pair would be "some strawberries are melons" and "no strawberry is a melon"). So none follows.
