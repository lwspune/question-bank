subtopic: Articles, Determiners and Comparison
difficulty: EASY

Identify the incorrect sentence.
A: Injured were taken to the hospital.
B: The baby laughed on seeing itself in the mirror.
C: Whose book is this?

She always ______ her failures to bad luck rather than taking ownership.

B: turns out
C: breaks into
D: boils down

=== 27
chapter: Arithmetic Reasoning
subtopic: Speed, Time and Work
difficulty: EASY
answer: C
stem:
A train traveling at a constant speed enters a 200-meter tunnel. It takes 10 seconds for the entire train to pass through the tunnel. If the train is 100 meters long, what is the speed of the train in meters per second?
A: 10 m/s
B: 20 m/s
C: 30 m/s
D: 40 m/s
solution:
To pass through the tunnel completely the train covers the tunnel plus its own length: \(200 + 100 = 300\) m.

Speed \(= \frac{300}{10} = 30\) m/s.

Check: at 30 m/s the train covers 300 m in exactly 10 seconds.

Read the following statement carefully and identify the conclusion that follows.

Statement:
All planets are stars.

difficulty: EASY

stem:
Who was the Pallava king that built the 'seven rathas' at Mahabalipuram?
A: Pulkeshin I
B: Rajendra I
C: Senguttuvan
D: Narasimhavarman
solution:
The monolithic rathas of Mahabalipuram (Mamallapuram) were carved under the Pallava king Narasimhavarman I, called Mamalla, after whom the town is named. (The well-known group is the five Pancha Rathas; the site is also called the Seven Pagodas.)
