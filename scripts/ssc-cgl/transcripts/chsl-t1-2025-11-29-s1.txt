subtopic: Active and Passive Voice
difficulty: EASY
answer: D
stem:
From the alternatives given below, select the correct passive voice of the following sentence.
The students were imitating the way of the description by the teacher.
A: The way of the description by the teacher was imitated by the students.
B: The way of the description by the teacher were being imitated by the students.
C: The way of the description by the teacher had been imitated by the students.
D: The way of the description by the teacher was being imitated by the students.
solution:
The active verb is past continuous ("were imitating"), so the passive is "was being + past participle". The new subject "the way" is singular, so "was being imitated", not "were being imitated". A is simple past and C is past perfect.

answer: C
stem:
Choose the option that is the most appropriate conversion of the following sentence into Direct Speech.
He said that by the time she arrived, the guests had already started leaving.
A: He said, "By the time she has arrived, the guests were already leaving."

If 117 " 78 = 13 and 192 " 9 = 61, then what is the value of 114 " 12?
A: 34
B: 31
C: 28
D: 37
solution:
The rule is (first number minus second number) divided by 3: \(\frac{117 - 78}{3} = \frac{39}{3} = 13\) and \(\frac{192 - 9}{3} = \frac{183}{3} = 61\).
