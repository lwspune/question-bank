chapter: Inequalities
subtopic: Direct Inequalities
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: C
stem:
The given statements are followed by two conclusions. Find out which conclusion is TRUE based on the given statements.

Statements:
\(J > K = L, M \ge N < K, O < J < P, N = Q \ge R\)
Conclusions:
I. \(M > R\)
II. \(M = R\)
A: Neither conclusion I nor II is true
B: Both conclusion I and II are true
C: Either conclusion I or II is true
D: Only conclusion II is true
solution:
\(M \ge N = Q \ge R\), so \(M \ge R\). Neither \(M > R\) nor \(M = R\) is definite alone, but together they make up \(M \ge R\), a complementary pair. So either I or II is true.

difficulty: EASY
answer: A
stem:
A tank can be filled by an inlet pipe in 8 hours. Another outlet pipe can empty the same tank in 12 hours. If both pipes are opened simultaneously, how long will it take to fill the empty tank completely?
A: 24 h
B: 16 h
C: 20 h
D: 48 h
solution:
Net filling per hour: \(\frac{1}{8} - \frac{1}{12} = \frac{3 - 2}{24} = \frac{1}{24}\) of the tank. The tank fills in 24 hours.

Check with a tank of 24 units: the inlet adds 3 units an hour, the outlet removes 2, net 1 unit an hour, so 24 hours.
