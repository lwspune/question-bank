What should come in place of the question mark '?' in the following number series?
12, 6, 9, 22.5, ?
A: 79.75
B: 77.75
C: 78.75
D: 76.75
solution:

A and B can do a job in 6 days. A alone can do it in 10 days. In how many days can B do it alone?
A: 8
B: 20
C: 12
D: 15
solution:
B's one-day work = \(\frac{1}{6} - \frac{1}{10} = \frac{5 - 3}{30} = \frac{2}{30} = \frac{1}{15}\), so B alone takes 15 days.

subtopic: Inferences
difficulty: MODERATE

stem:
**Instruction:** Choose the inference that are clearly supported by the statement.
**Statement:** All successful candidates for the astronaut training program possess a doctoral degree in a STEM field, and a perfect physical fitness record is also required.
**Inferences:**
Inference I: A candidate with a doctoral degree in a STEM field who also has a perfect physical fitness record will be selected for the astronaut training program.

Which of the following best represents the relationship among Doctors, Males, and Surgeons?
