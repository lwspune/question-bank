Three of the following four options are alike in a certain way and thus form a group. Which is the one that does NOT belong to that group?
(NOTE: Operations should be performed on the whole numbers, without breaking down the numbers into its constituent digits. chapter: Analogy
subtopic: Number Analogy
difficulty: HARD
answer: D
stem:
61 is related to 18 following a certain logic. Following the same logic, 213 is related to 38. To which of the following is 509 related, following the same logic? (NOTE : Operations should be performed on the whole numbers, without breaking down the numbers into its constituent digits. E.g. 13 – Operations on 13 such as adding /deleting /multiplying etc., to 13 can be performed. Breaking down 13 into 1 and 3 and then performing mathematical operations on 1 and 3 is not allowed.)
A: 78
B: 64
C: 72
D: 66
solution:
Each first number is a cube minus 3, and the second is the square of that cube root plus 2.

\(61 = 4^3 - 3\) and \(4^2 + 2 = 18\); \(213 = 6^3 - 3\) and \(6^2 + 2 = 38\).

\(509 = 8^3 - 3\), so the answer is \(8^2 + 2 = 66\).

=== 17
chapter: Syllogism
subtopic: Three-Statement Syllogism
difficulty: EASY
answer: C
stem:
Three statements are given, followed by three conclusions numbered I, II and III. Assuming the statements to be true, even if they seem to be at variance with commonly known facts, decide which of the conclusions logically follow(s) from the statements.

Statements:
All spoons are forks.
All forks are knives.
No knife is a spatula.

Conclusions:
I. Some knives are spoons.
II. No fork is a spatula.
III. Some spoons are spatulas.
A: Neither conclusion follows
B: Only conclusion III follows
C: Only conclusions I and II follow
D: Only conclusion I follows
solution:
Spoons sit inside forks, which sit inside knives, so some knives are spoons: I follows.

Every fork is a knife and no knife is a spatula, so no fork is a spatula: II follows.

Spoons are knives, so no spoon is a spatula: III is false. Only I and II follow.

=== 25
chapter: Syllogism
subtopic: Three-Statement Syllogism
difficulty: EASY
answer: A
stem:
Read the given statements and conclusions carefully. Assuming that the information given in the statements is true, even if it appears to be at variance with commonly known facts, decide which of the given conclusions logically follow(s) from the statements.
Statements:
Some Mirrors are Decoratives.
No Decorative is a Plant.
All Plants are Trees.
Conclusions:
(I) Some Mirrors are Plants.
(II) Some Decoratives are Trees.
A: Neither conclusion (I) nor (II) follows.
B: Only conclusion (I) follows.
C: Both conclusions (I) and (II) follow.
D: Only conclusion (II) follows.
solution:
Mirrors meet Plants only through Decoratives, and no Decorative is a Plant, so no link to Plants is forced: I does not follow.

Decoratives are kept out of Plants, but Trees can lie outside Plants too, so Decoratives may or may not be Trees: II does not follow. Neither follows.
