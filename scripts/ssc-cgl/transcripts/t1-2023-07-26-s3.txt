Energy : Joule

=== 3
chapter: Analogy
subtopic: Number Set Analogy
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: D
stem:
Select the set in which the numbers are related in the same way as are the numbers of the following set.
(NOTE: Operations should be performed on the whole numbers, without breaking down the numbers into their constituent digits. E.g. 13 – Operations on 13 such as adding/substracting/multiplying etc. to 13 can be performed. Breaking down 13 into 1 and 3 and then performing mathematical operations on 1 and 3 is not allowed.)
(7, 15, 11)
(9, 19, 14)
A: (8, 16, 13)
B: (6, 13, 9)
C: (13, 25, 21)
D: (11, 23, 17)
solution:
Second number = first \(\times 2 + 1\); third number = half the sum of the first two.

(7, 15, 11): \(7 \times 2 + 1 = 15\) and \(\frac{7 + 15}{2} = 11\). (9, 19, 14): \(9 \times 2 + 1 = 19\) and \(\frac{9 + 19}{2} = 14\).

(11, 23, 17): \(11 \times 2 + 1 = 23\) and \(\frac{11 + 23}{2} = 17\). It fits.

The others fail: \(8 \times 2 + 1 = 17 \ne 16\); \(\frac{6 + 13}{2} = 9.5 \ne 9\); \(13 \times 2 + 1 = 27 \ne 25\).

chapter: Non-Verbal Series and Analogy
subtopic: Symbol Grids
difficulty: HARD
answer: A
figure: stem+options
stem:
Select the figure that will replace the question mark (?) in the following figure series.
A: [figure]
B: [figure]
C: [figure]
D: [figure]
solution:
Each figure holds five symbols: top-left, top-right, centre, bottom-left, bottom-right. The figures read
1: 8, K, B, V, =
2: K, 8, V, =, B
3: C, V, =, B, 8
4: V, C, B, 8, =

The steps alternate between two rules.

Steps 1 to 2 and 3 to 4: the top two symbols swap; the centre goes to bottom-right, bottom-left goes to the centre, bottom-right goes to bottom-left.

Step 2 to 3: the centre goes to top-right, bottom-left to the centre, bottom-right to bottom-left, top-right to bottom-right, and the top-left symbol is replaced by a new one (K gives way to C).

Step 4 to 5 repeats that second rule: top-right = B (old centre), centre = 8 (old bottom-left), bottom-left = = (old bottom-right), bottom-right = C (old top-right), and a new symbol at top-left in place of V.

Option 1 (2 at top-left, B top-right, 8 centre, = bottom-left, C bottom-right) fits. Option 3 has the same four but keeps V at top-left, which the rule replaces.

chapter: Analogy
subtopic: Letter Analogy
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: B
stem:
Select the option that is related to the fifth letter-cluster in the same way as the second letter-cluster is related to the first letter-cluster and the fourth letter-cluster is related to the third letter-cluster.
BET : UXM :: SYP : LRI :: HOX : ?
A: NVE
B: AHQ
C: BHQ
D: MVE
solution:
Each letter moves 19 places forward (the same as 7 back), wrapping after Z.

B(2) + 19 = 21 (U), E(5) + 19 = 24 (X), T(20) + 19 = 39 - 26 = 13 (M). S, Y, P give L, R, I the same way.

H(8) + 19 = 27 - 26 = 1 (A), O(15) + 19 = 34 - 26 = 8 (H), X(24) + 19 = 43 - 26 = 17 (Q). Answer AHQ.

Check backwards: A, H, Q each 7 back give H, O, X.

=== 19
chapter: Syllogism
subtopic: Three-Statement Syllogism
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: A
stem:
Three statements are followed by three conclusions numbered I, II and III. You have to consider these statements to be true, even if they seem to be at variance with commonly known facts. Decide which of the given conclusions logically follow(s) from the given statements.

Statements:
All tools are spanners.
Some spanners are utensils.
No spanner is an iron.

Conclusions:
(I) Some tools are irons.
(II) Some utensils are tools.
(III) Some utensils are not iron.
A: Only conclusion III follows
B: All conclusions I, II and III follow
C: Only conclusion I follows
D: Either conclusion I or conclusion III follows
solution:
All tools are spanners and no spanner is an iron, so no tool is an iron: I is false.

The utensils that are spanners need not include any tool: II does not follow.

Some utensils are spanners, and no spanner is an iron, so those utensils are not iron: III follows.

Only III follows.

subtopic: Three-Statement Syllogism
difficulty: EASY

stem:
Three statements are given followed by three conclusions numbered I, II and III. Assuming the statements to be true, even if they seem to be at variance with commonly known facts, decide which of the conclusions logically follow(s) from the statements.

Statements:
All cancers are diseases.
All tuberculosis are diseases.
All diseases are curable.

Conclusions:
I. All tuberculosis are curable.
II. Some cancers are tuberculosis.
III. All cancers are curable.
A: Only conclusions II and III follow
B: Only conclusions I and III follow
C: All conclusions follow
D: Only conclusions I and II follow
solution:
Tuberculosis and cancers are both inside diseases, which are all inside curable, so I and III follow.

Cancers and tuberculosis are both subsets of diseases but need not overlap, so II does not follow.

Only I and III follow.
