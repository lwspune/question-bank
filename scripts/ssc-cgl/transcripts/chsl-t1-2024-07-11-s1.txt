difficulty: MODERATE

stem:
Select the option that will improve the given sentence by using the most appropriate verb.
Soorya walking to college every day.
A: Soorya walk to college every day.
B: Soorya walked to college every day.
C: Soorya have walked to college every day.
D: Soorya is walking to college every day.
solution:
The sentence lacks a finite verb. "Every day" marks a habit, which takes a simple tense: "Soorya walked to college every day" (a past habit) is correct.

chapter: Blood Relations
subtopic: Coded Statements and Expressions
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: A
stem:
In a certain language,
A & B means A is the wife of B,
A \(\div\) B means A is the daughter of B,
A \(\times\) B means A is the son of B,
A + B means A is the sister of B and
A @ B means A is the father of B.
P & Q @ L + T & U @ V
Based on the above, how is Q related to V?
A: Mother’s father
B: Sister
C: Mother
D: Father’s mother
solution:
Q @ L: Q is L's father. L + T: L is T's sister, so Q is T's father too.

T & U: T is U's wife. U @ V: U is V's father, so T is V's mother.

So Q is the father of V's mother: V's mother's father.

chapter: Syllogism
subtopic: Three-Statement Syllogism
difficulty: EASY
answer: C
stem:
In this question, three statements are given, followed by three conclusions numbered I, II and III. Assuming the statements to be true, even if they seem to be at variance with commonly known facts, decide which of the conclusion(s) logically follows/follow from the statements.
Statements:
All ladders are chairs.
All chairs are tables.
No table is a stair.
Conclusions:
I. Some tables are ladders.
II. No chair is a stair.
III. Some ladders are stairs.
A: Only conclusion III follows.
B: Only conclusion I follows.
C: Both conclusions I and II follow.
D: Only conclusion II follows.
solution:
All ladders are chairs and all chairs are tables, so all ladders are tables, and some tables are ladders: I follows.

All chairs are tables and no table is a stair, so no chair is a stair: II follows.

Every ladder is a table and no table is a stair, so no ladder is a stair: III is false. Both I and II follow.
