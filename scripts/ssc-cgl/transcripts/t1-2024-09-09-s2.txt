=== 7
chapter: Syllogism
subtopic: Three-Statement Syllogism
difficulty: EASY
answer: D
stem:
In this question, three statements are given, followed by three conclusions numbered I, II and III. Assuming the statements to be true, even if they seem to be at variance with commonly known facts, decide which of the conclusions logically follow(s) from the statements.

Statements:
All fans are brown.
Some brown are chairs.
Some chairs are plastic.
Conclusions:
I.Some brown are fans.
II.Some chairs are brown.
III.No chair is plastic.
A: Only conclusions II and III follow.
B: Only conclusion III follows.
C: None of the conclusions follows.
D: Only conclusions I and II follow.
solution:
I: All fans are brown, so some brown things are fans. I follows.

II: Some brown are chairs converts to some chairs are brown. II follows.

III: Some chairs are plastic, which directly contradicts "No chair is plastic". III does not follow.

Only I and II follow.

subtopic: Coded Relations
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: C
stem:
In a certain code language,
R + T means ‘R is the sister of T’.
R – T means ‘R is the father of T’.
R * T means ‘R is the wife of T’.
R \(\div\) T means ‘R is the mother of T’.
Based on the above, how is F related to C if ‘A + B – C \(\div\) D * E – F \(\div\) G’?
A: Wife
B: Daughter
C: Daughter’s daughter
D: Mother
solution:
C \(\div\) D: C is the mother of D. D * E: D is the wife of E, so D is female, C's daughter.

F is the daughter of C's daughter: C's daughter's daughter.
