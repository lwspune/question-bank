Which of the following terms will replace the question mark (?) in the given series?
XGR, TKN, POJ, LSF, ?, DAX
A: HVB
B: HWB
C: GWB
D: HWA
solution:
First letters go back 4 each time: X, T, P, L, H, D.

Second letters go forward 4: G, K, O, S, W, A (W + 4 wraps round to A).

Third letters go back 4: R, N, J, F, B, X (B - 4 wraps round to X).

The missing term is HWB.
