chapter: Order, Ranking and Arrangement
subtopic: Scheduling and Puzzles
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: D
stem:
Directions: Study the following information carefully and answer the questions based on it.

There are six people A, B, C, D, E and F who live in a six-storey building, the lowest floor is numbered 1 and the highest is numbered 6. E lives on an even-numbered floor. There are 2 floors between E and C and 1 floor between E and F. C lives on the first floor. F doesn’t live immediately above C. There are 2 people between A and D. D doesn’t live on an odd floor.
Who lives 2 floors below B?
A: A
B: B
C: D
D: C
solution:
C is on floor 1. With 2 floors between E and C, E is on floor 4 (even, as required).

1 floor between E and F puts F on 2 or 6; F is not immediately above C, so F is on 6.

Two floors below B (floor 3) is floor 1: C.

Two resistors, one with a resistance of 2 ohms and the other with a resistance of 4 ohms, are connected in parallel to a 6-volt battery.

A horde is a large group of people, typically one on the move (nomadic hordes, hordes of tourists). A crowd is a large number of people gathered in one place; a convoy is a group of vehicles or ships travelling together; a swarm is used of insects.
