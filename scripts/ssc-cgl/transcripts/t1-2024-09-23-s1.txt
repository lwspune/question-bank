=== 1
chapter: Classification
subtopic: Letter Gap Patterns
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: A
stem:
Based on the position in the English alphabetical order, three of the following letter-clusters are alike in some manner and one is different. Select the odd letter-cluster.
Note: The odd one out is not based on the number of consonants/vowels or their position in the letter cluster.
A: PSKI
B: TWGD
C: JMQN
D: MPNK
solution:
In each cluster the second letter is the first plus 3, and the fourth letter is the third minus 3.

TWGD: T + 3 = W, G - 3 = D. JMQN: J + 3 = M, Q - 3 = N. MPNK: M + 3 = P, N - 3 = K.

PSKI: P + 3 = S, but K - 2 = I. PSKI is the odd one.

D: J

chapter: Dice and Cubes
subtopic: Opposite Faces of a Dice
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: C
figure: stem
stem:
Three different positions of the same dice are given. Find the number on the face opposite to the face showing ‘2’.
A: 6
B: 4
C: 3
D: 1
solution:
The figures show (top, left, right): (3, 5, 4), (3, 4, 6) and (2, 1, 6).

6 touches 3, 4, 2 and 1, so 6 is opposite 5. The faces round 3 then run 5, 4, 6 and one more face, which must be 1 or 2.

If 1 is that fourth face, 2 is opposite 3 and 1 is opposite 4. Seen from the 2 face, the order is then 5, 1, 6, 4, which gives 1 on the left and 6 on the right as in the third figure. The other choice (2 next to 3, opposite 4) puts 6 to the left of 1 when 2 is on top, which contradicts the third figure.

So 2 is opposite 3.

=== 23
chapter: Blood Relations
subtopic: Coded Relations
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: C
stem:
If
‘A & Z’ means ‘A is the brother of Z’,
‘A # Z’ means ‘A is the sister of Z’,
‘A ^ Z’ means ‘A is the brother of Z’s father’,
‘A @ Z’ means ‘A is the wife of Z’,
‘A % Z’ means ‘A is the mother of Z’,
‘A \(\alpha\) Z’ means ‘A is the father of Z’,
then how is J related to Z in the following expression?

J % K # L @ M \(\alpha\) N & Z
A: Father’s mother
B: Mother
C: Mother’s mother
D: Mother’s sister
solution:
J is the mother of K; K is the sister of L, so J is also L's mother.

L is the wife of M, and M is the father of N, who is the brother of Z. So L is Z's mother.

J is the mother of Z's mother: Z's mother's mother.

Ourselves should not boast of one's knowledge.
A: Ourselves
B: knowledge
C: boast
D: one's
solution:
A reflexive pronoun ("ourselves") cannot be the subject of a sentence, and "one's" later needs "one" as its antecedent. The correct sentence is "One should not boast of one's knowledge."
