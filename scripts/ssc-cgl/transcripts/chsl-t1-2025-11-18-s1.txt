subtopic: Subject-Verb Agreement
difficulty: EASY

Identify the part of the sentence that contains a grammatical error.
A. The group of students, B. along with the professor, C. were discussing the new findings D. in the research lab.
A: The group of students
B: along with the professor
C: were discussing the new findings

Choose the correct passive voice transformation of the sentence:
You must finish your homework.
A: Your homework must be finished by you.
B: Your homework must being finished by you.
C: Your homework must have been finished by you.
D: Your homework is must be finished by you.
solution:
A modal in the passive takes "modal + be + past participle": "must finish" becomes "must be finished". The object "your homework" becomes the subject and "you" goes after "by". B and D are ungrammatical, and C changes the time to the past.

Two numbers differ by 12. If their product is increased by the square of their difference, the result is 1008. Find the larger number.
A: 18
B: 24
C: 30
D: 36
solution:
Let the larger number be \(x\), so the smaller is \(x - 12\).
