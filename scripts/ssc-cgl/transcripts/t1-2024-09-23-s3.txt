chapter: Blood Relations
subtopic: Coded Statements and Expressions
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: D
stem:
In a certain code language,
'A & B' means ‘ A is the brother of B’,
'A x B' means ‘A is the father of B’,
'A ? B' means ‘A is the mother of B’ and
'A ! B' means ‘A is the wife of B’.
Which of the following means that W is the mother's mother of P?
A: W & R ! Y x O ? P
B: W ? R x Y ! O & P
C: W ? R ! Y & O x P
D: W ! R x Y ? O & P
solution:
Option 4: W ! R (W is the wife of R), R x Y (R is the father of Y), so W is Y's mother. Y ? O (Y is the mother of O) and O & P (O is the brother of P), so Y is P's mother. W is therefore P's mother's mother.

Option 1 starts with W as a brother, so W is male. Option 2 makes W the mother of R, whose daughter Y is the wife of P's brother O, so W is not P's grandparent. Option 3 makes W the mother of R, the wife of P's paternal uncle Y, so W is not P's grandparent.

subtopic: Banking, RBI and Financial Institutions
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: C
stem:
Which of the following is a difference between self-help groups (SHGs) and microfinance institutions (MFIs) in India?
A: SHGs provide only credit, while MFIs provide a range of financial services.
B: SHGs rely on government subsidies for funding, while MFIs rely on commercial sources of funding.
