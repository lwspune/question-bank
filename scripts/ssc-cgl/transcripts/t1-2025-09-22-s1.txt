chapter: Inequalities
subtopic: Direct Inequalities
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: D
stem:
A statement is given followed by two conclusions. Find which conclusion is TRUE based on the given statement.

Statement:
\(C < A \le T = D \ge O;\ G \ge D < S;\ T \ge A\)
Conclusions:
I: \(A \le O\)
II: \(C < G\)
A: Either I or II
B: Neither I nor II
C: Only I
D: Only II
solution:
I: \(A \le T = D \ge O\). The signs between A and O point in opposite directions, so there is no definite relation. I is not true.

II: \(C < A \le T = D \le G\), so \(C < G\). II is true.

Only II is true.
