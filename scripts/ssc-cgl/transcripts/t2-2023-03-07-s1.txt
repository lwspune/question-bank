chapter: Probability
subtopic: Independent and Mutually Exclusive Events
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: D
stem:
A person can hit a target 5 times out of 8 shots. If he fires 10 shots, what is the probability that he will hit the target twice?
A: \(\frac{1135 \times 3^8}{8^{10}}\)
B: \(\frac{1165 \times 3^8}{8^{10}}\)
C: \(\frac{1175 \times 3^8}{8^{10}}\)
D: \(\frac{1125 \times 3^8}{8^{10}}\)
solution:
Each shot hits with probability \(\frac{5}{8}\) and misses with probability \(\frac{3}{8}\), independently.

Exactly 2 hits in 10 shots: \(\binom{10}{2}\left(\frac{5}{8}\right)^2\left(\frac{3}{8}\right)^8 = \frac{45 \times 25 \times 3^8}{8^{10}} = \frac{1125 \times 3^8}{8^{10}}\).

chapter: Heights and Distances
subtopic: Observer Above the Ground
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: A
stem:
The angle of elevation of the top of a tower from the top of a building whose height is 680 m is \(45^\circ\) and the angle of elevation of the top of same tower from the foot of the same building is \(60^\circ\). What is the height (in m) of the tower?
A: \(340(3 + \sqrt{3})\)
B: \(310(3 - \sqrt{3})\)
C: \(310(3 + \sqrt{3})\)
D: \(340(3 - \sqrt{3})\)
solution:
Let the tower be H m high at a distance d m from the building.

From the foot: \(H = d\sqrt{3}\). From the top: \(H - 680 = d\) (since \(\tan 45^\circ = 1\)).

So \(d\sqrt{3} - d = 680\), \(d = \frac{680}{\sqrt{3} - 1} = 340(\sqrt{3} + 1)\).

\(H = 340(\sqrt{3} + 1)\sqrt{3} = 340(3 + \sqrt{3})\).

Check: \(H - d = 340(3 + \sqrt{3} - \sqrt{3} - 1) = 680\).
