chapter: Time and Work
subtopic: Work Rates
difficulty: EASY
answer: D
stem:
P can finish a work in 18 days. When he had worked for 5 days, Q joined him. If both of them together completed the remaining work in \(\frac{13}{5}\) days, then in how many days can Q alone finish \(66\frac{2}{3}\%\) of the same work?
A: 5
B: 4
C: 2
D: 3
solution:
In 5 days P does \(\frac{5}{18}\) of the work, leaving \(\frac{13}{18}\).

Together they do \(\frac{13}{18}\) in \(\frac{13}{5}\) days, so their daily rate is \(\frac{13}{18} \div \frac{13}{5} = \frac{5}{18}\).

Q's rate \(= \frac{5}{18} - \frac{1}{18} = \frac{4}{18} = \frac{2}{9}\), so Q alone takes 4.5 days for the whole work.

\(66\frac{2}{3}\% = \frac{2}{3}\) of the work takes \(\frac{2}{3} \times 4.5 = 3\) days.

Check: in \(\frac{13}{5}\) days P and Q do \(\frac{13}{5} \times \frac{5}{18} = \frac{13}{18}\), as required.

subtopic: Congruence and Similarity
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: A
stem:
In \(\Delta ABC\), \(AB = 20\) cm, \(BC = 7\) cm and \(CA = 15\) cm. Side BC is produced to D such that \(\Delta DAB \sim \Delta DCA\). DC is equal to:
A: 9 cm
B: 8 cm
C: 10 cm
D: 7 cm
solution:
From \(\Delta DAB \sim \Delta DCA\): \(\frac{DA}{DC} = \frac{AB}{CA} = \frac{DB}{DA} = \frac{20}{15} = \frac{4}{3}\).

So \(DA = \frac{4}{3}DC\) and \(DB = \frac{4}{3}DA = \frac{16}{9}DC\).

D lies beyond C, so \(DB = DC + 7\): \(\frac{16}{9}DC - DC = 7\), giving \(\frac{7}{9}DC = 7\) and \(DC = 9\) cm.

Check: \(DA = 12\), \(DB = 16\), and \(DA^2 = DB \times DC\) gives \(144 = 16 \times 9\).
