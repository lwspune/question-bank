chapter: Coding-Decoding
subtopic: Word-Code Sentences
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: B
stem:
In a certain code language, 'stop cool cream brick' is coded as 'at bt ct mt', 'through truck cool' is coded as 'vt kt at' and 'brick clear truck' is coded as 'ct ot kt'.

How will 'stop clear cream' be coded in the same language?
A: at, ct, mt
B: bt, ot, mt
C: vt, ot, mt
D: ct, ot, kt
solution:
'cool' is common to the first two sentences and 'at' to their codes, so cool = at. 'truck' is common to the second and third, so truck = kt. 'brick' is common to the first and third, so brick = ct.

From the third sentence, clear = ot. From the first, stop and cream are bt and mt in some order.

So 'stop clear cream' is coded as bt, ot, mt.
