Identify the part of the sentence that contains a grammatical error.
A. The team's success was largely B. a result of it's coach's dedication, C. a fact which they would all D. attest to.

subtopic: Active and Passive Voice
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: B
stem:
Change the following sentence into the Active form.
The old castle was believed to have been built by a legendary king.
A: A legendary king built the old castle, it was believed.
B: People believed that a legendary king had built the old castle.
C: The old castle had been built by a legendary king, it was believed.
D: A legendary king was believed to have built the old castle.
solution:
"The castle was believed ..." is passive with no agent, so the active form needs a general subject: "People believed that ...". The perfect infinitive "to have been built" refers to a time before the believing, so it becomes the past perfect "had built". A and C keep "it was believed" (passive), C is wholly passive, and D still has "was believed".
