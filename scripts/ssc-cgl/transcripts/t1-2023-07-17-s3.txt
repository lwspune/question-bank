subtopic: Square and Cube Rules
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: A
stem:
Select the option that is related to the third term in the same way as the second term is related to the first term and the sixth term is related to the fifth term.
6 : 646 :: 5 : ? :: 4 : 190
A: 373
B: 377
C: 337
D: 376
solution:
Second term = 3 \(\times\) (first term)\(^3\) \(-\) 2.

\(6^3 = 216\), \(3 \times 216 - 2 = 646\). \(4^3 = 64\), \(3 \times 64 - 2 = 190\).

So \(5^3 = 125\), \(3 \times 125 - 2 = 373\).

Each vowel in the word "ETHICAL" is changed to the following letter in the English alphabetical series and each consonant is changed to the previous letter in the English alphabetical series. In the newly formed word, how many alphabets are there in the English alphabetical series between the alphabet which is \(3^{\text{rd}}\) from the left and \(1^{\text{st}}\) from the right?
A: Four
B: Three
C: Two
D: Five

chapter: Syllogism
subtopic: Three-Statement Syllogism
difficulty: EASY
answer: B
stem:
Three statements are given followed by three conclusions numbered I, II and III. Assuming the statements to be true, even if they seem to be at variance with commonly known facts, decide which of the conclusions logically follow(s) from the statements.

Statements:
All computers are laptops.
All laptops are mobiles.
Some mobiles are tablets.

Conclusions:
I. Some mobiles are computers.
II. All computers are mobiles.
III. Some computers are tablets.
A: Only conclusions II and III follow.
B: Only conclusions I and II follow.
C: Only conclusions I and III follow.
D: All conclusions follow.
solution:
Computers sit inside laptops, which sit inside mobiles, so all computers are mobiles (II follows) and therefore some mobiles are computers (I follows).

Tablets are linked only to some mobiles; they need not overlap computers at all, so III does not follow.

subtopic: Poverty, Employment and Human Development
difficulty: MODERATE

stem:
Consider the following statements about unemployment in India:
a) Seasonal unemployment is mainly found in urban areas.
b) Educated unemployed people are mainly found in rural areas of India.

B: Only b and c
C: Only a and b
D: Only a
