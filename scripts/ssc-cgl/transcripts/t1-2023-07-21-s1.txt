The second number in the given number pairs is obtained by performing certain mathematical operation(s) on the first number. The same operation(s) are followed in all the number pairs, EXCEPT one. Find that odd number pair.
A: (8, 62)
B: (10, 100)
C: (14, 194)
D: (12, 142)
solution:
The rule is (first number)\(^2 - 2\).

In a certain code language, 'BREAD' is coded as RBBDA and 'FLOUR' is coded as LFLRU. How will 'PAINT' be coded in the same language?
A: AFPTN
B: APLTN
C: NTFAP
D: APFTN
solution:
The first two letters swap, the last two letters swap, and the middle letter moves 3 places back.

BREAD: BR becomes RB, E becomes B, AD becomes DA, giving RBBDA. FLOUR: FL becomes LF, O becomes L, UR becomes RU, giving LFLRU.

PAINT: PA becomes AP, I becomes F, NT becomes TN, giving APFTN.
