=== 1
chapter: Series
subtopic: Letter Clusters
difficulty: EASY
answer: C
stem:
Which of the following letter-clusters will replace the question mark (?) in the given series to make it logically complete?
ICT, FES, CGR, ZIQ, ?
A: WJP
B: XKP
C: WKP
D: XLO
solution:
First letters I, F, C, Z go back 3 each time, so next is W. Second letters C, E, G, I go forward 2, so next is K. Third letters T, S, R, Q go back 1, so next is P. The cluster is WKP.

In a certain code language,
\(A + B\) means ‘A is the brother of B’.
\(A - B\) means ‘A is the father of B’.
\(A \times B\) means ‘A is the mother of B’.
\(A \div B\) means ‘A is the wife of B’.

B: Mother
C: Sister
D: Wife

chapter: Figure Counting
subtopic: Counting Triangles
difficulty: HARD
answer: B
figure: stem
stem:
How many triangles are there in the given figure?
A: 17
B: 16
C: 14
D: 15
solution:
The figure is a rhombus with both diagonals, drawn inside a hexagon and a pentagon that share its top vertex; the hexagon also passes through its bottom vertex, which lies on the pentagon's base.

Inside the rhombus: 4 small triangles and 4 made of two small ones (the two halves cut by each diagonal), 8 in all.

On each side, where the hexagon and pentagon cross: the sliver at the top between the hexagon's upper edge and the pentagon's upper edge, the small triangle at the pentagon's side corner, the triangle at the hexagon's lower corner, and the triangle at the pentagon's bottom corner beside the base. That is 4 on each side, 8 in all.

Total \(8 + 8 = 16\).
