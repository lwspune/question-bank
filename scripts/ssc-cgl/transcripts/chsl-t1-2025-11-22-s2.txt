subtopic: Tenses and Verb Forms
difficulty: EASY

stem:
Identify the incorrect sentence.
A: All of the cities in California is being affected by the rain yesterday.
B: Seven people were rescued from the fire.
C: Half of the boxes are empty.
D: Some of the TV programs are very interesting.
solution:
In "All of the cities ... is being affected by the rain yesterday" the plural subject "cities" needs a plural verb, and "yesterday" needs the past tense: "All of the cities in California were affected by the rain yesterday." Which of the following is free from grammatical error?
A: She is senior than me by two years.
B: He was prevented to go out by the guard.
C: I look forward to meet you soon.

This paper is \(\underline{\text{made up of wood}}\).

B: made up from wood

=== 93
subject: Economics

subtopic: National Income Accounting
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: C
stem:
Which of the following statement regarding income is correct?
I. Out of private income, what finally reaches the households is known as personal income.
II. The total national income of the country is composed of two parts private income and public income.
A: Only I
B: Only II
C: Both I and II
D: Neither I nor II
solution:
Private income is the income accruing to the private sector; part of it stays with firms (corporate savings) or goes to the government as corporation tax. What remains and actually reaches households is personal income, so I is correct.

National income is the sum of the income accruing to the private sector (private income) and to the government sector (public income from its property and enterprises), so II is correct too.
