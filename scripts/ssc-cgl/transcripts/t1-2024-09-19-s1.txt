stem:
In a certain code language,
‘A + B’ means ‘A is the mother of B’;
‘A \(-\) B’ means ‘A is the brother of B’;
‘A \(\times\) B’ means ‘A is the wife of B’ and
‘A \(\div\) B’ means ‘A is the father of B’.

B: Father’s brother

subtopic: Counting Triangles
difficulty: HARD
answer: C
figure: stem
stem:
How many triangles are there in the given figure?
A: 13
B: 14
C: 15
D: 16
solution:
The figure is a rectangle with a diamond joining the midpoints of its sides and a vertical line through the top and bottom midpoints. Inside the right half a horizontal line runs from the centre to the right midpoint. A chord across the lower part of the diamond meets the vertical line. A line from the top right corner meets the upper right side of the diamond, and a line from the bottom left corner meets the lower left side of the diamond at the chord's left end.

Corner regions outside the diamond: top left 1; top right 3 (two small and the whole corner); bottom right 1; bottom left 3 (two small and the whole corner). That is 8.

Left half of the diamond: the half itself and the small triangle below the chord, 2. Right half: the half itself, the triangle above the horizontal line, the triangle below it, and the small triangle below the chord, 4. The triangle made by the whole chord and the bottom vertex, 1. That is 7.

Total \(8 + 7 = 15\).

Six numbers 5, 6, 7, 8, 9 and 10 are written on different faces of a dice. Two positions of this dice are shown in the figure below. Find the number on the face opposite to ‘7’.
A: 8
B: 10
C: 9
D: 6
solution:
The first position shows 7 on top with 8 on the left and 9 on the right. The second shows 9 on top with 8 on the left and 10 on the right.

chapter: Syllogism
subtopic: Three-Statement Syllogism
difficulty: EASY
answer: C
stem:
Read the given statements and conclusions carefully. Assuming that the information given in the statements is true, even if it appears to be at variance with commonly known facts, decide which of the given conclusion(s) logically follow(s) from the statements.

Statements:
Some eagles are sparrows.
All sparrows are parrots.
No parrot is a penguin.

Conclusions:
(I) Some parrots are eagles.
(II) No penguin is a sparrow.
A: Only conclusion I follows.
B: Only conclusion II follows.
C: Both conclusions I and II follow.
D: Neither conclusion I nor II follows.
solution:
The eagles that are sparrows are parrots, so some eagles are parrots, that is some parrots are eagles: I follows.

Every sparrow is a parrot and no parrot is a penguin, so no sparrow is a penguin, that is no penguin is a sparrow: II follows.
