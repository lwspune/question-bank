chapter: Analogy
subtopic: Number Analogy
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: A
stem:
15 is related to 45 following a certain logic. Following the same logic, 25 is related to 125. Which of the following numbers is related to 80 using the same logic?
A: 20
B: 21
C: 18
D: 22
solution:
The second number is the first multiplied by one fifth of itself: \(15 \times \frac{15}{5} = 15 \times 3 = 45\) and \(25 \times \frac{25}{5} = 25 \times 5 = 125\).

The number related to 80 is the first term x with \(x \times \frac{x}{5} = 80\), so \(x^2 = 400\) and x = 20. Check: \(20 \times 4 = 80\).

chapter: Order, Ranking and Arrangement
subtopic: Linear and Circular Seating
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: C
stem:
Six students - Aman, Bobby, Chintu, Gita, Pranay and Ram - are sitting around a circular table, facing the centre.
Ram is sitting second to the right of Gita.
Pranay is the immediate neighbour of Gita and Bobby.
Aman is sitting third to the right of Gita. Who among the following is the immediate neighbour of both Ram and Bobby?
A: Pranay
B: Chintu
C: Aman
D: Gita
solution:
Going to Gita's right: seat 1 to her right, Ram at seat 2, Aman at seat 3 (opposite her), then seats 4 and 5, with seat 5 next to Gita on her left.

Pranay sits next to Gita and next to Bobby. Seat 1 is between Gita and Ram, so Pranay cannot be there (his other neighbour would be Ram). So Pranay is at seat 5 and Bobby at seat 4, and Chintu takes seat 1.

Ram (seat 2) and Bobby (seat 4) both sit next to seat 3, which is Aman.

=== 16
chapter: Direction and Distance
subtopic: Distance from the Start
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: D
stem:
Sally drives a mini school bus. She starts at the school gate and drives 7 km north for her first pick-up. She then turns right and drives 10 km picking up children along the way. She takes another right turn and drives 5 km. She then takes a final right to return to the school. How far would Sally need to drive to reach back to the school gate again?
A: 17 km
B: 22 km
C: 10 km
D: 12 km
solution:
Take the gate as the start. 7 km north, then a right turn means east for 10 km, then another right means south for 5 km. She is now 10 km east and 7 - 5 = 2 km north of the gate.

The final right turn points her west. Driving back along the roads she covers 10 km west and then 2 km south: 10 + 2 = 12 km.

In a certain code language,

A + B means ‘A is the Brother’s Wife of B’
A – B means ‘A is the Sister of B’
A \(\times\) B means ‘A is the Wife of B’

C: Wife’s Sister
D: Son’s wife

Find out the correct synonym of the following word: Obvious
A: dubious
B: clear
C: ambiguous
D: doubtful
solution:
Obvious means plainly seen or understood, that is, clear. Dubious, ambiguous and doubtful all suggest uncertainty, the opposite.

stem:
Why does the author of the passage ask us to forget the history that we were taught at school?
A: because the history book taught us that our nation is ancient
B: because only the modern people can imagine that way
C: because the history book taught us that there are no ancient nations in the world
D: because the history book asks us to celebrate festivals which make us believe that our nation is modern
solution:
The author says it is that school history, drilled into us since childhood, "that make it seem obvious to you that your nation is ancient", which the author holds to be untrue. So we must forget it because it taught us our nation is ancient. C reverses this, and D wrongly says festivals make the nation seem modern.

stem:
What does the expression “drill into your heads” mean?
A: make someone learn or understand something by repeating it
B: threaten someone repeatedly drill machine
C: force someone to move his/her head in a particular direction
D: repeatedly question someone regarding a certain issue
solution:
To drill something into someone is to teach it by constant repetition until it is fixed in the mind, as school history is taught again and again from childhood. The other options read the words literally or wrongly.
