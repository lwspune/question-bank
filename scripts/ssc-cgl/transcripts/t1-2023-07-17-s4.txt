subtopic: Opposite Faces of a Dice
difficulty: EASY

stem:
Two different positions of the same dice are shown, the six faces of which are numbered from 1 to 6. Select the number that will be on the face opposite to the one showing 1.
A: 4
B: 5
C: 3
D: 2
solution:
Face 3 is in front in both positions. Going clockwise round 3 (top, right, bottom, left), the first position gives 4 then 2, and the second gives 2 then 1. So the order round 3 is 4, 2, 1, and the top and bottom faces 4 and 1 are opposite each other. The face opposite 1 shows 4.

subtopic: Three-Statement Syllogism
difficulty: EASY
answer: D
stem:
Read the given statements and conclusions carefully. Assuming that the information given in the statements is true, even if it appears to be at variance with commonly known facts, decide which of the given conclusions logically follow(s) from the statements.

Statements:
Some pens are boxes.
No box is a fan.
Some pens are fans.

Conclusions:
I. No pen is a fan.
II. No box is a pen.
III. Some fans are pens.

B: Only conclusions II and IV follow

(10, 8, 6)

=== 10
chapter: Blood Relations
subtopic: Coded Relations
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: B
stem:
‘A + B’ means ‘A is the father of B’.
‘A – B’ means ‘A is the son of B’.
‘A \(\times\) B’ means ‘A is the sister of B’.
‘A \(\div\) B’ means ‘A is the wife of B’.
If H + I – J \(\times\) K \(\times\) L – M \(\div\) N, then which of the following statements is NOT correct?
A: H is the husband of the sister of K.
B: J is the wife of the son of M.
C: H is the husband of the sister of L.
D: H is the husband of the daughter of M.
solution:
H is the father of I, and I is the son of J, so J is the mother of I and H's wife.

J is the sister of K, K is the sister of L, and L is the son of M (M is the wife of N). So J, K and L are all children of M, and J is M's daughter.

H is J's husband, so H is the husband of the sister of K, of the sister of L and of the daughter of M (A, C, D are correct).

J is M's daughter, not the wife of M's son, so B is NOT correct.
