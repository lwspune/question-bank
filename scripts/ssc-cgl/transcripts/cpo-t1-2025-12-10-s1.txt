The Chennai seminar was 2 days before the Bengaluru seminar. The Hyderabad seminar was 5 days after the Chennai seminar. If the Bengaluru seminar was on \(18^{\text{th}}\) April, on which date was the Hyderabad seminar?

chapter: Order, Ranking and Arrangement
subtopic: Scheduling and Puzzles
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: D
stem:
Study the following information to answer the given questions.

Seven persons: A, B, C, D, E, F, and G purchased some items one after another i.e., eraser, pen, TV, printer, mouse, mug, and phone but not necessarily in the same order.
A purchased a mouse and there is a gap of 2 persons between A and E. E didn't come first or at last. As many persons came after A are the same as the number of persons who came before F who purchased the pen.C purchased an eraser immediately before A.There is a gap of 4 persons between E and G who purchased a printer.D purchased a phone immediately before E who purchased a TV.
The mug is purchased by B.
There is a gap of how many persons between F and G?
A: 1
B: 2
C: 4
D: 3
solution:
With 4 persons between E and G, E and G are 5 places apart. E is neither first nor last, so either E is 2nd and G 7th, or E is 6th and G 1st.

If E is 6th: D is 5th, and A, 2 persons away from E, is 3rd with C 2nd. Then 4 persons come after A, so F must be 5th, but D is there. This case fails.

If E is 2nd: D is 1st, G is 7th, A is 5th and C is 4th. Two persons come after A, so 2 come before F, and F is 3rd. B takes the last place, 6th. Order: D, E, F, C, A, B, G.

F is 3rd and G is 7th, so 3 persons (C, A, B) are between them.

=== 16
chapter: Blood Relations
subtopic: Family Puzzles
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: C
stem:
There are eight persons A, C, F, L, M, Q, R and T in the family of three generation. There are three married couples in the family. C is the wife of F. T is son of F. A is son of M who is married to L. L is mother of C. R is daughter-in-law of M. Q is niece of C.

All of the given pairs are related in some way except one. Find the pair which is NOT belongs to the group?
A: M-A
B: R-Q
C: C-A
D: F-T
solution:
L is the mother of C and is married to M, so M and L are the parents of both C and A; C and A are siblings. R, the daughter-in-law of M, is the wife of A, making the third couple (C-F, M-L, A-R). Q, the niece of C, is then the daughter of A and R, and T is the son of C and F.

M-A is parent and child (father and son), R-Q is mother and daughter, and F-T is father and son. C-A are brother and sister of the same generation, not parent and child, so C-A does not belong.

=== 18
chapter: Syllogism
subtopic: Two-Statement Syllogism
difficulty: EASY
answer: D
stem:
Read the following statement carefully and identify the conclusion that follows.

Statement:
Some computers are mobiles. No mobile is a radio.

**Conclusions:**
I. Some computers are radios.
II. No computer is a radio.
A: Only I follows
B: Only II follows
C: Both I and II follow
D: Neither I nor II follows
solution:
"Some computers are mobiles" and "No mobile is a radio" give only "Some computers are not radios" (the computers that are mobiles). The other computers may or may not be radios.

So neither "Some computers are radios" nor "No computer is a radio" is certain by itself. (The two form a complementary pair, so one of them must be true, but no option offers "Either I or II", so the answer is Neither I nor II follows.)
