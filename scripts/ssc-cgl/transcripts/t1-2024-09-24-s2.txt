=== 1
chapter: Blood Relations
subtopic: Coded Relations
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: C
stem:
In a certain code language,
‘A + B’ means ‘A is the mother of B’;
‘A – B’ means ‘A is the brother of B’;
‘A x B’ means ‘A is the wife of B’ and
‘A \(\div\) B’ means ‘A is the father of B’.
Based on the above, if M + Q – R + K – Y, then how is M related to Y?
A: Mother
B: Father’s mother
C: Mother’s mother
D: Brother’s wife
solution:
M + Q: M is the mother of Q. Q – R: Q is the brother of R, so R is also M's child. R + K: R is the mother of K. K – Y: K is the brother of Y, so Y is also R's child.

R is Y's mother and M is R's mother, so M is Y's mother's mother.

subtopic: Interchanging Two Signs
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: B
stem:
Which two signs should be interchanged to make the following equation correct?
\(29 \times 145 + 5 - 640 \div 15 = 216\)
A: \(-\) and \(\times\)
B: \(\div\) and \(+\)
C: \(-\) and \(+\)
D: \(\div\) and \(\times\)
solution:

chapter: Series
subtopic: Letter Series with Blanks
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: C
stem:
Select the combination of letters that when sequentially placed in the blanks of the given series will complete the series.

LM_O_NPMRO_KU_TI
A: RPMO
B: PNOR
C: NORP
D: NOPR
solution:
Split the series into groups of four: LM_O | _NPM | RO_K | U_TI. Read the same position across the groups:

First letters: L, _, R, U go up by 3 (L, O, R, U), so the blank is O.

Second letters: M, N, O, _ go up by 1, so the blank is P.

Third letters: _, P, _, T go up by 2 (N, P, R, T), so the blanks are N and R.

Fourth letters: O, M, K, I go down by 2, which confirms the grouping.

In order, the blanks are N, O, R, P: NORP. The series is LMNO ONPM RORK UPTI.

=== 13
chapter: Syllogism
subtopic: Three-Statement Syllogism
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: B
stem:
In this question, three statements are given, followed by three conclusions numbered I, II and III. Assuming the statements to be true, even if they seem to be at variance with commonly known facts, decide which of the conclusions logically follow(s) from the statements.

Statements:
All necklaces are squares.
Some squares are circles.
No circle is a ring.
Conclusions:
I.No square is a ring.
II.No ring is a circle.
III.Some squares are necklaces.
A: Only conclusions I and III follow.
B: Only conclusions II and III follow.
C: All the conclusions follow.
D: Only conclusion III follows.
solution:
I: Only the circles are kept away from rings; squares that are not circles may be rings. I does not follow.

II: "No circle is a ring" converts to "No ring is a circle". II follows.

III: "All necklaces are squares" converts to "Some squares are necklaces". III follows.

So only II and III follow.
