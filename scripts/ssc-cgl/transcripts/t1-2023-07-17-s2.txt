=== 1
chapter: Blood Relations
subtopic: Coded Statements and Expressions
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: B
stem:
‘\(A + B\)’ means ‘A is the sister of B’.
‘\(A - B\)’ means ‘A is the brother of B’.
‘\(A \times B\)’ means ‘A is the father of B’.
‘\(A \div B\)’ means ‘A is the wife of B’.
If \(A + B - C \div D \times E - F\), then which of the following statements is NOT correct?
A: C is the mother of F.
B: E is the brother of B.
C: A is the sister of the wife of D.
D: A is the sister of C.
solution:
A is the sister of B, B (male) is the brother of C, C is the wife of D, D is the father of E, and E (male) is the brother of F.

So A, B and C are siblings, C and D are the parents of E and F. C is the mother of F, A is the sister of C (the wife of D): those are correct.

E is the son of B's sister C, so E is B's nephew, not his brother. The statement that is NOT correct is "E is the brother of B".

subtopic: Three-Statement Syllogism
difficulty: EASY

stem:
In this question, three statements are given, followed by three conclusions numbered I, II and III. Assuming the statements to be true, even if they seem to be at variance with commonly known facts, decide which of the conclusions logically follows/follow from the statements.

Statements:
All clowns are books.
Some books are heavy.
Some heavy are trucks.

Conclusions:
I. Some books are clowns.
II. All books are heavy.

B: Only conclusions I and III follow.
C: Only conclusion I follows.
D: None of the conclusions follows.
