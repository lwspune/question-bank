difficulty: EASY

stem:
In the following questions, the symbols @, #, \(\text{\textdollar}\), © and % are used with the following meaning as illustrated below:
P @ Q \(\Rightarrow P \ge Q\)
P # Q \(\Rightarrow P \le Q\)
\(P \text{ \textdollar } Q \Rightarrow P < Q\)
P © Q \(\Rightarrow P > Q\)
P % Q \(\Rightarrow P = Q\)
**Statements:**
M © N
N # O
**Conclusions:**
I. M © O
II. M @ O

stem:
A two-digit number is 4 times the sum of its digits. If the digits are reversed, the new number is 18 more than the original. What is the number?
A: 24
B: 30
C: 36
D: 32
solution:
Let the number be \(10a + b\). Then \(10a + b = 4(a + b)\), so \(6a = 3b\) and \(b = 2a\).

The study was completed and then funding was requested, a sequence, not a contrast, so *Despite* does not fit, and *Having having* is ungrammatical. *Upon* (immediately after) gives: Upon having completed the feasibility study, the team requested additional funding. *On* means the same here, so the item has two defensible answers; *Upon* suits the formal register.
