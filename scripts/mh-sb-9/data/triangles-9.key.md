## Notes on the key itself

* **`Practice set 3.2` Q1(i) prints "SSC Test".** There is no such test; the chapter
  teaches ASA, SAS, SSS and Hypotenuse-Side. Fig. 3.19(i) marks all three sides,
  so the intended answer is the **SSS** test. A one-letter typo in the key.
* **`Practice set 3.2` Q4's third pair reads "∠LNM ≅ ∠PMN".** Under the
  correspondence LMN ↔ PNM the third angle pair is ∠LNM ≅ ∠PMN — this is correct,
  and looks wrong only because the letters are not in the obvious order. Do not
  "fix" it. (Its second pair, ∠LMN ≅ ∠MNP, is likewise correct: M ↔ N.)
* The key is complete for every non-proof question. Its gaps (3.1 Q8/Q10,
  3.2 Q5/Q6, 3.4 Q5-Q8, 3.5 Q3, Prob Q2-Q8) are all proofs, constructions or
