The parallel sides of a trapezoid are 3 cm and 9 cm. The non - parallel sides are 4 cm and 6 cm. A line parallel to the bases divides the trapezoid into two trapezoids of equal perimeters. The ratio in which each of the non - parallel sides is divided is:

C: 3 : 1
D: 3 : 2

subtopic: Recognising a Quadratic Equation
difficulty: MODERATE

stem:
Which of the following equations is a quadratic equation?
A: \((x - 1)(x + 2) = 2x + 1\)
B: \((x + 2)^3 = 2x\left(x^2 - 1\right)\)
C: \(x^2 = (x + 1)^2\)
D: \(\sqrt{x} = x^2\)

\(x^2 = (x + 1)^2\) reduces to \(2x + 1 = 0\), linear.

\(\sqrt{x} = x^2\) is not a polynomial equation.
