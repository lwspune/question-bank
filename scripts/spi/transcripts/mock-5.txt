=== 25
chapter: Statistics
subtopic: Median and Mode of Raw Data
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: B
stem:
Which measures of central tendency get affected if the extreme observations on both the ends of a data arranged in descending order are removed?
A: Mode and Median
B: Mean and Mode
C: Mean and Median
D: Mean, Median and Mode
solution:
Removing the first and last value of an ordered list leaves the middle value(s) in the middle, so the median does not change.

The mean uses every value, so removing the extremes changes it. The mode can change too, when an extreme value is (or ties for) the most frequent one.

The median is the one measure that stays the same, so the measures that get affected are the mean and the mode.
