subject: Chemistry
chapter: Mole Concept and Stoichiometry
subtopic: The Mole and Avogadro's Number
difficulty: HARD
answer: C
note: The source key marks Ag, Zn, Cu; that is wrong: silver ions are 1+ and zinc and copper ions 2+, so they need different charges per mole (settled 2026-10-21).
stem:
Which of the following set of metals require the same amount of charge of deposition of one mole of metal?
A: Na, Ag, Al
B: Al, Cu, Zn
C: Mg, Zn, Cu
D: Ag, Zn, Cu
solution:
To deposit one mole of a metal from ions of charge n+ needs n faradays of charge (\(n \times 96500\) C). So the metals in a set need the same charge only if their ions carry the same charge.

\(\text{Mg}^{2+}\), \(\text{Zn}^{2+}\) and \(\text{Cu}^{2+}\) all carry 2+, so each needs 2 faradays per mole.

The other sets mix charges: Na and Ag are 1+ but Al is 3+; Al is 3+ but Cu and Zn are 2+; Ag is 1+ but Zn and Cu are 2+.

The solubility of a gas in liquid can be increased by
A: Increasing the temperature
B: By increasing the pressure
C: By decreasing the temperature
D: By increasing the pressure and decreasing the temperature
solution:
A gas dissolves more under higher pressure (Henry's law) and at lower temperature, because dissolving a gas gives out heat and a warmer liquid lets the gas escape (a warm soft drink goes flat faster).

Both changes together raise solubility the most, so the answer is increasing the pressure and decreasing the temperature. Raising the temperature lowers solubility, and each of the other two options gives only half of the full answer.
