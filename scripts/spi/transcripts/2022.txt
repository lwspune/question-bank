subtopic: Parallelograms and Other Quadrilaterals
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: C
flawed: yes
note: The shape depends on which side is joined and how: rhombus (bases), parallelogram (equal sides, one triangle turned) or kite (equal sides, one reflected). Pass 1 keyed rhombus, the blind pass parallelogram; parallelogram is kept because a rhombus is also a parallelogram, so it covers two of the three shapes (settled 2026-10-21).
notice: As printed this question has no single answer: the shape depends on which sides are joined and how. The key takes the answer that covers the most cases.
stem:
If two congruent isosceles triangles are joined along one of the sides, figure formed will be____.
A: rhombus
B: rectangle
C: parallelogram
D: None of these.
solution:
Take isosceles triangles with equal sides a and base b.

Joined along their bases, the four outer sides are all a, so the figure is a rhombus. A rhombus is a special parallelogram.

Joined along an equal side instead, the outer sides are a, b, a, b: a parallelogram if one triangle is turned half round, or a kite if it is reflected.

So the figure is a parallelogram in two of the three ways of joining (counting the rhombus), and a kite in the third. A rectangle needs right angles, which these triangles do not give.

At what time will the angle between hour hand and minute hand of a clock be 30 degrees?

B: 11 o'clock

At an exact hour the hands are \(30^\circ\) apart for each hour gap between them.

Deepak, Sanju and Kunal working together can finish a work in one day. Deepak is thrice efficient as Sanju. Sanju takes twice the number of days as Kunal takes alone. What is the difference between number of days taken by Deepak and Kunal, each working alone to complete the work?

Renu travels to her school on a bicycle. While going, her average speed is 50% greater than her average speed while returning due to the traffic. B: 18.6% more
C: 30% less
D: 30% more.
