=== 1
subject: English
chapter: Reading Comprehension
subtopic: Finding Stated Facts
difficulty: EASY
answer: C
context:
Read the following passage carefully and answer the questions given below:-

Frogs are environmental assets, especially in an agricultural country like India. They consume pests that are responsible for diseases like malaria and also those that are a threat to plants. In fact, it has been found that an adult frog consumes its own weight of insects daily and that if their population goes down the insect population goes up. What all this also means is that a decline in the frog population necessitates an increase in the use of pesticides which are a serious health hazard for all living beings.
stem:
Why are pests harmful?
A: They spread disease.
B: They destroy crops.
C: They cause malaria and threaten plants.
D: They cause panic among farmers.
solution:
The passage names two kinds of pests: those "responsible for diseases like malaria" and those that are "a threat to plants". Only "They cause malaria and threaten plants" covers both. Spreading disease or destroying crops alone gives half the reason, and panic among farmers is not mentioned.
