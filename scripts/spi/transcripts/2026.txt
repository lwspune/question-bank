If the sum of the squares of zeroes of the polynomial \(x^2 - 8x + k\) is 40. Find the value of 'k'.
A: 8
B: 14
C: 12
D: 10
solution:
Let the zeroes be \(\alpha\) and \(\beta\). Then \(\alpha + \beta = 8\) and \(\alpha\beta = k\).

\(\alpha^2 + \beta^2 = (\alpha + \beta)^2 - 2\alpha\beta = 64 - 2k = 40\), so \(k = 12\).

Check: \(x^2 - 8x + 12\) has zeroes 2 and 6, and \(4 + 36 = 40\).

chapter: Time, Speed and Distance
subtopic: Boats and Streams
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: B
stem:
A motor boat whose speed is 18 km/hr in still water takes 1 hour more to go 24 km upstream than it takes to return downstream to the same spot. Then the speed of the stream is -----.
A: 8 km/hr
B: 6 km/h
C: 7 km/hr
D: 7.5 km/hr
solution:
Let the stream speed be \(s\) km/hr. Upstream speed \(18 - s\), downstream speed \(18 + s\).

\(\frac{24}{18 - s} - \frac{24}{18 + s} = 1\), so \(24 \times 2s = 324 - s^2\), that is \(s^2 + 48s - 324 = 0\).

\((s + 54)(s - 6) = 0\), so \(s = 6\) km/hr.

Check: upstream \(24 / 12 = 2\) h, downstream \(24 / 24 = 1\) h, a difference of 1 hour.
