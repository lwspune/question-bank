chapter: Surds, Indices and Simplification
subtopic: Fractional Indices and Exponential Equations
difficulty: MODERATE
answer: B
stem:
The value of x, if \(5^{2x - 1} = 25^{x - 1} + 100\) is ____.
A: 0
B: 2
C: 8
D: 5
solution:
Write \(25^{x-1} = 5^{2x-2}\). Then \(5^{2x-1} - 5^{2x-2} = 100\).

Take out \(5^{2x-2}\): \(5^{2x-2}(5 - 1) = 100\), so \(5^{2x-2} = 25 = 5^2\).

So \(2x - 2 = 2\) and \(x = 2\).

Check: \(5^3 = 125\) and \(25^1 + 100 = 125\).

If A = \((-1)^1 + (-1)^2 + (-1)^3 +\) ____ \(+ (-1)^{1001}\) and B = \((-1)^1 - (-1)^2 + (-1)^3 - (-1)^4 +\) ____ \(+ (-1)^{1001}\), then find A - B.

C: 1001

subtopic: Similar Triangles and Proportional Sides
difficulty: HARD
answer: D
figure: stem
stem:
A construction company wants to connect two parks on opposite sides of town with a road. Surveyors have laid out a map as shown. The road can be built through the town or around town through point R. The roads intersect at a right angle at point R. The line joining Park A to Park B is parallel to the line joining C and D.

i. What is the distance between the parks through town?
ii. What is the distance from Park A to Park B through point R?
A: (i) 8 m, (ii) 12.5 m
B: (i) 9 m, (ii) 13 m
C: (i) 9 m, (ii) 14 m
D: (i) 8.75 m, (ii) 12 m
solution:
The figure shows lines A-R-D and C-R-B crossing at R, with CR = 1.2 m, CD = 1.4 m and RB = 7.5 m.

Since AB is parallel to CD, angle RAB = angle RDC and angle RBA = angle RCD (alternate angles), so triangle RAB is similar to triangle RDC with RB matching RC.

Scale factor \(= \frac{RB}{RC} = \frac{7.5}{1.2} = 6.25\).

(i) Through town: \(AB = 6.25 \times CD = 6.25 \times 1.4 = 8.75\) m.

(ii) The angle at R is \(90^\circ\), so \(AR = \sqrt{AB^2 - RB^2} = \sqrt{76.5625 - 56.25} = \sqrt{20.3125} = 4.51\) m (to two decimals).

AR + RB = 4.51 + 7.5 = 12.01 m, about 12 m.

Check with the small triangle: \(RD = \sqrt{1.4^2 - 1.2^2} = \sqrt{0.52} = 0.72\), and \(6.25 \times 0.72 = 4.5\) m, again about 12 m in all.

subtopic: Tangents from an External Point
difficulty: MODERATE

stem:
Two equal circles touch each other externally at C and AB is a common tangent to the circles. Then,\(\angle\) ACB =
A: \(30^\circ\)
B: \(45^\circ\)
C: \(90^\circ\)
D: \(60^\circ\)
solution:
Draw the common tangent at C; let it meet AB at M. Tangents from an external point are equal, so MA = MC and MB = MC.

So the part between the Teesta and the Dihang is the Assam Himalayas.
