=== 1
chapter: Number System
subtopic: Irrational Numbers
difficulty: EASY
answer: B
stem:
The product of a rational and irrational number is
A: Rational
B: irrational
C: both of above
D: none of above
solution:
If \(r\) is a non-zero rational and \(s\) is irrational, suppose \(rs = q\) were rational. Then \(s = \frac{q}{r}\) would be rational, which is false.

So the product of a (non-zero) rational and an irrational number is irrational, for example \(2 \times \sqrt{3} = 2\sqrt{3}\).
