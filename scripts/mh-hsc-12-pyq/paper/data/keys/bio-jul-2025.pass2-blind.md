Defective or ambiguous:

- Q. 1(v): BOOK vs GENERAL KNOWLEDGE. The book attaches the 1:2 (instead of 3:1) ratio to pleiotropy (sickle-cell, lethal recessive homozygote), so D. In general genetics that ratio is the mark of a LETHAL gene, usually written 2:1 (and 1:2 here reverses the usual order); pleiotropy itself does not change a ratio. None of the other options gives 1:2 (incomplete dominance and codominance give 1:2:1, multiple alleles no fixed ratio), so D is the only answer the book supports. Medium only because the stem's "1 : 2" might be a truncated "1 : 2 : 1"; if so, A and B would both fit and the item would have no single key.
