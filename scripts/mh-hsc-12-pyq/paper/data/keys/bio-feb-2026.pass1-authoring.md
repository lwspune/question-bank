## Notes

- **Q. 1(v) — book and general knowledge differ.** Biologically, *Spirulina* is
  a cyanobacterium (blue-green "alga"), not a true alga. The book lists it under
  "Algae" among SCP organisms, and no other option is an alga in any sense, so
  (a) stands on either reading.
- Q. 1(iii): the book's table gives Neanderthal 1400 cc. General references give
  a range of roughly 1300-1600 cc (a mean near 1450-1500, often above modern
  humans), so outside the book (d) 1450 could be argued; the book's figure is
  1400 and its 1450 belongs to *Homo sapiens*. The value was read from the
  flattened table text (7 column heads, 5 cranial-capacity cells: 450-600,
  650-800, 900, 1400, 1450 for Australopithecus to Homo sapiens); the book's own
  exercise on the same point also offers 1400 cc.
- Q. 1(ii): "Chalone isomerase" is the book's and the paper's spelling of
  chalcone isomerase. Q. 1(v) "Trichoderma viridi" (book: viride) and Q. 1(vii)
  "Sansvieria" are printed as such on the paper; neither affects the key.
