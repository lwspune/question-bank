## Working

**Q. 1(i) — A.** Isotopes share the proton number and differ in neutrons.
Isobars share the mass number, isotones share the neutron number, isomers share
both and differ in nuclear energy state.

**Q. 1(ii) — D.** A molecule in the bulk is pulled equally in all directions; one
at the surface has no neighbours above, so the net inward pull means work must be
done against it to bring a molecule to the surface. That stored work IS surface
energy, so surface molecules sit at **maximum potential energy**. (Kinetic energy
is set by temperature and is the same throughout.)

**Q. 1(iii) — D.** By definition a perfect black body absorbs all incident
radiation: \(a=1\), hence \(r=t=0\). By Kirchhoff's law emissivity equals
absorptivity, so \(e=1\). Therefore \(e=1,\ t=0\).

**Q. 1(iv) — C.** Under forward bias, electrons and holes are injected across the
junction and recombine; the band-gap energy is released as a photon. Reverse bias
widens the depletion region and emits nothing.

**Q. 1(v) — A.** At equal kinetic energy \(p = \sqrt{2mK}\), so
\(\lambda = h/\sqrt{2mK} \propto 1/\sqrt m\). The electron is by far the lightest
of the four, so its wavelength is longest. *Note the proton and the hydrogen atom
differ by one electron mass — their wavelengths differ by ~0.03% and are not
practically distinguishable, which makes them a weak distractor pair.*

**Q. 1(vi) — C.** A cyclotron accelerates charged particles by a reversing field
across the dees; neutrals feel neither the magnetic nor the electric force, so
(a) and (d) fail. Std-XII presents protons, deuterons and \(\alpha\)-particles
as the canonical examples, giving (c). Strictly, negative ions can also be
cyclotron-accelerated, so (b) is not absurd — but (d) "all types" is firmly
wrong and (c) is the intended framing.

**Q. 1(vii) — B.** From \(e = L\,\dfrac{dI}{dt}\),
\(1\ \text{H} = 1\ \text{V·s/A} = 1\ \Omega\cdot\text{s}\). Watt is power, dyne is
force, Wb/m² is magnetic flux density.

**Q. 1(viii) — D.** Stefan's law: \(E \propto T^4\) in **kelvin**.
\(927^\circ\text{C} = 1200\) K, \(327^\circ\text{C} = 600\) K, so the ratio is
\((1200/600)^4 = 2^4 = 16:1\). Using Celsius directly would give (c)-ish nonsense
— the conversion is the whole question.

**Q. 1(ix) — B.** Power factor is \(\cos\phi = \cos 45^\circ = 1/\sqrt2 = 0.7071\).

**Q. 1(x) — B.** A pipe closed at one end has a node at the closed end and an
antinode at the open end. The fundamental has exactly one node. Two nodes is the
next allowed mode, which for a closed pipe is the **third** harmonic (closed
pipes support odd harmonics only), i.e. three times the fundamental.
