## Notes

- **Q. 1(v) — book and general knowledge part company.** Standard genetics
  would call a 3 : 1 that becomes 1 : 2 (i.e. 2 : 1) a *lethal gene* effect,
  not pleiotropy as such, and none of the options is "lethal gene". The book,
  however, files exactly this ratio under pleiotropy (sickle-cell example: "The
  ratio is 1:2 instead of 3:1"), so (d) by the book. Incomplete dominance and
  codominance give 1 : 2 : 1, multiple alleles no fixed ratio.
- Q. 1(i): the same book paragraph also says "DNA helix of 200 bps wraps around
  the histone octamer by 1¾ turns", which could be read as supporting (a). The
  stem asks for the DNA *around the octamer*, which the book quantifies as 146
  bp (200 is the whole nucleosome including linker DNA), so (b) — high, but a
  reader leaning on the second sentence could argue (a).
- Q. 1(iii): Azospirillum (b) fixes nitrogen for cereals too, but the book
  associates it with "corn, wheat and jowar"; rice is named only for Azolla.
