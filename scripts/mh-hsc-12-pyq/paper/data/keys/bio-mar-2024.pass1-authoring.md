## Notes

- **Q. 1(i) — two options the book supports.** The book (Ch.7, Growth and
  Development) says "Growth inhibitors in plants are ethylene and abscissic acid
  (ABA)", so both (b) and (d) are inhibitors by the book's own classification.
  Keyed (b) because the book singles out ABA as "a natural growth inhibiting
  hormone" and its effects section is built round inhibition (dormancy, stops
  cell division, cambium suppression), while ethylene is presented mainly as a
  ripening/abscission hormone. A grader accepting (d) would have the book on
  their side; consider marking this item for both.
- Q. 1(x): option (a) is printed "S-CA²" (a hyphen where "=" belongs, and an
  exponent 2 instead of Z); it is wrong either way. This item is the book's own
  exercise (Ch.15 Q.1 item 1), copied verbatim.
- Q. 1(iii) option (a) "tunica albugenia" is the paper's misspelling of
  albuginea; irrelevant to the key.
- Q. 1(v): the book's own exercise asks the reverse ("Which ... is NOT caused by
  unsterilized needles?"); the paper's positive form has exactly one answer,
  AIDS.
